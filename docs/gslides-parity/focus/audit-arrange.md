# Selection, arrange and the canvas: the production audit

Area: selection, arrange and the canvas. Auditor: the focus round subagent. Date: 2026-09-15 (the run started after midnight UTC, so the scratch deck ids carry 20260916). Target: https://turboslide.vercel.app serving `ec61c6b` (round four, the four hotfixes and the poll cadence). No source file was touched; this note, its screenshots and `rows.json` under `docs/gslides-parity/focus/audit-arrange/` are the only writes to the repository.

## 1. Method

One Playwright script, `audit-arrange.mjs`, kept under the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/focus/arrange/`), imports `playwright-core` from the repository root and nothing else from the repository. Headless Chromium, viewport 1440 by 900 at 1x, human speed: the mouse arrives at every press point in steps, drags are a real pointer down, moves in 14 to 18 steps and a release, keys are pressed 60 to 110 ms apart, typing is 40 to 90 ms per character, double clicks are real double clicks. The clipboard permissions were granted to the browser context so Cmd+C and Cmd+V reach the page the way they do in a desktop browser.

The scratch deck: `/new`, the title placeholder double clicked and typed ("Arrange audit"), Escape (the first write creates the deck and moves the address to `/edit/<id>`). Three rectangles placed through Insert > Shape > Shapes > Rectangle and a click on the sheet; the first placement converts the title slide to a canvas, and its own heading, lead and mark become positioned blocks, which the script parks along the bottom of the sheet through `block.set /pos` (the walk of `build-4/hotfix-4.md` does the same) so no click lands on them. The rectangles are then put at known homes through the same window API call: A at 200,150, B at 340,240 (overlapping A, for the stacking rows) and C at 1000,500, each 240 by 150 sheet px. Between sections the script writes the homes back the same way. Those `block.set` writes are the setup, never a driven step; every row below is driven through the product's own surfaces (the mouse on the sheet, the menubar, the toolbar, the keyboard).

One row per interaction. A row is "works" when its check passed on the first try, "flaky" when it passed on the second or third try after a reset (Escape, menus closed, zoom to fit, homes restored), "broken" when all three tries failed, and "not driven" with the reason when the audit could not drive it. The evidence column holds the observed values: the chip text, the handle controls, the positions read back through `slide.get`, the menu row's label and `aria-disabled`, the readout during and after a drag, the computed colours, the console entries, and the screenshot path.

Severity: 3 the seller cannot complete a top task, 2 a wrong or lost result, 1 cosmetic, 0 the row works (nothing to fix). Need, judged against `research/07-sales-users.md` sections 2, 3 and 8: core is a seller's weekly task (select, align, order, copy, paste, duplicate, delete, undo, nudge, fit), useful helps but a seller finishes without it (group, distribute, zoom steps, snapping, the readout), park is a designer's tool a seller rarely opens (rulers, guides, rotation, the snap overrides).

Undo and redo: every Arrange row runs its own undo and redo and reports the route, rotating the routes across the rows so all four are exercised: Cmd+Z with Cmd+Shift+Z, the toolbar Undo and Redo buttons, and Edit > Undo with Edit > Redo. The clipboard rows exercise the four routes again on a duplicate, and one row tries Cmd+Y.

Two earlier runs were stopped for script faults and their decks trashed and deleted forever through the product path (`untitled-20260916-4z7r`: the first insert converts the title slide, so every block gained a position at once and the script took the slide's own mark for rectangle A; `untitled-20260916-y281`: two waits were handed functions that expected the page argument, so the "paste on another slide" row threw after clicking New slide and left the stage on an empty slide for the rows after it). Both decks answer 404. The table below is the third run (deck `untitled-20260916-7bfu`, 71 rows, 1223 s, started 2026-09-16T01:10:54Z), on a fresh deck, with those faults fixed; the second run's first 32 rows agree with it, including the two align drifts, apart from Align > Right, which run 2 passed on its first try and run 3 on its second.

The third run had a cascade of its own that the table accounts for row by row: its "paste on another slide" row pressed Cmd+V straight after the toolbar's New slide button, which pastes nothing (a finding in itself), and each of its three tries left a new empty slide; the rows after it then met a stage that did not show the first slide or its rectangles, so their checks failed for that reason. Every such row was re-driven on a fresh deck by one of eight follow-up probes (`followup.mjs` to `followup8.mjs`, each on its own deck, each trashed and deleted forever), at the same human pace, and the table gives the follow-up's observation as the row's evidence, with the run 3 reading and its cause named alongside. Where a follow-up found a defect the run had not, the table adds a row. The run 3 raw rows with every try are in `audit-arrange/rows.json`; the table as data is `audit-arrange/final-rows.json`.



Teardown, in the script's `finally` block: File > Move to trash (the page returns to `/decks`), then `/decks/trash`, Delete forever on the deck's card, the confirmation, and a GET of `/edit/<id>` polled until it answers 404.

## 3. Summary

### 3.1 What works

Selection is sound. A click selects an object and shows the chip, the eight resize handles and the rotate ring; Shift click adds and removes; the marquee draws while the pointer is down (`.ts-marquee`, `data-marquee` on the editor) and takes every object it touches; a click on the empty slide and Escape both clear the selection; Cmd+A selects every object on the slide (a group counts its members). Grouping works by the chord, by the menu (the group stays selected afterwards) and in reverse; the arrow keys move a group as one. Stacking works by the four menu rows and by the Cmd+arrow keys, with undo and redo by all four routes. Align, Distribute, Center on page and Rotate 90 all act and all undo and redo by Cmd+Z, the toolbar and the Edit menu; Distribute is greyed with two objects and says so; Center on page lands the centre on 800 and 450 exactly. Copy and paste by the keys and by the Edit menu put the copy 16 px right and down and select it; Delete and Backspace remove; the undo and redo of a delete work by the keys; Cmd+D duplicates the object when one is selected and the current slide when none is (Google's rule); the nudges move 1 px and 10 px and undo one step per press. The zoom presets work: the arrow menu, Cmd+0, a typed value, View > Zoom > Fit; 100 percent holds. The size readout shows "W × H" while a handle is dragged at Fit and at 200 percent and is gone after the release. Rulers show and hide; a drag out of the top ruler adds a vertical guide with an inch readout; View > Guides adds, shows and clears guides, and a guide drags to a new position; Snap to > Guides toggles. The selection ring and chip are `#1a73e8` on the light theme and `#3d86f0` on the dark theme, both measured on the rendered page. Escape inside a text box steps to the box, and a second Escape clears it.

### 3.2 What is broken, with the mechanism seen

1. Align > Top and Align > Bottom (and any align whose shared edge is off the 8 px grid) move every object, the reference object included, to the nearest multiple of 8: tops 150, 240, 500 became 152, 152, 152; bottoms 300, 390, 650 became 648, 648, 648; lefts 203, 600, 1000 became 200, 200, 200. The production `block.align` action snaps the shared edge to the grid unless the input says `snap: false`, and the menu's plan does not say it; the same action called with `snap: false` landed the tops on 150. Left, Center, Right and Middle passed the audit only because 200, 720, 1240 and 400 already sit on the grid. Severity 2, core: a seller aligning three logos sees them all shift up to 4 px, and the one they aligned to moves too.

2. Cmd+V pastes nothing while the keyboard focus sits on the toolbar's New slide button or on a filmstrip card, which is where a click leaves it; two Escapes do not move it. The seller's sequence "copy, click the next slide, Cmd+V" does nothing and says nothing. A click on the slide, a selected object or Tab gives the stage the focus and the paste lands at the source position; Edit > Paste works from the menu. Severity 2, core.

3. After Cmd+Z removes a duplicate that was the latest action, there is nothing to redo: Cmd+Shift+Z, Cmd+Y, the toolbar Redo and Edit > Redo do nothing and the Redo button reads disabled (four drives). The redo of an undone delete works by every route, the redo of an undone slide duplicate works, and a duplicate undone after later actions had followed it came back on Cmd+Shift+Z, so the gap is the undo issued straight after the duplicate. Severity 2, core.

4. With the stage focused and nothing selected, Backspace and Delete remove the current slide, with no prompt; on the last slide the deck is left with no slide and the console logs a 400. Google removes a slide on Delete only while the filmstrip has focus. Severity 3, core: the seller who presses Backspace believing a text is selected loses the slide.

5. Every zoom step lands on 25 percent: View > Zoom > Zoom in and Zoom out, Cmd+= and Cmd+-, from Fit, from 100 percent and from a typed 150 percent, all set the stage to 25 percent; from a preset the zoom box keeps the old text ("100%" over a stage at 25 percent), and View > Zoom > Fit then reads checked and does nothing on its first click. Cmd+Shift+= does nothing. The presets are unaffected. Severity 2, useful.

6. Snapping commits what it previews, and the deck guide sometimes loses the snap to another line. With Snap to > Guides on and a guide at x 600, a drag that leaves the left edge at 603 or 598 previews and lands on 600; at 596 or 597 a different snap line is drawn, the preview stays where the pointer is and the object lands 3 or 4 px off the guide (three drives of five). An object edge snap (704 beside an edge at 700) and the sheet centre snap (796 to 800) landed exactly. Severity 1, useful: a seller sees a snap line and lands a few pixels off.

Two smaller things. Edit > Duplicate leaves the original selected where Cmd+D selects the copy, so Edit > Delete straight after removes the original and the seller keeps the copy 16 px away (severity 1). After a zoom step the zoom box can keep the previous preset's text over a stage at 25 percent (part of item 5).

7. Two rows are flaky rather than broken. Cut then two quick Cmd+Z presses (about half a second apart) left the cut object missing in run 3; the same chain 1.5 s apart restored it in follow-up 3. Edit > Copy and Edit > Paste pasted nothing in run 3 right after that state, and pasted four times out of four on a clean stage. Align > Right once ran with a single object because the Shift clicks did not form the pair while a 409 was logged.

### 3.3 What a seller needs here

The seller's weekly tasks in this area, per `research/07-sales-users.md` sections 2 and 3, are: click and Shift click to select, marquee a few objects, align them, bring a logo forward, copy a card to the next slide, duplicate, delete, undo, nudge, and get back to Fit before presenting. Of these, the audit finds the selection, stacking, duplicate, delete and nudge rows sound, and four things in the seller's direct path wrong: the align drift (fix the menu's `snap` input or the action's default), the paste that needs the stage focused (return the focus to the stage after the New slide button and after a filmstrip card click, or let the shell route the paste to the stage), the empty redo after an undone duplicate, and Backspace on an empty selection deleting the slide. The zoom steps are useful rather than core (the presets and the box work) but land on 25 percent every time and should be fixed or hidden. Rulers, guides, rotation and the snap overrides are a designer's tools; they work (except the guide snap landing) and can stay as they are. Kevin's directive is to keep what works and works well; on this area's evidence the selection, arrange, clipboard and nudge features can stay, the four defects above are the ones a seller will hit in the first week, and the zoom steps should not ship as they are.
