## DR-D2#1: layering

### Items

- One doorway for an anchored plate, `packages/chrome/src/usePlate.ts` (new): `usePlate(ref,
  { layer, anchor, side, align, gap, fit, follow })` puts the plate in its layer through D1's
  `useLayer` and on its anchor through D1's `place()` (which follows the anchor while the plate is
  open), and `useStart(anchor, side)` gives the place a plate stands at for the microtasks before
  its first placement, so a plate whose row or field takes the focus as it mounts is never hidden
  (a hidden element refuses the focus, VERIFICATION-3 finding 13). `usePointOrElement` turns a
  pointer into one stable anchor.
- The three placement functions leave: `placeMenu` (`Menu.tsx`), the tooltip's `place`
  (`Tooltip.tsx`) and `anchoredAt` (`pickers/ColorPlate.tsx`) are gone; every caller is on
  `place()`.

    context menus, the toolbar's arrow menus, the menu bar, the Slideshow options, the `/decks`

  - `bar`: the name prompt bar (`dialogs/NamePrompt.tsx`).
- Every z-index of 5 or more in D2's files reads the scale: the conflict and edit banners
  (`routes/edit.$deckId.css`) `--ts-layer-bar`; the shell banner, the narrow right panel, the
  sidebar overlay and its scrim, the source drawer and the WordArt bar `--ts-layer-docked`; the
  plates above in their layer's property (the custom property holds the order where the browser
  has no top layer). The stage's own plates keep local order under 5: the comment card from 6 to

- The name prompt bar (DESIGN.md 2.5, Kevin's screenshot 1): 4 px under the title row, the
  `.pt-float` plate (paper, the `--pt-edge` frame, the 6 px corner, the ring), its Continue button

  `cv11` and `ss01`.
- Tests: `menus/__tests__/menu-component.test.tsx` loses the `placeMenu` cases (the rules are

  no dialog of the editor opens a menu of the chrome.
- `chrome.layers.follows-anchor` reads the Theme panel's font picker, the editor's one font

- The export menu (`ExportMenu.tsx`, D1's file) is mounted nowhere in the product; its sheet's
