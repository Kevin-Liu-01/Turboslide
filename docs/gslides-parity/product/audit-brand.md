# Brand audit: what a brand needs from Turboslide

The auditor for the area "What a brand needs: brand kit, theme, fonts, templates, a slide library, translation", written on 2026-09-19 against `main` at `6e15d60` and production `https://turboslide.vercel.app`. Every observation below is either read from a file on that tree (named with its line numbers) or measured on production with Playwright at human speed on scratch decks that were trashed and deleted forever at the end of each run (the deck answered 404). The scripts are `brand-walk.mjs`, `brand-walk-2.mjs` and `template-walk.mjs` under the auditor's scratch folder; they import nothing from the repository but `playwright-core`. The evidence is under `docs/gslides-parity/product/audit-brand/`: the row tables `brand-walk.json`, `brand-walk-2.json` and `template-walk.json`, the screenshots `b-*.png` (walk one), `c-*.png` (walk two) and `t-*.png` (the template walk), the downloaded files `walk1-*.pdf`, `walk1-native-*.pptx`, `walk2-*.pptx`, and `pdf-walk1-page-1.png`, the first PDF page rasterized by poppler. Nothing was fixed. Copy rules: plain technical English, sentence case, no metaphors.

## 1. What exists today

The theme. One theme id, `gt-ink-paper` (`packages/theme/src/theme.ts` line 7 "The one theme; a second theme is additive"; `packages/schema/src/deck.ts` lines 23 to 25 `THEMES`). Its tokens are constants (`packages/theme/src/tokens.ts` lines 26 to 51: paper, ink, ink-2, titanium, hair, hair-soft, plate, cross, edge, thumb, in a light and a dark set), its font stacks are constants (lines 242 to 249: Inter for display and text, a mono stack, and CJK, Arabic and Indic stacks), and its geometry is constants (lines 129 to 150: the 1600 by 900 sheet, the 56 px rails, the wordmark at left 72 bottom 18, the counter at right 72 bottom 22). The CSS is `packages/theme/src/gt-ink-paper/sheet.css` (the wordmark rule at lines 126 to 132, the counter at 116 to 125) and `tokens.test.ts` pins the constants to the CSS. A deck carries `theme: ThemeId` and `defaults: { appearance, counter, background, notes }` (`deck.ts` lines 72 to 88); `deck.set` writes `/title`, `/theme` and `/defaults/*` (`packages/schema/src/actions.ts` lines 983 to 1008) and `deck.setBackground` writes the deck default background (lines 2651 to 2652). No field on the deck names a colour, a font, a logo or a footer.

The frame. The renderer draws the GT wordmark on every slide of every deck: `packages/render/src/stage.ts` lines 11 to 13 `WORDMARK_HTML` is the GT mark symbol at 28 by 18 in titanium, appended to every stage beside the counter (lines 42 to 48). The title kind requires a `mark: { w, h }` field (`deck.ts` line 251; `docs/grammar.md` title table), so every title slide draws the GT mark. Slide > Edit theme is a Later stub with the sentence "The footer mark, the slide counter and the rails belong to the GT theme" (`packages/chrome/src/menus/model.ts` lines 2005 to 2010), View > Theme builder is omitted with "The GT theme is edited in the repository" (lines 1100 to 1102), and Insert > Placeholder is omitted (line 1493). The PowerPoint writer puts the frame rules, the crosses and the GT wordmark PNG on the paper master (`packages/export/src/pptx/masters.ts` lines 39 to 92, `altText: 'GT wordmark'`).

The Themes panel. Slide > Change theme and the toolbar Theme button open `packages/chrome/src/ThemesPanel.tsx`: one theme "GT" as two tiles, Light and Dark, and an "In this presentation" group with the same two tiles; a click writes `deck.set /defaults/appearance` (lines 17 to 26, 59 to 63). The Import theme stub is hidden while the GT theme is the one theme (`docs/RETURN.md` 2.13; the words stay in `packages/chrome/src/menus/strings.ts` lines 758 to 766).

Fonts. `packages/fonts` ships InterVariable 4.001 upright and italic (`src/inter.ts` lines 9 to 37, SIL OFL 1.1), a metric matched Arial fallback (lines 59 to 70) and the export instances cut per optical size and weight under the family names `GT Inter Display`, `GT Inter Text 14` to `26` and their Medium twins (`src/export.ts` lines 1 to 9, 156 to 186; `export/fonts.json` textSizes 26 to 14, weights 400 and 500). `Typography` has size, weight, align, tracking, leading, spacing, columns and indent and no family (`packages/schema/src/typography.ts` lines 59 to 76). Round five built a catalog of 26 open licence families on the branch `round-five/integration` (`packages/fonts/src/catalog.ts` there: Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Poppins, Source Sans 3, Source Serif 4, Merriweather, Playfair Display, Lora, PT Serif, Libre Baskerville, EB Garamond, Nunito, Raleway, Work Sans, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Roboto Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono, Fira Code, fetched from google/fonts at commit `1ac2012`, OFL or Apache, 14.6 MB) with a Font dropdown, `font.list` and `turboslide fonts list` (`SPEC-5-amendments.md` A5; `VERIFICATION-5.md` row 24), and its verification found the Editable text export naming no catalog face (finding 5: the capture's theme bundle loaded no `@font-face` for the deck's families). Colours. A colour is a token name or a hex (`packages/schema/src/color.ts` lines 13 to 26, 55 to 68: Ink, Paper, Ink 2, Titanium, Hairline, Soft hairline, Plate, Edge, Green, Amber, Red, GT blue). Exports. Translation. General Translation's own repository has the pieces a deck translation needs, named in section 6.

## 3. What a seller and a marketer feel today

A seller who opens `/new` gets a General Translation deck: the GT mark on the cover, the GT wordmark and a counter in the frame of every slide, Inter, black and white. They can flip the whole deck between light and dark and nothing else about its look. The Font box is a disabled word. Colours are the GT designer's twelve tokens and one custom hex per pick, named in the designer's vocabulary (Titanium, Plate, Edge, GT blue). Their company logo is a picture pasted on one slide; every new slide starts without it, and the upload takes long enough that they wonder whether it worked. There is one template, the 85 slide GT brand deck, and no way to save their own. Import slides works from any deck on the instance and copies the whole source deck unless they notice the preselection. The downloads carry every GT element into the customer's PowerPoint and PDF, name the files by the deck id, and hand PowerPoint an Office colour and font theme, so a customer who touches a colour in PowerPoint sees Office blue and orange. A marketer at General Translation is well served by the defaults and blocked by the same things: no kit to hand to sales, no lock on the elements sales should not touch, no template gallery of approved decks, no approved slide library, and no translation, which is the company's own product.

## 6. Translation: the integration shape (not built)

What exists on Turboslide's side: every block's text fields are declared (`docs/grammar.md` "Text fields:" per block; `packages/schema/src/text.ts` for the inline marks), `text.splice` and `block.set` write them, the type ladder has `ladderStepDown` and Shrink text on overflow (`typography.ts` 162 to 170), `slide.import` copies slides with assets, `deck.copy` copies a deck, and the theme carries script font stacks for CJK, Arabic and Devanagari (`tokens.ts` 246 to 248).

What exists on General Translation's side (`/Users/kevinliu/gt/gt-cloud`): the files API with `POST /v2/project/files/upload-files`, `POST /v2/project/translations/enqueue`, `GET /v2/project/translations/files/status/:fileId` and `GET /v2/project/files/download/:fileId` (`apps/api/src/routers/files/{upload,enqueue,info,download}/index.ts`) and `POST /v2/translate` (`apps/api/src/routers/translate/translationRouter.ts` 293); a Google Slides translation path that takes a deck as keyed rich text with inline formatting kept as `<gwmN>` markers, batches it under a token limit, reuses previous translations, applies the glossary and reports batch progress (`packages/node/src/formats/google-workspace/translate.ts` lines 1 to 60; the data format `GOOGLE_WORKSPACE_CONTENT_JSON`, `packages/integrations/src/providers/google-drive/types.ts` 12); and a layout fidelity engine with a domain neutral IR (`packages/formats/src/doc-layout/ir.ts`: `LayoutDoc` of pages, boxes with a normalized `bbox` and units with `baseFontPt`, `equivClass` and `sizeRank`; `FitConstraints` with `minFontPt`, `preserveOrder`, `equalClassesScaleTogether`, `maxShrinkRatio`), a `FitSolver`, a `Verifier` whose per box verdicts are overflow, clipped, legible, underflow and trails, and a `DomainProfile` plus `LayoutAdapter` per domain (`domainProfile.ts`).

The shape:

1. `deck.translate { locales: string[], glossary?: string, linked?: true, baseRevision }` (group `deck`, mutates, every transport; MCP `deck_translate`; CLI `turboslide translate fr de ja`). For each locale it runs `deck.copy` into `<id>-<locale>`, writes `Deck.translation: { sourceDeckId, sourceRevision, locale, provider: 'generaltranslation', projectId }` on the copy (additive), and `Deck.translations: [{ locale, deckId }]` on the source.
2. The extractor walks the source's blocks through the schema's text fields and emits one keyed rich text document per deck, keys `slides/<slideId>/blocks/<blockId>/<field>`, the inline marks (`*`, `_`, links, the GT mark span) as paired markers the way the Google Slides path uses `<gwmN>`; the speaker notes as their own keys; the kit's lexicon (product names, the company name) as do not translate terms.
3. The document goes to the org's GT project through upload-files and enqueue with the source and target locales; the studio polls the status route and writes the downloaded translation back into the copy with `block.set` per field in one commit per slide, so the room, undo and versions carry it.
4. Fitting: Turboslide is a `DomainProfile` (page noun "slide", `bounds: true`, page local classes) with a `LayoutAdapter` whose extract maps a slide to a `LayoutPage` (boxes are the blocks with `pos` or their slot boxes on the 1600 by 900 sheet normalized to 0 to 1; units are the text fields with `baseFontPt` from the ladder, `equivClass` the ladder step, `sizeRank` the step's rank), whose render is the existing render route at 2x, and whose apply writes `typography.size` as `ladderStepDown` steps with `minFontPt` 15 (the sheet floor, `tokens.ts` 219). The verifier's overflow and clipped verdicts drive the loop; underflow grows a step back.
5. Right to left: the sheet root gains `lang` and `dir` from the copy's locale (SPEC-5 7.1 planned `lang`; `dir="rtl"` for ar, he, fa, ur), the text and paragraph alignments mirror (left to right, the rows key column to the right), the Arabic stack of `tokens.ts` 247 applies, and the exports write `a:rPr lang` and `algn` mirrored.
6. Update: when the source revision moves, the copy's Details shows "3 slides changed since the translation" with "Update translation" running the same path for the changed keys only (the GT path already reuses previous translations by key).
7. Presenting: item 15; the presenter's language switch reads `Deck.translations`.

What to decide before building: whether the org's GT project id and API key live on the principal record (`Preferences`, SPEC-5 7.1) or on a Turboslide instance setting; whether a translated copy is read only until "Detach" (Google's linked slides are one directional); and whether the extractor sends the notes by default.
