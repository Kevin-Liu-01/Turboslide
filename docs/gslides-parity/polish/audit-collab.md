# Polish audit: collaboration, sync and performance

Auditor's note for the polish round, 2026-09-28, against https://www.turboslide.com (main at `877eb690`, the blob tier, shadow authorization, anonymous principals). Every interaction was driven through the product's own controls with headless Chrome for Testing (`playwright-core` 1.62.1 from the worktree, channel `chromium` with the macOS GPU flags of AGENTS.md, viewports 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800, device scale factor 2) at human speed: the mouse moved in steps, keys landed 40 to 90 ms apart, double clicks and drags were the browser's own. A and B are two browsers of one person (B carries A's cookies); C is a fresh browser with no cookie of A's. Six scratch decks were made from `/new` and every one was moved to the trash through File and deleted forever on `/decks/trash`; `/edit/<id>` and `/deck/<id>` answered 404 for each (`untitled-20260928-dgg2`, `-v9sk`, `-gukj`, `-rp1z`, `-ns5g`, `-w6an`). Nothing else on the store was touched. No token, cookie or header was written anywhere; the one share link a ledger held was replaced by `/s/<token>`.

The scripts are under the session scratchpad, `polish/collab/` (`lib.mjs`, `collab.mjs` pass 1, `collab2.mjs` pass 2, `collab3.mjs` pass 3, `perf.mjs`, `filmstrip.mjs`). The screenshots and the ledgers are in `docs/gslides-parity/polish/audit-collab/` (`collab-ledger.json`, `collab2-ledger.json`, `collab3-ledger.json`, `perf-ledger.json`, `filmstrip-ledger.json`). Screenshot names below are relative to that folder.

Severity: 3 a seller would call it broken or jank, 2 a seller notices, 1 a person who looks closely notices. Effort 1 to 5. Google Slides is the reference for every row. A step my own script drove wrongly is marked as not judged, never as passed.

## Summary

What a seller feels in this area today. One person alone is fine: a fresh deck is ready in half a second, a typed word is saved in one to three seconds, the editor of a 40 slide deck opens in 0.7 to 0.9 s and answers a filmstrip click in 3 ms, a reload after thirty writes lands on the same slide with the same text in 1.6 s, and an idle tab or a show costs almost nothing. Two people on one deck is where it stops feeling like Google Slides. Typing in the same title at once is kept, but the merged words arrive three to five seconds later and neither person sees the other's caret. When both press New slide within half a second, the second slide is refused with a card that names an internal id and covers the toolbar, and the refused tab's title row reads "Couldn't save, retrying" for as long as the card stands, although nothing retries and its later writes go through. A colleague on the shared Viewer link never sees an edit without a reload. Follow is never offered, because every seller on production is anonymous and the rule only follows accounts. A joiner's chip takes 2.5 to 10 s to appear. Under a busy store the tab reads "Reconnecting…" while it is connected, a slide added in one browser takes more than five seconds to reach the other, and `/decks` can render a broken page with "Vercel Blob: Too many requests" in the console. `/decks` is the slow surface: 1.6 to 1.8 s to first paint at the median, 7 to 11 s at the worst, with 31 to 77 function requests per load and a 502 from the thumbnail route on every load. The present link flashes the viewer chrome over a black stage for about half a second and the sheet then jumps.

The ten items to fix first, in this order: 3 (the retry word after a refusal), 1 (the colliding slide id), 4 (the viewer page that never updates), 2 (`/decks` load and its 502s), 5 (the busy store's words and stalls), 6 (the name prompt during a drag), 7 (the reject card's words and placement), 8 (Follow for anonymous editors), 9 (the present link's flash and jump), 11 (the filmstrip on End, Home and Duplicate).

What was driven: 25 features across 24 two browser scenarios in three passes, 10 filmstrip steps, 64 route loads (8 routes, 2 viewports, 3 cold and 1 warm each), 4 idle windows and 5 editor interactions on the 40 slide deck. Three scenarios of pass 1 were driven wrongly by my script and repeated in pass 2 (the chip on a three slide deck, the thirty writes) or read from the screenshot alone (the offline words).
