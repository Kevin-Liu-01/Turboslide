# The polish round

The binding specification of the polish round (2026-09-28), sized for one build of about a day and a half of the pipeline: seven lanes with disjoint files (section 4), the acceptance rows of 5.1, one gate on the enforce preview and one on production after the ship (5.2), and the verifier's hand re-walk of every area with pictures as the second acceptance (5.3). `docs/OBJECTS.md`, `docs/VECTOR.md`, `docs/FEATURES.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/SYNC.md`, `docs/RETURN.md`, `docs/FOCUS.md` and `docs/gslides-parity/focus/AMENDMENTS.md` A1 stand under it; the parked list of record is `docs/gslides-parity/focus/ship-c1a7ff3.json`. Google Slides is the parity reference for every feature. The home page's grammar is `docs/brand.md` and the round four notes of `docs/gslides-parity/SPEC-4.md` section 2; the sheet's grammar is `docs/grammar.md`.

Everything below was measured first. Nine audits drove production at human speed on 2026-09-28 (headless Chromium through the worktree's playwright-core, 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800, the home page at 390 by 844 too, device scale factor 2, the mouse moved in steps, 40 to 90 ms per key, the browser's own double clicks and drags), every screenshot looked at, every scratch deck trashed and deleted forever and read as 404. The audits and their evidence are under `docs/gslides-parity/polish/`: `audit-text.md` (10 items), `audit-tables.md` (20), `audit-objects.md` (22), `audit-media.md` (26), `audit-chrome.md` (53), `audit-pages.md` (45), `audit-collab.md` (25), `audit-assist.md` (33) and `audit-home.md` (31), with their screenshot folders `audit-<area>/`. The tree the fixes land on is `main` at 877eb690, which `https://turboslide.vercel.app` serves; `https://www.turboslide.com` serves an older build (section 0), so an audit that names `www` measured the older build and says so.

## 1. Kevin's asks and what the round does

Kevin's asks of 2026-09-28, verbatim. First, with a screenshot of a selected table whose guide grid ran a row past the ring's bottom edge, its first cell's prompt "Type to add text" wrapped onto two lines in a narrow cell, and the header rule sitting above an empty header row: "update turboslide.com to be better and check literally every single feature again. table is kinnd of jank, for example". Second: "now completely update /home too and amek it a lot better annd more readable with far better spacing and far more minimal lettignn diagrams, icons and images do more work, with standard technical english, no interruptions in thought between phrases or headers and stuff, etc".

His standing words hold: "we must, must must be able to drag and move around ANYTHING"; "make sure to actually test our slide features and make sure they work"; plain technical English in the product and on the home page (sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, no trailing periods on headings, one thought per sentence, no phrase or header that interrupts a thought); the GT chrome grammar (Inter, black and white, hairlines from the tokens, nothing decorative).

What the round does, each part measured by rows of 5.1 and shipped through one gate on the enforce preview and one on production:

1. Every feature re-checked by a person. The nine audits drove 265 items' worth of features at human speed and compared each with Google Slides. The measure of this round is the same person's re-walk after the build (5.3): a fix whose picture still shows the jank is not done, whatever its row reads. The gate's rows all read green on 877eb690 while a seller still saw the table Kevin photographed, so the rows of 5.1 are written as a screenshot judges them: a pixel read of the frame, a box read of the DOM, or a spec assertion on the file the export wrote.
2. The jank fixed (section 2). 120 items are on the build, ranked by severity then effort inside each area, Kevin's table first. A defect is anything a seller would call wrong, broken, slow or ugly: a misaligned ring, an overflowing grid, a wrapping placeholder, a wrong size, a flash, a jump, a missing state, a dead control, a wrong word. The items a day and a half cannot hold are in section 6 with the reason.
3. The home page remade (section 3): seven sections, about 200 words, one picture or one diagram per section, the spacing system with its numbers at 1440, 1280 and 390, the load and layout shift budgets, the card.
4. The domain (section 0): the three guards, and the question for Kevin.

Where the audits ran. The text, pages, collab and assist audits drove `https://www.turboslide.com`; the tables, objects, media and chrome audits drove both and read every code path against 877eb690 on `https://turboslide.vercel.app`. An item read on `www` alone (the older build) is in the audits' own "on www alone" tables and not in section 2: those leave with the redeploy.

Severity and effort. Severity 3: a seller would call it broken, wrong or jank. 2: a seller notices. 1: a person who looks closely notices. Effort 1 to 5 in days of one lane. A row's `severity` in 5.1 is the item's, since the audits already wrote severity in the matrix's scale.
