### Where the build differs from the letter of section 3, and why

1. The `h1` "Build the pitch, present it and send the link" carries the list comma of its three
   verbs, which 3.2 item 1 and the row `decks.home.seller-lead` fix verbatim, while 3.1 says no
   comma in a heading. The lints and the copy rules script read the six `h2`s with no comma and
   the `h1` with no clause after a comma; the list comma stays. Kevin's rule is "no phrase or
   header that interrupts a thought", and three verbs of one task do not.
2. The menus crop is 612 by 505, not under 480: the Insert menu of this build (Image to Shader,
   15 rows) stands about 500 px under the title row, and a crop under 480 would cut its last
   rows. The whole menu with the title row's mark is the picture; the ceiling in the script and
   the test is 520.
3. The hero's 2x candidate at JPEG quality 82 weighs 395 KB (dark) and 392 KB (light) on the Blue
   Marble slide, over 3.5's 300 KB for the first screen (a two tone dither is the worst case for
   JPEG). Measured alternatives on the same capture: quality 70, 311 KB; a palette PNG, 491 KB;
   a 1.5x candidate would be about 230 KB at 1.04 of the pixels on a 2x display. The build keeps
   3.3's 2x at 82 (the crisp picture) and reports the number; `shots.test.ts` holds a 450 KB
   ceiling for the hero and 3.5's 800 KB for the hero and the two crops together (measured 660 KB
   dark, 690 light). On the dev server the two crops load before any scroll too (Chrome's lazy
   distance reaches them at 1440 by 900), so "images before the first scroll" reads 678 KB dark
   and 687 KB light against the 300 KB row, and "after a full scroll" the same. The choice
   (quality 82 at 2x, a 1.5x candidate, or a lower quality) is Kevin's or the integrator's; one
   constant in `build-home-assets.ts` changes it.
4. The two crops draw at 609 px in the 611.3 px slot (the frame takes 2), 0.995 of their 612 px
   capture; the spec reads them within the frame's two pixels of 1:1.
5. The canvas crop's picture is the Blue Marble slide's own picture, selected at 0.3 of the
   sheet's size (View > Zoom, the `view.zoom` action) so the whole ring, the eight handles, the
   rotation handle and the readout fit the crop, held at 12 degrees and cancelled with Escape
   before the button is released, so nothing is written. 3.2 item 2 pictured a free picture
   selected at fit size. The upload path that would place one (Insert > Image > Upload from
   computer, the row `images.insert.upload`, named for its owner) landed nothing on this tree on
   2026-09-28 16:17 to 16:21 (the file chooser opened, the file was set, no revision, no block,
   no error, on the seed deck and on a fresh deck from /new while B4's `picture-place.ts` and
   `inspector/picture.tsx` are in progress), and `block.insert` with a `pos` or as a slot block
   answered the unchanged slide with revision 33 and no error. When the upload works on the
   merged tree the integrator can switch the capture back to a placed picture; the zoomed
   selection is the product's own render either way.
6. `cursor-arrow-rays` and `arrow-down-tray` are not in the theme sprite, so `SectionIcon.tsx`
   carries the two Heroicons paths inline until request 5 lands.
7. With a stored light appearance the head manager renders a second `theme-color` meta beside
   the one the boot script rewrote (`__root.tsx`, B5's; the dark stored page has one). The spec
   reads the first meta and reports the count; a request for B5, since the root route is theirs.
