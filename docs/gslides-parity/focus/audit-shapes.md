# Shapes, lines and drawing on production: the audit

Auditor: the shapes area of the focus round. Target: https://turboslide.vercel.app (round four with its hotfixes, `ec61c6b`). Run: 2026-09-15, headless Chromium 1440 by 900 through playwright-core at human speed (mouse moves in steps, 40 to 90 ms between keys, real drags), scratch deck `untitled-20260916-9l8u` created from `/new`. Script: `scratchpad/focus/shapes/audit-shapes.mjs` (imports only playwright-core); its stdout is kept as `audit-shapes/run-1.log`, the screenshots are under `audit-shapes/`.

The run was cut short. The orchestrator's stop signal arrived while the script was at row 35, after the Insert > Shape menu, the four galleries, fifteen presets inserted by click and six drawn by drag, and before the battery slide (select, move, resize by every handle, rotate, fill, border, text, duplicate, delete, undo, Format options, lines, connectors, the PDF and PPTX exports, the reload). Those interactions are recorded as not driven with that reason. The audit process was stopped and the scratch deck was trashed and deleted forever by `scratchpad/focus/shapes/cleanup-deck.mjs` (File > Move to trash, Delete forever on /decks/trash, then the 404 check); its output is `audit-shapes/cleanup.log`.

## Summary

What works. The Insert menu opens, the Shape row and its four category rows (Shapes, Arrows, Callouts, Equation) are enabled, and each category opens a gallery plate. A gallery tile arms the draw tool (`data-tool="shape"`); one click on the slide inserts the preset at the default 240 by 160 box and a drag inserts it at the drawn box, within a pixel of the drag, for every one of the 21 presets tried across the four categories. The inserted block carries the right preset id (`shape: triangle`, `rightArrow`, `wedgeRectCallout`, `mathPlus` and so on) and the svg carries the same id in `data-shape`. The toolbar carries Shape and Line buttons (`toolbar.insertShape`, `toolbar.insertLine`) with nothing selected, as research/07 rule 3 asks.

What is broken, and the mechanism. Every preset draws as a plain rectangle. In the galleries all 135 tiles show the same rectangle outline and the preset's name exists only in a hover tooltip, so a seller cannot tell an arrow from a star from a callout before picking (screenshots 01 to 04). On the slide the triangle, diamond, hexagon, star, cloud, right arrow, left right arrow, chevron, both callouts and both equation signs render as the same hairline rectangle as the plain rectangle; only the ellipse draws its own outline (screenshots 06 and 08). The stored block and the svg's `data-shape` say the right preset; the drawn path does not. The shape picker source at the production commit says why: "Until the geometry interpreter lands (merge 1b) every glyph is the preset's box" (`packages/chrome/src/pickers/ShapePicker.tsx`), and the renderer's preset branch draws `shapePath(kind, ...)`, which on production returns the box for every preset except the ellipse. The gallery counts also differ from the shape table in the source (99, 26, 4 and 6 tiles against 100, 20, 10 and 6 presets), so the production build's category table is not the one in `packages/schema/src/shapes.ts` at `ec61c6b`. A second cosmetic defect: a fresh shape has no fill and a `var(--hair)` 1 px stroke, so on the dark theme it is a faint outline a seller can miss (screenshot 06).

What a seller needs here. A rectangle, a rounded rectangle, an ellipse, a straight line and an arrow with a fill, a border and text inside, plus move, resize, rotate, duplicate, delete and undo, and the shape surviving the PDF and PPTX exports. The rectangle and the ellipse insert correctly today; the other 133 presets insert as rectangles, which is worse than not offering them, because the seller picks a callout and gets a box. Kevin's instinct matches the evidence: keep Rectangle, Rounded rectangle, Ellipse, Line and Arrow, and take the four galleries off the menu until the geometry interpreter draws them. The battery of interactions on those five kinds (resize by every handle, rotate, fill, border, text, exports, reload) was not reached by this run and is the first thing to drive next; the last production walk (build-4/hotfix-4.md section 3.8) passed the rectangle's move, eight handle resizes, rotation, duplicate, delete and undo, but did not drive fill, border, text in a shape, connectors or the shape in the exports.
