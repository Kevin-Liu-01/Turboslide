## 6. Deviations and decisions, recorded for the integrator and the verifier

1. The zip step is injected at the package boundary (section 1.2) instead of `package.ts` importing `readZip`, because the workspace edge to `@turboslide/store` does not exist at `BASE` and a builder cannot install it; the test only `__tests__/unzip.ts` bridges the gap and goes when B3-1 lands. The refusal for a zip64 archive and the inflation cap are the store reader's (`readZip` throws its own `TypeError`s), so `PackageRefusalCode` carries no `zip64` code of its own; the orchestrator maps those sentences on day 4.
2. `validatePackage` is reimplemented over the part map with the export's five rules rather than calling `packages/export/src/ooxml/validate.ts`, whose signature takes a jszip `Package`; `resolveTarget` and `sourceOfRels` are the same arithmetic (the reader's `resolveTarget` also decodes a percent encoded target, which some producers write). When B3-1 lands, the two helpers can be imported instead; the rules stay one set.

## 9. Fix round (2026-09-15): VERIFICATION-5 finding 13, the Sales pitch's demo clip

### 9.1 The finding and the decision

Finding 13 (severity 2, S2b, SPEC-5 3.2): the Sales pitch's Product demo slide has no Play step. `motion.compile product-demo` answers `steps: []`, the show reads `data-steps="0"`, and in the verifier's run ArrowRight left the slide and nothing played. The proposed fixes were a `playMedia` row in `decks/templates/sales-pitch/slides/product-demo.json` (this lane's file) or a step derived from `playback.start === 'click'` in `compileMotion` (B1's `packages/render/src/motion.ts`).

Decision: no row is added and the template slide stays as written. The finding is recorded as a deviation with the reason below, and the miss the verifier saw was finding 12, which has landed on the shared tree since (9.2 proves the clip playing on the checkout).

The reason, file by file:

- SPEC-5 0.22 and section 4 row 6 describe the slide as a media block on Play (on click) and name no Play row. SPEC-5 2.1 (the media rows table) writes the row for `auto` alone: `playRowsFor` in `packages/schema/src/blocks/media.ts` (B2, b1.md request 5) appends `{ effect: 'playMedia', trigger: 'withPrevious' }` when a block's start becomes `auto` and removes every `playMedia` row of the block when its start leaves `auto`; `media.insert` and `media.setPlayback` call it on every write. A click row in the template would be the state the first Format options write on the block removes.
- The show plays a click block with no row on the first click after the slide's own steps: `apps/studio/src/components/Slideshow.tsx` `advance` calls `controller.playClickMedia(slideRoot)` when `state.step >= state.steps` (its comment cites R11 5.4), and `packages/viewer/src/present/media-controller.ts` `playClickMedia` plays every mounted `click` entry of the slide root not yet clicked and answers true, so the slide stays; the click after that moves on. `data-steps="0"` on the Product demo slide is therefore the designed state, and the presenter console's "Playing" row comes from the controller's `onState`, not from a step.
- The file writes the same click step: `packages/export/src/ooxml/timing.ts` appends `clickMediaStep` for every media block on `click` that no row played. The Sales pitch's Editable text file confirms it: `ppt/slides/slide6.xml` carries one `mainSeq` with one `clickEffect` (`p:cmd playFrom(0.0)`) and the `p:cMediaNode` of the `p:video`; `export check` reads "7 effect node(s) (clickEffect 7, mainSeq 3, tmRoot 3); 1 bldP with 1 build="p"; 0 p:audio and 1 p:video node(s)".
- Why the verifier saw nothing play: finding 12. On the verified tree the browser's render context passed `assetUrl` and no `media` resolver, so `renderMedia` wrote `data-src=""`; `mountOne` then mounts an entry with `element: null` (the element is created only when `playback.src !== ''`) and there is nothing to play. On the tree of this fixer round the show's demo root carries `data-src="/decks/<id>/assets/demo.cc0aef83.webm"` and `data-duration="5000"` and mounts a `video`, and the click after the steps plays it (9.2).
- Why a click row would be worse, not only unnecessary: `controller.play(blockId)`, the step advance's call, never sets `entry.clicked`, so after a click row's step played the clip the next click would still reach `playClickMedia`, which plays the entry again (or restarts an ended clip) and answers true; the slide would need one more click to move on, the opposite of what a rep on a call wants. The Motion panel would also list "Play Product demo (On click)", a row Google shows for automatic media alone (R02 a.2, SPEC-5 2.1).
