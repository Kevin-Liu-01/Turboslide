## 2. Summary

What a seller feels today. The core of the weekly loop holds: a picture arrives by upload, drop or paste in under four seconds and lands as a 480 by 300 object, the crop cycle works, Find and replace renames the customer across the deck, the speaker notes and Presenter view work with the timer and the next slide, the show pages with every Google key and keeps paging without the network, comments post and resolve, the Google chords the research names all fire, and the editor fits a 1280 laptop and folds its toolbar at phone width without horizontal overflow. The seller stops at five places. Replace image > Upload from computer never swaps the picture, from the toolbar or the right click menu, so swapping a customer logo means delete and insert and lose the frame. A PowerPoint file is refused by both import dialogs. The font control is disabled with one sentence. A long paragraph runs out of the bottom of its text box while the panel says the box should grow. A link can only be a URL: the "Slides in this presentation" picker exists in the code and no route opens it. Behind those, the seller finds no video, no transition, no page size, no handouts, no spell check card, a mention picker that lists only themselves, and a Publish dialog that forgets it published.

The ten items this round should do first, in the table's order: fix Replace image by upload (1); port the PPTX reader (2); port the font catalog and the Font dropdown (3); give the link popover the slide picker (4); make a text box grow with its text (5); read the published record on open and gate the player (6); a YouTube video block by URL, played in the show (7); port the Templates pane and the Sales pitch template (8); return Alt text to the default view (9); fix paste without formatting for text from outside the product (10). Items 1, 4, 5, 9 and 10 are fresh work on `main` of a day or less each; 2, 3 and 8 port self contained branch modules (the reader, the fonts package, the templates) whose tests exist on the branch; 6 and 7 are fresh work because the branch's publish state is unchanged and its media stack is the part the verifier found broken hosted.

What to leave. The sync engine rework of round five (A3; `room-client.ts`, `room.ts`, `blob-store.ts`, `blob.ts`, together about 4,000 changed lines on the branch) is not a feature and `main` has since rewritten the same files for the focus and return rounds (the presence store, the pulse, the access cache: 15,458 insertions in the same folders since the merge base), so nothing in this table depends on it and no item proposes porting it; a port would be a rewrite. Leave for a later round: the theme mode as the brand kit (the right answer for "any brand", but 977 lines of mode plus the theme CSS scope, and the Themes panel of `main` already carries the appearance switch), the Motion panel (research 07's own frustration table says not to invest, and a seller's two effects are a Fade and an Appear by paragraph), Page setup (the page sweep touched 123 sites on the branch), handouts (the pure layout function exists on the branch and its wiring did not land), the spell check card (a new package plus a Worker; the browser's own marks stand today), ODP and SVG, equations, the Dictionary, chat (broken on the blob tier by the verifier's finding 3), offline editing (the show already works without the network), and the mask galleries (133 of 136 cells draw as a box until the geometry interpreter lands with its tests).

## 4. What round five built, feature by feature, and the risk of bringing it to main

The branch is one commit on `d5a1be5`, 1,281 files and 407,632 insertions against its base, most of it the font files, the fixtures and the dictionaries. `main` has moved eight commits since that base (the fix, focus and return rounds) and rewrote the files a port would touch: against `d5a1be5`, `main` changed `packages/viewer/src/Editor.tsx` by 1,186 lines, `EditorShell.tsx` by 228, `model.ts` by 669, `room.ts` by 1,154, `blob-store.ts` by 1,034 and added the presence store and the pulse; the branch changed `Editor.tsx` by 312, `InlineText.tsx` by 108, `actions.ts` by 1,709 and `blob-store.ts` by 638. Nothing on the branch merges cleanly into those files, so every item below is read as a module port or a fresh build, never a merge.
