# Judge seller: the product round as a seller and a marketer would use it

Written 2026-09-19 by the judge for the seller lens, over `docs/PRODUCT.md` (the synthesizer's specification, 540 lines) and the five production audits under `docs/gslides-parity/product/` (`audit-seller.md`, `audit-brand.md`, `audit-gaps.md`, `audit-interface.md`, `audit-assist.md`) with their evidence folders. Every source fact below was read on the shared checkout at `6e15d60` (`packages/schema/src/layouts.ts`, `packages/chrome/src/menus/model.ts`, `strings.ts`, `dialogs/Share.tsx`, `dialogs/Download.tsx`, `apps/studio/src/server/authorize.ts`, `decks/templates/blank/deck.json`, `docs/gslides-parity/SPEC-5.md`, `docs/gslides-parity/research/07-sales-users.md`) or from the audits' JSON tables and pictures. I drove nothing on production; the audits' measurements stand as the evidence and each rejection names the item that carries it. No source file was touched and no git write command ran. Copy rules: plain technical English, sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, full sentences.

The lens: the seller and the marketer at General Translation first, and at any brand after. Does the round make the daily work faster and friendlier, with the friction list fixed and not patched; can a marketing team set the brand up in an afternoon in a way a seller never breaks; do the fonts and templates answer Kevin's asks; is translation shaped right for General Translation; is anything a designer's toy rather than a seller's tool; is the wording plain.

## Verdict

Revise. The document is the right shape and most of its friction fixes are the right size, and it is not ready to bind seven lanes because it asserts two things a seller depends on without a row that proves them (a colleague co editing from the copied link, and the one click PowerPoint on a deck with a table), leaves the brand kit's deployment default and its reset pointing at General Translation on every deployment, drops two audit items that decide a General Translation seller's week (the 85 slide company deck's exports never arriving, and the tailoring pass), names the 85 slide GT narrative "General presentation" on every brand's gallery, and puts one designer's word in front of a sales org where it means a prospect. The nine rejections and fourteen additions below are each one change to the document, not a new round.

## 1. What holds for the seller

The document read the seller audit whole and kept its order: ranks 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 10, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 22 of section 2 are the fixes the audit asked for at the size it asked (select what was inserted and land it in free space, one title over one body, the file named after the title, download from the menu row with honest progress, typed addresses linked, the picture centred and drawn at once with a caption row, no engineering noun in a snackbar, the trash with Undo and a Recent row that drops the deck, the toolbar Image button opening the chooser, the home page leading with the seller's sentence). The brand kit as a record on the deck with a panel, live preview and one write per control (section 4.1) is the shape a marketer can learn in an afternoon and the one an agent can drive, and the decision to keep the theme mode P1 follows research 07's own sentence that a seller never opens a theme editor. The font catalog of 26 open faces with the kit's two roles first (4.2) answers "add more font options too" and the export capture fix makes the face travel into the PowerPoint. Save as template, the Templates pane, the gallery page and the Sales pitch (4.3) are what a marketer hands a seller. The PowerPoint theme part from the kit and fonts embedded by default (4.5) fix the customer seeing Office blue and orange. The interface pass (section 3) is craft a seller reads by and not decoration: contrast a seller can read at a laptop's brightness, a toolbar that folds instead of cutting its last three controls, tooltips that stop covering the row under them, Bold that lights after Cmd+B. Section 8's rows are specific enough to drive and section 9 gives a reason for every deferral. The document carries no em dash, its headings carry no trailing period, and it reports measurements as measurements.

## 4. Notes

- The assist v1 (section 6) is not a designer's toy; the cards, the accept gate, one undo step and the author mark are the right guardrails, and the outside write snackbar fixes a real defect (audit-assist 1 and 7). It is the least measured surface against the seller's week: research 07's seller tailors an existing deck and rarely writes new copy, and the rewrite, notes and agenda cards serve the marketer building a deck more than the seller sending one. Addition 6 puts the deterministic pass first and the cards over it; the cost figures in 6.3 are sound.
- The document's own copy passes the rules: no em dash, sentence case, headings without trailing periods, measurements reported as such and specification marked as specification. The `/home` hero sentence "Build the pitch, present it and send the link, in one place" is a plain list and not a parallelism.
- Rank 17 fixes Insert > Image as a one row parent and then returns By URL in section 5, so Insert > Image is a two row submenu again and only the toolbar button's click opens the chooser; the row `images.insert.menu-direct` tests the toolbar button only, which is consistent, and rank 17's "After this round" cell should say so plainly.
- The failure snackbar "The picture could not be uploaded: <reason>" needs the rule that the reason is a sentence and never a code or an action id, under research 07 rule 22.
- The kit is on the deck and the Brand kit panel is one click from the Theme button for every seller with edit rights, so "a seller would never break" the kit holds for the slots (no handles, no drag) and not for the record: any seller can change the primary colour or the logo of their own deck. Without sign in there is no marketer role to gate the panel; the document should say that the lock is the template (read only) and the reset, and that a role gate comes with sign in.
- The brand audit's "Use on every slide" and the panel's Logo section are the two things a General Translation marketer and a customer's marketer both need on day one, and the document has them right, including the snackbar with Undo.
- Section 4.2's catalog omits Geist, the one face the seller audit named that is not in the 26; question 7 lets Kevin add it as one row and one folder, and nothing in the document blocks it.
- The document cites audit-interface 5 for the kit and never cites audit-interface 6 (the assistant entry) or audit-assist 12, 16 and 18 by number; the items are covered by description in 6.6, and the citations would make the coverage checkable.
- The two departures the synthesizer made from the audits (the assist shipped this round rather than designed, per audit-interface's summary; translation deferred rather than the assist audit's row 6) are defensible on the two day size and are recorded in section 1; with additions 6 and 9 they stop costing the seller and the company anything this round.
