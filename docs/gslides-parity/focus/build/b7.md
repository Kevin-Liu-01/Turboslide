## 1. The diagnosis per fix

Every line below was read from the day 0 tree and reproduced in a unit test against the blob fake (`packages/store/src/blob-fake.ts`) or the file store, as the tests of section 3 say; the audits are cited by row.

### Rank 3 (severity 3): undo or redo of a saved slide delete refused with a RangeError

Two mechanisms, both on the blob tier.

1. The admission judged the write against this instance's mirror without reading the client's base. `admitOnBlob` (`room.ts`) took `room.live()`, which on the blob tier is `store.read()` within the store's 750 ms sync window, and ran `landCandidate` on it; `post.base.seq` was never compared. An instance whose mirror was one revision behind the client (the delete had landed on another instance) applied the undo's `slide.insert` to a document that still carried the slide and refused it as `invalid` with the reducer's sentence, "Slide "split-5" already exists"; the redo's `slide.remove` on an instance behind the undo met "No slide "split-5"". The client showed the refusal with the mutation (audit-slides rows 93, 95, 96). The tab's revision equalled the server revision before each refusal, exactly as the audit recorded: the client was right and the instance was behind.
2. The mirror could claim a document at the manifest's revision with another slide set. `pull` (`blob-store.ts`) proved a document by the manifest's etag alone on the fresh path and read the slide bodies from the prefix listing, which lags the store by up to a minute (the note on `pull`): a slide added a moment ago was not in the listing and never fetched, a removed slide's body was still listed. `loadDeckDir` reads the slides the manifest lists, so a missing body left the slide out of the document while the manifest named it, and the next write on that slide met "No slide".

### Rank 5 (severity 3): pictures refused as stale while a write is pending

The editor's `call` (`packages/viewer/src/Editor.tsx` 1079 to 1082, B3's file) stamps `asset.add` with `revisionRef.current`, the page's count, which runs one ahead of the store while the page's own write is in flight and one behind while another tab's write is; the store refused either as stale, `insertPicture` caught the error and returned. B3 has landed the server half in `packages/materials/src/actions.ts` (`commitAssetsAtHead`: the record is additive and is written against the store's head). This lane owns the client stamping in the controller: the window handler of the asset actions waited for nothing and forwarded the page's base as it came, and a refusal reached no snackbar from the controller.

### Rank 6 (severity 3): a picture as the first action on a /new draft

`runDeckActionFn` (`agent-actions.ts`) opened the deck's dispatcher on an unsaved draft: `openDeckStore` on the blob tier answered "No deck untitled-… in the Blob store" (audit-images row 1). Only the draft's first `writeDeck` created the deck (`write.ts`: `createStoredDeck` and `recordNewDeck` before the decision); an asset action had no such step, and the controller had no way to learn the deck was created by it (the address moves on `DECK_CREATED_EVENT`, which only `writeDeck` raised).

### Rank 7 (severity 2): the card Rename, the row Rename, Make a copy and the listing after Restore

`blobDecks.list()` read every manifest through one origin `get`, whose body the CDN keeps in its overwritten form for a while after `head()` answers the new etag (the measured fact the store's own docblock records). The listing therefore showed the state before the last push, the card sent that revision as `baseRevision`, and the store refused the rename as stale ("Rename: baseRevision 1 is stale; the document is at revision 2", audit-decks rows 34, 36); Make a copy met the same stale base through `checkRevision` (row 37); a restored deck was listed as trashed until the CDN caught up (row 51). The trash and restore stamps and the upload of a new deck pushed `deck.json` without a snapshot, so no immutable name held the current manifest for a reader that met the lagging body.

### Rank 13 (severity 2): typing bursts after a refused write

Two halves. On the server, `admitOps` and `admitOnBlob` judged the entries of one POST in order on a running document; when entry k was refused, the entries after it were judged on the document without k while they had been written on a text that carried k's insertion, so each later splice met "text.splice: 10 plus 0 is outside a text of 7 characters" in turn (audit-text row 21). On the client, the room client dropped a refused op from `pending` and re-folded the rest on the server document without moving them, so a splice typed after a refused word was folded at the old offset or dropped as unplaceable, and the ops still unsent left with the old offsets. The doubled words the audit saw ("Onboarding Onboarding") are the text session's re-insertion after the document lost the word (the text and slides lane's half, request R3).

### Rank 19 (severity 2): a comment on the title placeholder

`placeAnchor` (`comments.ts`) ran `resolveAnchor` on the live document and refused any orphan with "the anchor names nothing on the current document (block removed)". The chrome's `anchorAtSelection` builds a block anchor from the selection's block id, and on a title or statement slide the selected placeholder is a slide field (the heading, the big line), not a block, so the id named nothing (audit-present row 35). The chrome lane has since made `anchorAtSelection` fall back to the slide when the slide has no such block (`comments-model.ts`); the server had no fallback and a wrong sentence.

### Rank 20 (severity 2): a second browser's edits arrive late or never

The version record went up after the manifest commit (round four of SPEC-4 0.33). Every other instance pulls only when the manifest's etag moves (`syncNow`) and fetches records by number inside that pull; a record stored after the commit was therefore missed by the pull the commit triggered and not fetched again until the next commit. The blob channel's `announce` reads `store.records()`, found nothing above its position, and the watch fires only when the mirror's revision changes, so the op was delivered with the next write or never (audit-present rows 31 and 33: 2.4 s once, then three edits lost for three minutes).

### Rank 21 (severity 2): Restore refused with "the version log breaks between versions 3 and 4"

The same window. A writer whose pull missed the newest record (stored after the other instance's commit) numbered its own record from its mirror's last one, reused the number and put the record with `overwrite: true`, so the other instance's record was overwritten and the log carried version 4 from revision 4 right after version 3 at revision 3 (audit-present row 44, the exact sentence of `assertContiguous`). Name current version met the collision as "Another instance saved version 16 first" (row 43).

## 2. The change

### `packages/store/src/blob-store.ts`

- `pull`: the bodies a document is made of are the ones its manifest names (`manifestSlideIds`, exported), fetched by name and each proven against its own head, on the fresh mirror's base (step 2) and on the fresh path (step 4); a missing or lagging body leaves the document unproven, and `write()` refuses to commit on it (`StaleMirrorError`, a lost race for the room). The replay (step 3) skips a record that moves nothing (a named version) and stops once the derived manifest is the head's. After a document is proven, a local record whose revision is above it leaves the mirror (`dropRecordsAbove`): a claim in flight or a leftover, fetched again by number once the manifest moves.
- `write()`: the version record joins round two as a claim on its number, `claimRecord`, put with `overwrite: false` beside the snapshot and the changed bodies, before the commit. A number another writer holds is `RecordTakenError`: the write is run once more from the store's current document after `CLAIM_RETRY_MS` and a second refusal is the conflict outcome. A record whose revision is above the base and older than `CLAIM_GRACE_MS` (60 s) is the leftover of a writer that stopped before its commit and is taken over. A claim this write stored and could not commit (a precondition failure, a contested snapshot, any other error) is released. Round four is the removed bodies alone.
- `pushDeckDir` (the seed, `create`, `copy`) and `stamp` (`trash`, `restore`) store the snapshot of the manifest they push before `deck.json`, through the module level `storeSnapshot`.
- `list()`: `currentManifest` reads each deck's manifest at the head: this instance's mirror when its manifest row names the head's etag (no body read), else the origin body when its md5 is the etag, else the snapshot the etag names, else the body the CDN served.
- `saveVersion`: unchanged in behaviour; the docblock says why the refusal now names a true race.

### `packages/realtime/src/blob.ts`

`announce` publishes an external checkpoint at the store's revision when that revision is above the last record it could announce, so a tab reloads at the head instead of waiting for an op that never comes.

### `apps/studio/src/server/room.ts`

- `admitOnBlob` reads the live document through `liveForBase`: when the client's base is above the mirror's revision the store is synced by force and the document read again. A candidate the reducer refuses is answered through `blobRefusal` (exported): judged against a document at another revision than the client's base it is a 409 `resync` at the head, never an `invalid` rejection; judged against the base it stays a rejection whose message names both revisions.
- `admitOps` and `admitOnBlob` transform the entries after a refused one past the undo of its splices (`undoOfSplices`, exported), as they transform past what landed since the base.
- `admitServerWrite` on the blob path: an `invalid` outcome's message names the instance's revision and the write's base.

### `apps/studio/src/server/agent-actions.ts`

`DRAFT_CREATING_ACTIONS` and `createsDraft` (exported): `asset.add`, `asset.dither`, `material.capture`, `picture.materialize`, `slide.setBackgroundPicture`, `slide.setBackgroundMaterial` and `slide.import` run on an unsaved draft create the deck first (`createStoredDeck` and `recordNewDeck`, before the decision, as `writeDeck` does). The handler answers `{ output, created }`; `runDeckAction` keeps its output only contract and `runDeckActionDetailed` (exported) hands the controller both.

### `apps/studio/src/editor/controller.tsx`

- `serverSide` for the announcing asset actions: `await idle()` then the request's `baseRevision` is the acknowledged revision (`reportedRevision()`); a refusal is published to the state and said in the snackbar with the action's name before it is rethrown.
- On a draft with no room, the asset action marks `draftInFlight` so the bursts typed during the upload queue (`draftEnqueue`), and `adoptCreatedDeck` on `created`: the editor deck is read back, the document set, the room attached, `DECK_CREATED_EVENT` raised (the address moves, the save words change), the queue replayed.

### `apps/studio/src/server/comments.ts`

`placeAnchorOn` (exported, pure): a block or text anchor orphaned as `block removed` on a slide the document holds becomes the slide anchor; any other orphan is refused with a sentence that names the anchor kind, the block or cell and the slide, and the reason.

### `packages/realtime/client/room-client.ts`

`PendingOp.inverse` (the mutations that undo the op on the document it was applied to, from `apply`); `rebasePast(refused)` moves every pending op recorded after a refused one past its inverse and returns an op that cannot move to its author; called from the `reject` event, the POST's `rejected` list and `fold` when an op no longer applies.

### 8.1 The diagnosis

**F12, `decks.trash.lists-after-restore`.** The finding's two suggested mechanisms are both already in place on this tree: the listing reads every manifest at the head (section 2, `currentManifest`), and the trash page refetches after Restore. What remains is the reload inside the restore's own flight time. The row clicks Restore, asserts the card left the trash (the optimistic hide, instant) and loads `/decks` at once; the trash page's `restoreStoredDeck` is still in flight, and the fresh `/decks` load reads the store before the stamp's `deck.json` push lands, so its listing still carries the trash stamp and the page, which refetches only after a rename, a copy or an undo, never gains the card. Evidence, `b7-restore-listing.mjs` against the enforce preview, three cycles of File > Move to trash, Restore, a fresh `/decks` load:

| Cycle | The restore server function answered | `/decks` hydrated after the click | `home.card` in the first response | Within 5 s | The next GET of `/decks` |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------ |
| 1     | 300 ms after the click               | 824 ms                            | yes                               | yes        | listed, not in the trash |
| 2     | 665 ms after the click               | 992 ms                            | no                                | no         | listed, not in the trash |
| 3     | 416 ms after the click               | 693 ms                            | yes                               | yes        | listed, not in the trash |
