# Turboslide identity and performance specification, round four

The binding specification for round four of Turboslide, written 2026-09-13 against `main` at `28cb63b` (`61b16e4` plus the README commit) and the round three tree in flight in the working directory (`docs/gslides-parity/SPEC-3.md`, `MILESTONES-3.md`, `build-3/b1.md` to `b6.md`; round three is being built as this document is written). This document is an addendum in the shape of SPEC-2.md and SPEC-3.md: where it is silent, SPEC.md, SPEC-2.md and SPEC-3.md stand; where they disagree, this document wins for round four. It is the synthesis of the three brand proposals in `docs/gslides-parity/design-4/` (`proposal-1-dither.md`, `proposal-2-shader.md`, `proposal-3-type.md`) and the performance plan beside them (`performance-plan.md`, `perf-budget.mjs`) under the three judgments (`judge-1.md`, `judge-2.md`, `judge-3.md`). Two judges chose proposal 1 (47 and 45 points against 43 and 44 for proposal 3 and 39 and 42 for proposal 2); judge 2 chose proposal 2 made monochrome (47 against 46 for proposal 1). Proposal 1, the dither is the identity, is the winner; the grafts the judges named from proposals 2 and 3 are carried in section 0; judge 2's dissent is recorded in section 8 so Kevin can override the pick. The five research reports of `docs/gslides-parity/research-4/` are the facts behind every claim.

Kevin's directives for the round, verbatim: (a) "make transitions between different websites and doing stuff in the slides so much faster and more performant"; (b) "also make a custom turboslide icon and favicon and everything and custom ui aesthetic and branding that uses awesome bayer dithers and shaders and unique beautiful ui like a modern clean but also technical and agent-supporting vercel and opinionated and better and turbo-fast (and explain why its turbofast, like built on rust?) vercel-ified version of google slides almost. and give it a /home page too". The standing directives bind every sentence: plain technical English (sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, no trailing periods on headings, full sentences), Google Slides' behaviours mimicked and Google's and Vercel's artwork never copied (Vercel is the reference for tone, density and finish), a sales organisation plus agents as the audience, every capability an action, and a speed story that maps to what the code does and never claims what is not true.

## How to read this specification

- "P1", "P2", "P3" are the three proposals; "J1", "J2", "J3" the three judges; "PP" is `design-4/performance-plan.md` and "PP 3.2" its section 3.2; "R01 6.3" cites `research-4/01-brand-references.md` section 6.3, and R02 to R05 the other four reports; "SPEC-3 6.7" cites the round three specification. SPEC, SPEC-2, AGENTS.md and `docs/hosting.md` are the repository documents by those names.
- Round three ships first (0.1). A change the performance plan proposed that round three already builds is marked "carried by round three" and is not built again; a change round three's design supersedes is marked so with the SPEC-3 row that replaces it; the rest is this round's. Every line citation below names the tree at `28cb63b`; the integrator re-reads each one on the round three ship commit before merge 1 (section 6.6).
- The identity is one mark, one wordmark, one icon set, one card, one token sheet and one build script; every asset is produced by `scripts/build-brand.ts` from `packages/theme/brand/` and `packages/theme/src/brand.ts`, so swapping the mark (section 8, decision 1) costs the brand builder's files and nothing else.
- Every number the `/home` page shows is a fact of the tree or a measurement of a named run, and the page carries the file or report under it (0.20, 0.25). "Today", "after" and "never" (PP section 4) are the tenses the copy may use: present tense only for a row marked today on the tree the page ships from.
- Rules of the text: plain technical English, sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, no trailing periods on headings, full sentences in body text; buttons are Title Case; the product is written "Turboslide" (R01 6.1).

## 1. The identity

### 1.1 The mark

A slide and the plate cut from it. On the 1600 by 900 sheet every GT opener puts a two tone picture under the whole slide and a title plate lower left, and `plateClear` (`packages/effects/src/metrics.ts`, `PLATE_BOXES.opener` = `[137, 500, 740, 271]`) measures that no lit cell intrudes into the plate. The mark is that composition at its smallest.

Construction (`packages/theme/src/brand.ts`, moved from `design-4/dither/mark.mjs` with its tests):

- Grid: 8 by 8 cells. `WINDOW = [1, 5, 4, 7]` (columns 1 to 4 and rows 4 to 6 are the window), so the body keeps a one cell rail on the left and the bottom and the window is 4 by 3 cells: 50 percent of the width and 37.5 percent of the height, at 12.5 percent from the left and the bottom.
- Field: `field(u, v) = 1 - 0.75 * (windowDistance(u, v) / D_MAX)`, density 1 along the window's edge and 0.25 at the far corner (`FIELD_END = 0.25`; 0.5 would be a checkerboard, 0 would dissolve the silhouette).
- Threshold: the pipeline's own test, `tone > (bayer8(y, x) + 0.5) / 64`, so the mark's cells are the cells the pipeline would light for that field.
- `markBits(N)` for N a multiple of 8: at N = 8 every body cell is ink (the solid form); N = 32 gives 2 px cells at 64 px, N = 64 gives 8 px cells at 512 px. A raster is drawn on its own cell grid and never resampled (R02 5.4).
- `markPath(unit)`: the solid form as one even odd path on a 16 unit box, `M0 0h16v16H0z M2 8h8v6H2z` at `unit = 2`. It is what the favicon, the title row, the app bar, the print bar and the terminal banner draw.
- `cellRects(image)`: row runs of lit cells as `<rect>` elements, so `mark.svg` at 64 cells is a few hundred elements and not 4,000 (J3 2.2 item 5).
- `markBlocks(N)`: the bitmap as half block characters, two grid rows per text line.

The sizes, with the threshold of 0.5:

| Use                                                           | Size           | N         | Cells                                                                           | Colours in the raster                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Favicon, `icon.svg`, app bar, print bar, terminal             | 16 px          | 8         | solid                                                                           | 3 on the tile (plate, frame, ink); 2 on a transparent ground |
| Title row                                                     | 24 px          | 8         | solid (the 16 unit path at 1.5 units per pixel: integer edges at 3, 12, 15, 21) | 2                                                            |
| ICO 32 and 48 px entries, the horizontal lockup at 66 px type | 32 and 48 px   | 8         | solid                                                                           | 3 on the tile                                                |
| Not found, the stacked lockup, the README                     | 64 px          | 32        | 2 px                                                                            | 2                                                            |
| Touch icon tile (180 px tile, 128 px mark)                    | 128 px         | 32        | 4 px                                                                            | 2                                                            |
| Manifest icons (192 and 512 tiles)                            | 160 and 448 px | 40 and 56 | 4 and 8 px                                                                      | 2                                                            |
| The full mark, the OG card                                    | 512 px         | 64        | 8 px                                                                            | 2                                                            |

Every raster's colour count is read back by `build-brand.ts --check` and written to `mark-geometry.json` (0.11). Never: a gradient, a second colour, a 2.5D rendering, a rounded corner, an outline, a rotation. The mark is `currentColor` wherever it stands alone; the tile adds the plate and the frame as the host's colours.

Why this form: it is the product's own composition, the one the exporter measures; it survives 16 px because the small form is solid with two 2 px features; it carries the dither from 64 px with no second drawing; it is not a triangle, a cube, a Z, a roundel, a chevron pair, a speed line or a yellow rectangle with a white rectangle (R01 6.3 item 12); it reads as a form before a letter, so a page in another script carries it without a Latin word beside it (R01 item 13).

### 1.5 The icon set and the build

`scripts/build-brand.ts` at the repository root, run as `node scripts/build-brand.ts` through Node's type stripping, with `pnpm build:brand` and `pnpm build:brand -- --check` in the root `package.json`; no new dependency (sharp, pixelmatch, playwright-core are in the catalog). Steps, R02 6.3 adapted to the generated mark:

1. Import `markBits`, `markPath` from `@turboslide/theme/brand`; write the SVG sources of 1.4.
2. Rasterize each PNG at its target size on its own cell grid (`cellRects` as `<rect>` cells at the size's cell width; never a downscale), the plate tile for 16, 32 and 48, the ink tile for the touch and manifest icons, the paper plate for `icon-dark-*`'s inverse.
3. Encode 192 and 512 as palette PNGs; keep 16, 32, 48 and 180 truecolor RGBA for the ICO writer.
4. Write `favicon.ico` with three 32 bit BMP entries and AND masks (R02 6.3 step 5; the design copy's writer round trips in Pillow with zero differing pixels).
5. Write the maskable icons (the mark inside the 40 percent safe circle: a 256 px square's corner is 181 px from the centre, the circle's radius 205), the monochrome icon (the solid form as alpha), `manifest.webmanifest` and `robots.txt` from `site.ts` (`Allow: /og/`, `Disallow` for `/new`, `/decks/trash`, `/print/`, `/edit/`).
6. Capture and cut the twins: `turboslide material capture paper:liquid-metal --uniforms <hero.recipe.json> --anchor <scan> --two-tone --plate lower-left` against a scratch deck folder, the anchor scan of 0.7, `encodePng1` with the theme palette (0.12), the ink fraction sum check; copy the chosen frame's twins to `apps/studio/public/brand/hero-{dark,light}.png`, frames two and three to `figure-{dark,light}.png` and `notfound-{dark,light}.png`, and the card's 10 px cell screen to `og-screen-{dark,light}.png`. 7. Render `og-template.html` to `og/turboslide.png` through `@turboslide/headless` at 1200 by 630 and device scale factor 1; palette PNG when the render has at most 256 colours; fail over 1 MB; compare under `--check` only when the local browser is the `chromium-1217` build.
