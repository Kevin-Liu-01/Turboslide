### Deviations

- `fit`, the max height from the room on the plate's side, is passed for the menus alone.
  D1's `place()` shifts on both axes before floating-ui's `size`, which then reads the whole
  window's height, so a fitted plate taller than the room under its anchor moves over its anchor
  (read: the font picker grew to 884 px at top 8, over its control). The menus keep that, as
  `placeMenu` did; every other plate bounds its own height in its sheet and stays under its
  anchor: the font plate at 420 px, the toolbar's picker plate and the sidebar row menu from their
  placed top (`--ts-plate-top`). The fix in `place.ts` is a request to D1 (below).
- `chrome.layers.menu-over-dialog` reads the browser's own select list and a control's tooltip:
  no dialog of the editor opens a menu of the chrome.
- `chrome.layers.follows-anchor` reads the Theme panel's font picker, the editor's one font
  picker in a scrolling panel; DESIGN.md 11 names Format options, which has none.
- The export menu (`ExportMenu.tsx`, D1's file) is mounted nowhere in the product; its sheet's
  z-index reads the scale, and its card takes the layer when it returns (request below).
- The tooltip's name leaves `cv11` and `ss01` in this push, with its plate.

## Requests

1. **D1 or the integrator, `packages/chrome/src/place.ts`** (`chrome.layers.follows-anchor`,
   DESIGN.md 2.4): in `middlewareOf`, push `size` before `shift` when `fit` is set (or shift with
   `crossAxis: false` then). floating-ui's `size` gives the whole clipping height when `shift` is
   enabled on that axis, so today a fitted plate taller than the room under its anchor is shifted
   over the anchor. With the order changed the menus can stay under their titles and scroll inside,
   and every plate can take `fit`.
2. **D1 or the integrator, `packages/chrome/src/ExportMenu.tsx`**: if the export menu is mounted
   again, its card takes `usePlate(card, { layer: 'popover', anchor: button })` and `.pt-float`.
3. **The integrator, `packages/viewer/src/InlineText.tsx` and `InlineText.css`** (no lane owns
   them): the link popover over a slide (`.ts-link-pop`) is a small floating plate at radius 0 on
   `--pt-hair`; under the ladder it takes `.pt-float` (6 px, `--pt-edge`, the ring) and stays in
   the stage's local order.
