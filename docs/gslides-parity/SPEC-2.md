# Google Slides parity specification, round two

The binding specification for the second Google Slides parity round of Turboslide, written 2026-09-12 against `main` at `a65b313` (round one shipped: `docs/gslides-parity/SPEC.md`, `MILESTONES.md`, `BUILD-STATUS.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`). This document is an addendum in the shape of SPEC.md: where it is silent, SPEC.md stands; where the two disagree, this document wins for round two. Amended 2026-09-12 for Kevin's third directive (the canvas): section 1 is the canvas model, section 6 its features, and section 0 rows 0.69 to 0.95 record every decision the amendment makes; rows 0.96 onward record the findings of the canvas review (`docs/gslides-parity/build-2/review-canvas.md`) applied in place the same day. Section numbers of the form "SPEC 7.7" refer to SPEC.md; "section 2" alone refers to this document. The research reports R01 to R11 in `docs/gslides-parity/research/` are cited as in SPEC.md and were not reopened for this round; no Google page was opened.

Kevin's directives, verbatim: "so i think when we land here we should be on a new slide, but its a template with repeated kind of slide templates you can use. but otherwise it has all the features and exact behaviors of google slides...literally search up and research everything google slides offers and positioning of tabs to import stuff and do stuff and mimic it perfectly. then clean up our interface and make it so much easier to use, remember this is actually going to be used by majority sales in our org" and "keep going on all of these and dont stop until literally all google slides features are supported with full agent queryability and editability esp on locals". The third, of 2026-09-12: "but also remember, we must, must must be able to drag and move around ANYTHING, including backgrounds and shaders but all text in the same exact way the google slides is like a canvas. bring in ALL the canvas features". This document calls them directive (1), (2) and (3).

## How to read this specification

- Round two flips the round one Later stubs and the omitted-for-scope items that a standalone editor can offer. Google services (Drive, Photos, Meet, Gemini, Keep, Sheets linking, Apps Script, AppSheet) stay out. Everything else Google Slides does is in this round or in round three (section 12), and a round three item stays a correct Later stub with the tooltip formula of SPEC "How to read this specification".
- Kevin's mandate overrides the round one defaults of SPEC 15 where they held a Google feature back. Section 0 records each override with its reason and the round one decision it replaces.
- Every slide is a canvas (section 1). A grammar layout or a fixed kind is the template and the initial geometry; the first canvas manipulation converts the slide losslessly to the freeform layout, and from then on every top level block is an object with the behaviours of section 6. The overlay layer of the first draft (`SlideBase.objects`) does not exist; a row below that still reads "objects" means the objects of a canvas, which are its top level blocks.
- Every capability a person gets in the UI is also an action in `packages/schema/src/actions.ts`, every new action has a CLI handler that works on a local checkout's `decks/` folder as well as against the hosted studio, and the reads stay complete: `slide.get` returns every new field, `deck.info` and `slide.list` report the new counts, and `export.text` carries the new text.
- Facts about Turboslide are lines of source at `a65b313`, named by path. `packages/schema/src/actions.ts` holds 69 action ids. `packages/chrome/src/menus/model.ts` holds 243 rows. `SCHEMA_VERSION` is 1 and stays 1. The working tree carries the stopped first attempt's additive edits (git status names them); what they wrote for the objects layer is removed by the builders whose files they touch, as section 0 rows 0.69 onward say.
- Rules of the text: plain technical English, sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, no trailing periods on headings, full sentences in body text. Labels are Google's words; agent vocabulary never appears in the default view or its tooltips.

## 1. The canvas

Kevin's directive (3), verbatim: "we must, must must be able to drag and move around ANYTHING, including backgrounds and shaders but all text in the same exact way the google slides is like a canvas. bring in ALL the canvas features". This section is the document model that delivers it; section 6 is the behaviours. Section 0 rows 0.69 to 0.95 record the decisions.

### 1.1 The model

- Every slide is a canvas. Every top level block on every slide kind (`title`, `statement`, `content`, `opener`, `mood`, `closing`) is an object a person drags, resizes, rotates, reorders, groups, duplicates, deletes and edits in place as Google Slides does: the layout's own text (headings, paragraphs, leads, the big statement), pictures (an opener's or mood slide's full-bleed photograph is a picture object at the bottom of the z order that moves, resizes and crops like any picture), shader materials (a material block is a positioned object; resizing re-mounts the shader at the new box), plates, rules, marks, icons, tables, charts and diagrams.
- One document form holds a canvas: a content slide on the freeform layout whose `slots.main` holds every object with `pos: { x, y, w, h, z?, rotate?, flip?, group? }` (SPEC 2.1, `docs/freeform.md`, section 2.1 of this document). There is no second list: `SlideBase.objects`, `BlockSlot` `'objects'`, `slot: 'objects'` and the `.objects` layer of the first draft are removed (0.69). The grammar layouts and the five fixed kinds stay the templates and the initial geometry: New slide, Apply layout, the layout grid and the GT deck are unchanged, and a slide nobody has touched keeps its kind, its layout, its bytes and its pixels.
- The first canvas manipulation or insert on a slide that is not on the freeform layout converts that slide to the canvas losslessly (1.2) and the manipulation lands on the converted slide in the same write (1.6). Typing, table commands, typography, colour, fill and border writes, Apply layout, Change background's colour, Delete of a block and the round one reorder between the slots of a grammar slide do not convert: they are grammar writes and stay so. Selection, hover, Tab and a right-click never write, so they never convert.

### 1.2 The conversion

`packages/schema/src/canvas.ts` (B1) exports `toCanvas(slide: Slide, boxes: CanvasBoxes): { slide: ContentSlide; record: GrammarRecord; unplaced: BlockId[] } | null` (null for a freeform slide), `fromCanvas(slide): Slide | null` (the round one `toGrammar` extended to the kinds) and the `CanvasBoxes` type of 1.3. Pure over the document and a measurement; `packages/viewer/src/Freeform.tsx` `toFreeform` and `toGrammar` become calls to them. The result, kind by kind:

- Every kind: the slide becomes `{ kind: 'content', layout: { type: 'freeform' }, slots: { main }, template, grammar }`, keeping `id`, `title`, `notes`, `tags`, `skip`, `background` and `ext` as they are (the conversion writes no `ext` key, 0.99). `template` is the slide's `template` when written, else `derivedLayout(slide)` (SPEC 5.6), so Apply layout knows the layout the slide came from and the layout grid keeps marking it. `grammar` is the optional first class field `SlideBase.grammar?: GrammarRecord` (0.99): the round one `GrammarRecord` of `Freeform.tsx`, moved to `canvas.ts` with its own schema and extended with `kind`, `layout` (a content source), `slots` (block ids per slot, or `plate`), `boxes` (the `pos` each object received) and `fields` (a fixed kind's `mark`, `measure`, `picture`, `plate.side`, `plate.maxWidth`, `sectionId`), so `fromCanvas` restores the kind while nothing moved and Apply layout re-flows. It is not an `ext` key: `extPointers` never sees it, so the validator's `ext` issue (`validate.ts` line 372) does not fire on a converted slide. A slide the editor depth round converted carries the record at `ext.grammar`; `fromCanvas` reads that once and the slide's next write rewrites it as `grammar`.
- Every object gets `pos: { x, y, w, h, z }` from its measured box, integers in sheet pixels, `z` in paint order from 0; a block that already carried `pos` keeps it; a block without a measured box is stacked from the content origin and named in `unplaced` (round one rule).
- A grammar content slide: every block of every slot, in slot order, as today's `toFreeform`; `autofit` stays what the block carries (the content layouts' placeholders already carry `shrink`, 0.41).
- A title slide: the mark becomes `{ id: 'mark', type: 'mark', w: slide.mark.w, h: slide.mark.h }` at the svg's box (z 0); the heading becomes `{ id: 'heading', type: 'heading', level: 'h1', text: slide.heading, autofit: 'shrink' }` at the h1's box (z 1); the lead becomes `{ id: 'lead', type: 'paragraph', role: 'lead', tone: 'muted', measure: 56, text: slide.lead, autofit: 'shrink' }` at the p's box (z 2). The ids `heading` and `lead` are the ids the renderer gave the pseudo blocks (`data-block`), so a selection survives the conversion and `slide.get` names what the editor named.
- A statement: the big line becomes `{ id: 'big', type: 'heading', level: 'big', text: slide.big, typography: { align: 'center' }, autofit: 'shrink' }` at the `.big` element's box, whose width is the resolved measure; `measure` goes to `grammar.fields.measure`.
- An opener, a mood slide and the closing: the photograph becomes `{ id: 'picture', type: 'picture', asset: slide.picture.asset, position?: slide.picture.position, side: slide.plate.side }` (`side` is the plate side a material recipe composes for, 0.105) at `pos: { x: 0, y: 0, w: 1600, h: 900, z: 0 }`, the bottom of the stack (the kinds draw it at `inset: -57px` over the slide box, which is the whole sheet; `block-css.ts` line 15); the plate becomes `{ id: 'plate', type: 'box', fill: 'paper', strokeWidth: 0, padding: { top: 22, right: 26, bottom: 20, left: 26 }, pos: { …the plate's measured box, z: 1, group: 'plate' } }`; every block of `plate.blocks` follows at its measured box, z 2 onward in document order, with `pos.group: 'plate'` and `autofit: 'shrink'` on its heading and paragraph blocks; the closing's mark inside the plate becomes `{ id: 'mark', type: 'mark', w: 138, h: 88 }` in the same group, before the heading. `sectionId` goes to `grammar.fields.sectionId`; a converted Section header stays first in its section (the validator's opener rule applies to the opener kind) and Apply layout of Section header writes `sectionId` back from the section the slide sits in.
- Ids stay unique across the slide: the ids a kind's conversion creates are free on every stored slide of that kind in the GT deck and the templates (`picture`, `plate` and `mark` on the picture kinds, `mark`, `heading` and `lead` on the title kind, `big` on the statement; `canvas.test.ts` asserts it per kind, because the GT deck's content slide `mark.json` holds a block `mark`, which a content conversion never creates, 0.108); a collision takes the `-2` suffix as `withFreeId` does.
- The conversion is one `slide.replace`. `fromCanvas` is lossless while every object sits where the conversion put it (within 1 px) and the object set is the recorded set, and then restores the recorded kind with its fields; else the round one `grammarFit` reading applies to a content source and a fixed kind refiles through Apply layout. Apply layout on a canvas slide reads roles by block type in reading order (SPEC 5.5, unchanged: `extractContent` over `readingOrder`), reads the `picture` object as the slide's picture and the `plate` box as nothing (a ground), so Section header, Caption, Closing, Title slide and Main point re-flow a converted slide into fresh placeholders; SPEC 5.5's table gains the row "The picture object: the picture placeholder, or the background picture of a picture layout" and the row "The plate box: dropped (the layout draws its own plate)".

### 1.3 One measurer

- `packages/render/src/measure-dom.ts` (B2; browser safe, no `node:` import, no React) exports `measureCanvasBoxes(root: Element, stage: Element): CanvasBoxes`, the boxes the conversion needs from one rendered slide, in sheet pixels relative to the 1600 by 900 stage element, rounded to integers: `blocks[id]` for every `[data-block]` (a positioned block through its `.free[data-free]` wrapper, as `measureBoxes` does today), `picture` (`img.opener-img, img.mood-img`), `plate` (`[data-slot="plate"]`) and `mark` (the title's `svg[data-raster="mark"]` under `.left-mid`, or the closing's `svg.mark` inside the plate). The selectors are the constants `CANVAS_SELECTORS` the same module exports, so the renderer and the measurer change together. `CanvasBoxes.prompted` lists the ids whose measured box came from a prompt (the element holds the `.prompt` child), so `toCanvas` writes those objects at the prompt's box and never under one line box of the block's size (the fresh Title slide's empty `heading` and `lead` convert at the boxes the stage draws, and `positionObjectSchema`, whose `h` is positive, accepts the first drag; 0.97). The module also exports `awaitSheetReady(root: Element): Promise<void>`, self contained so `page.evaluate` can take its source: `document.fonts.ready`, then `document.fonts.load` for every face the root's text uses, `img.decode()` on every image under the root, the dither draw on every dither canvas under the root, then two animation frames; `packages/headless/src/ready.ts` `waitForReady` evaluates it for those steps and keeps its `ReadyInfo` reporting, so the editor and the headless page reach the same readiness before they measure.
- In the editor, the first manipulation renders the slide with `renderSlide` (`blockAttrs: true`, the current theme, `prompts: true`, so an empty placeholder keeps the prompt's box the stage draws, 0.97) into a hidden 1600 by 900 `.ts-sheet` root at scale 1 (`packages/viewer/src/canvas-measure.ts`, B4: `measureForCanvas(document, slide, theme): Promise<CanvasBoxes>`), awaits `awaitSheetReady(root)` (fonts, every image decoded, the dither canvases drawn), measures with `measureCanvasBoxes`, and unmounts it. The stage's own measurement (`measureBoxes` at the stage scale) keeps serving hover, selection and gestures and is not used for the conversion: a layout box at a half pixel divided by the stage scale rounds either way, and the CLI must write the same pos.
- From the CLI, MCP and HTTP, `slide.toCanvas` and every canvas write on a slide that is not on the freeform layout (1.6) render the slide the same way in a headless sheet page (`@turboslide/headless` `openSheetPage` at scale 1, `writeTempDocument` with `prompts: true`, `waitForReady`, which evaluates `awaitSheetReady`) and run `measureCanvasBoxes` inside `page.evaluate`: `packages/headless/src/measure.ts` gains `measureCanvas(page): Promise<CanvasBoxes>` beside `measureSlide`, evaluating the function's source from `@turboslide/render/measure-dom`. The two paths run one theme bundle, one font set and one function on a 1x page in Chromium (Chrome or Edge in the editor, Playwright's Chromium in the CLI), so the pos they write are identical there; Safari and Firefox lay out text at other fractional advances (the statement's measure is in `ch`), so a conversion made in one of them may differ from the CLI's by a pixel, which VERIFICATION-2 records and the walk does not cover (0.104). 11.8 asserts the identity in Chromium on one slide of every kind of the GT deck, and `packages/headless/src/canvas-measure.test.ts` asserts the headless measure of the fixture's `canvas-title` and `canvas-opener` equals the pos the fixture carries, that the fresh Title slide of `layouts.ts` `make` measures at the prompts' boxes with no zero height, and that a figure slide measures after its twins decode (0.97). `withCanvas(deps, slide, mutations)` (1.6) takes the measurement from `deps.measureCanvas(deck, slide): Promise<CanvasBoxes>`: the CLI and the MCP stdio server bind it to `@turboslide/headless` and open one sheet page per action call (`slide.toCanvas` with twenty ids launches one browser, a canvas write one), and the hosted studio binds it to the render worker facade of `apps/studio/src/server/render.ts` (the worker over HTTP when `TURBOSLIDE_WORKER_URL` is set, the CLI child process otherwise, the path `render.slide` takes), so headless Chromium never runs inside the actions function of the base tier (`vite.deploy.config.ts` line 128); B1 records the measured cost of one canvas write per transport in `build-2/b1.md` (0.104).
- The measurer reads `getBoundingClientRect` with no transform on the stage; the rotation order of 1.5 does not apply to a conversion, because a slide that is being converted has no rotated object yet.

### 1.4 The validator, the catalog, the renderer, the linter

- `pos` lives on the top level blocks of a freeform slide and nowhere else (the round one rule, `validate.ts` code `position`, severity 3); `pos.rotate` is a number in [0, 360), `pos.flip` is `'h' | 'v' | 'hv'`, `pos.group` is a slug and a tag on one block is allowed (an ungroup left it); `offSheet` feeds the lint `freeform/off-sheet` (0.96: severity 3 when the object's bounding box lies wholly outside the sheet, 2 when it crosses an edge), never a refusal, so a dragged object that leaves the sheet is a finding the judge reads, not a refused write; `offSheet` and `boxesOverlap` in `packages/schema/src/freeform.ts` read the axis aligned bounding box of a rotated `pos` box (0.107).
- The catalog's `allowedIn` loses `'objects'`; `credit` becomes `['plate', 'content']` so a converted plate's credit validates; the new `picture` type (2.6.4) is `['content']`, group `figure`, `export: 'raster'`, `assetPaths: ['/asset']`, `make` the fixture photo, `side` optional (0.105); `SlideBase.grammar` validates by its own schema and sits outside `extPointers` (0.99). `blockAssetRefs`, `slideLinkRefs`, `canonicalizeSlideText` and `documentBlocks` walk the slots and the plate as they do today and nothing else.
- The renderer draws a canvas slide through `renderFreeform` as it draws every freeform slide: each object in a `.free[data-free]` wrapper at its box in paint order (`sortByZ`, `z-index: order + 1`), `data-rotate`, `data-flip` and `data-group` when set, `transform: rotate(<deg>) scale(sx, sy)` with `transform-origin: center` (2.1.1, 2.1.2). ### 1.5 The two export paths

- Perfect: a canvas slide is in the 2x sheet raster by construction and an object past the sheet's edge is clipped by it (0.96); every `data-run` joins the invisible text layer; a rotated object's runs carry pptxgenjs `rotate` at the unrotated box (2.1.1); the picture object is in the raster; a linked object gets the invisible hit rectangle.
- Editable text: the measurer walks `.free[data-free]` as it does for every freeform slide; an object is native when its type is in `NATIVE_BLOCK_TYPES` (with `chart`) and a raster otherwise; the picture object is a raster of kind `shot` at its box, and when it covers the sheet at the bottom of the stack the builder writes it as `slide.background = { data }`, the form the picture kinds already export, so a converted and an unconverted Section header produce the same file; the plate box is a `SceneRect` with role `box`; an object past the sheet's edge is written at its box as is and PowerPoint clips it in the show (0.96); `pos.group` becomes the `@g:<tag>` object name suffix and the plate group one `p:grpSp` (2.1.3, 0.67); shapes emit in paint order.
- The order of one page pass in `extractScenes` (`packages/export/src/scene/extract.ts`, 0.46), because `Range.getClientRects()` and `getBoundingClientRect()` return transformed rectangles under a CSS transform and a screenshot of a rotated raster would be rotated twice by pptxgenjs `rotate`: (1) add `.ts-measure` to the sheet root, which sets `transform: none` on every `.free[data-rotate], .free[data-flip]`; (2) wait two animation frames; (3) measure every box and line box, and take the element screenshots of the blocks the export rasterises (icons, materials, pictures, shapes with text as rasters, charts in Perfect), writing `rotate` and `flip` on their scene entries from the data attributes; (4) remove the class; (5) wait two frames; (6) take the flatten sheet screenshot, which therefore shows the rotation. The measurer never measures with the class off and never screenshots the sheet with it on. `extract.test.ts` plants a rotated icon and asserts the element screenshot's aspect equals the unrotated box and the sheet screenshot differs from the unrotated render.
- The viewer's overlay (`packages/viewer/src/Freeform.tsx` `measureBoxes`) reads `getBoundingClientRect` of `.free` wrappers, which for a rotated wrapper is the bounding box of the rotated shape. The overlay therefore takes a positioned block's box from `pos` (scaled by the stage factor) and applies `rotate` and `flip` to the ring and handles itself; `measureBoxes` serves layout blocks and the run boxes only.
- TXT: object texts in reading order (`readingOrder`: y then x within a grid step).

Attachment follows the shape: `packages/schema/src/connect.ts` (B1) exports `followConnectors(slide, movedIds): Mutation[]`, the `block.set /pos` (and `/orientation`) mutations that put each attached end of every connector attached to one of `movedIds` back on its target's site after the target moved, resized, rotated or flipped. The editor's gesture end, the Size & rotation and Position fields, `block.align`, `block.distribute`, `block.rotate`, `block.flip` and the store action handlers of `block.set /pos` call it and travel the follow in the same write (one `slide.update`), so a connector never detaches on a move and the CLI behaves as the editor. `block.remove` of the target clears the attachment on its connectors in the same write; `block.duplicate` of a target together with its connectors renames the references, of a connector alone drops them; Apply layout of a grammar layout, which drops `pos`, drops the attachments too (a slot block has no `pos`). A connector is rerouted by dragging one of its end handles onto another shape (R05 A10, S15, S66; 0.103): while the handle is down the connection sites of the shape under the pointer show as 6 px rings, the end snaps to them, and the gesture end writes `pos`, `orientation` and `connect.start` or `connect.end` (set when the end lands on a site, removed when it lands away) in one `slide.update`; on the CLI the same write is `line.set` with `connect`, whose handler moves the end onto the site. A target's sites are rotated about its centre and mirrored by its flip before an end is placed (0.107). `connect.test.ts` asserts a moved, resized, rotated and flipped target keeps each connector's end on its site, that a reroute lands the end on the new target's site and drops the old attachment, and that removing the target detaches.

### 6.2 The surfaces the canvas keeps

- Rotation handle: 6.1 row 11. A rotated block's ring and handles rotate with it (the overlay applies the same transform); the eight resize handles resize along the rotated axes; the two end handles of a line kind drag the ends and write `pos` and `orientation`; while a handle is down the connection sites of the shape under the pointer show as 6 px rings and the end snaps to them, and the write sets `connect` for that end when it lands on a site and removes it when it lands away (a reroute, R05 A10; 0.103).
- Keys with an object selected and no caret: 6.1 rows 8, 10, 12 and 13 (0.78 for the rotate alias).
- Group selection: 6.1 row 14. A member's own fields are edited after the double click. A group rotates about the union box's centre: the drag ends in one `block.rotate` with `about: 'selection'`, which writes each member's `pos.rotate`, `pos.x` and `pos.y` (the document has no group rotation; 2.1.3 exports the members as rotated). A group resizes about the union box (0.102): a handle drag scales every member's `pos` by the union's x and y factors about the union's anchored edge or centre, typography and each member's `rotate` untouched (a rotated member's box changes ratio, not angle; whether Google scales font sizes with a group is unverified, section 13), as one `slide.update` of `block.set /pos` per member. A group flips as one `block.flip` with `about: 'selection'`: each member's `flip` toggles, its box mirrors about the union centre and its `rotate` becomes 360 minus the angle. A fill, border, dash, shadow or text write with a group selected (the group tail of 4.2, Format options' Colour, Text and Drop shadow) is one `slide.update` of the write per member that has the field; Format options on a group shows Size & rotation and Position over the union and Colour, Text and Drop shadow over the members. `canvas.test.ts` (viewer) asserts the member positions after a resize and a flip.
- Crop mode: 6.1 row 19; a drag inside pans the picture (writes the trim's offsets in the other direction); the converted photograph and a shot share the mode.
- Draw tools per kind: a shape preset pick or a line kind arms the tool (the pointer is a crosshair, the toolbar's Select is not pressed); a click places the default size (shape 240 by 160, text 480 by 64, line 320 by 8, table 960 by 320, chart 960 by 540) at the click; a drag draws the box; Shift constrains a shape to a square and a line to 45 degree steps; Option resizes from the centre; a connector's ends snap to the connection sites of the shape under the pointer (`sites()` of 2.3, rotated with the target, drawn as 6 px rings while the tool is armed and while an end handle of an existing line is dragged, 0.103, 0.107) and a snapped end records `connect.start` or `connect.end` (2.4.7), so the connector follows the shape from then on (0.65); an end dropped away from a site records nothing, and the Line section shows Detach for an attached end; Curve and Polyline take a click per point and end on a double click, Enter or a click on the first point (closed); Scribble samples the pointer every 8 px and simplifies to at most 64 points; Esc cancels a tool. ### 7.2 The markup rules

`packages/schema/src/text.ts` grows from four rules to five:

```
*text*                       the display run: weight 500 (unchanged)
[text](url)                  a link (unchanged); url may be a slide link
[text]{marks}                the mark span: marks is a space separated list of
                             i u s sup sub c:<color> h:<color>, in that canonical order
[text](url){marks}           a linked span
GT                           the mark (unchanged)
\*  \[  \GT                  escapes (unchanged); a literal "]{" after an escaped "[" stays literal
```

- `Run` becomes `{ t; b?; i?; u?; s?; sup?; sub?; color?: Color; hl?: Color; gt?; link? }`. `sup` and `sub` are exclusive; the parser keeps the first and drops the second with a canonicalization note in `validate` (severity 1, code `text`). A span may contain a display run (`[*x*]{i}`) and a display run may contain a span (`*[x]{i}*`); the canonical form puts the display run outside. Two adjacent spans with equal marks merge (`mergeRuns` compares every flag).
- `parseText` extends the `[` branch: after the matching `]`, an optional `(url)` and an optional `{marks}` in either order; at least one is required, else the brackets are literal. Unknown marks make the brackets literal. `serializeRuns` writes the marks in canonical order. `plainText` and the copy rules are unchanged (they read `t`).
- `canonicalText` per paragraph is unchanged in shape; `text.replace`'s inverse covers the whole new string as today.
- `renderRuns` wraps in this order: `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<s>`, `<sup>` or `<sub>`, `<span style="color">`, `<mark style="background">`, `<a>`; adjacent runs sharing outer marks share the element (as `<b>` does today).
- The measurer (`scene/measure.ts`) reads `font-style`, `text-decoration-line` (underline, line-through), `vertical-align` (super, sub), `color` and the `<mark>` background per run into `SceneStyle.italic`, `underline`, `strike`, `baseline`, `color`, `highlight`.
- `InlineText.runsFromNode` reads the elements back (section 6). The rewrite rule (0.54): the editor never rewrites the editable element's HTML while a burst is open; at a burst boundary it computes `serializeRuns(runsFromNode(el))`, and only when that string differs from the canonical string of the document's runs does it record the selection as plain text offsets (`[start, end]` counted in the Text's characters, with `\n` between paragraphs), replace the HTML with `renderRuns` of the canonical runs, and restore the selection from the offsets. Bold moves onto this path (`document.execCommand('bold')` leaves `InlineText.tsx`). `inline-text.test.ts` asserts the caret offsets before and after a rewrite are equal, that a burst with no drift performs no rewrite, and that Cmd+Z inside a burst still reverts the browser's last edit.

### 8.2 Immutable per revision documents

- Today (`packages/store/src/blob-store.ts`): `deck.json` and `slides/<id>.json` are overwritten in place; `versions/<n>.json` are immutable records; the commit point is `putDocument('deck.json', synced)` with `ifMatch` (lines 528 to 566), and the slides and the version record follow it. A mirror proves a body by hashing its bytes to the etag `head()` reports, which is the body's md5 (lines 194 to 196, measured 2026-09-11), or by replaying records, because the CDN serves an overwritten body stale for a while.
- This round (0.40): before it pushes `deck.json`, every committed write computes the md5 of the canonical `deck.json` bytes it is about to push and stores the whole `DeckDocument` as canonical JSON at `snapshots/<md5>.json` with `overwrite: false`; a snapshot that exists already (the same bytes, from a retry or an identical write) is left alone, which is correct because equal bytes are one document. Then `deck.json` is pushed with `ifMatch`, and the version record `versions/<n>.json` written by the same commit carries `snapshot: <md5>`. Two instances that both read revision r and race write different bodies and therefore different snapshot keys; the loser's snapshot is named by no record and no etag, is never served, and the retention prune removes it. A revision keyed name (the first draft) would let the loser's upload win the name while the `ifMatch` decided the revision (review 1).
- `pull()` gains a path before the replay: `head('deck.json')` answers the etag; when `snapshots/<the etag's md5>.json` exists, that snapshot is the current document, proven by the etag (its name is the hash of the bytes the etag names), the mirror is written from it, and no body is fetched and no revision is read. The replay path stays for decks written before this round and for a store whose `deck.json` push failed after the snapshot was stored (that snapshot is orphaned and pruned).
- `documentAtRevision(r)` reads `versions/<r>.json`, then `snapshots/<record.snapshot>.json` when the record carries the key, else the round one replay from the records. The batched export (8.1), the version history panel's Restore preview and `deck.copy` at a version read through it.
- The contention rule (B6, `docs/gslides-parity/build-2/b6.md` section 3 item 1; the integrator's amendment at merge 2): "different bodies and different keys by construction" holds while the two writers' manifests differ. Two writers that start from one revision and apply their writes inside the same clock millisecond push equal `deck.json` bytes (`revision` and `updatedAt` are the only manifest fields a slide write moves) with different slide bodies, and both compute one key. When the snapshot put is refused as existing, the store checks that the existing snapshot's etag is the md5 of its own body; a different body is `SnapshotContestedError`, which `write()` answers as a conflict before its manifest push, so no committed etag ever names a body its writer did not store and the caller retries from the current document as for any conflict.
- Retention: `write()` prunes, after a successful commit and without blocking the answer (`del` in one call), every snapshot that no record among the last 50 and no named version (`versions/<n>.json` with a note) points at; `deck.remove` deletes the prefix as today. `deck.info` reports `snapshots: number` on the Blob store.
- Migration: none runs. A deck with no snapshots reads as in round one; its next write creates the first snapshot. `hosted.test.ts` gains: the snapshot stored before `deck.json` and named by the record; the two writer race (the fake stands in for two instances that both read r; the winner's snapshot is the one `pull()` and `documentAtRevision(r + 1)` serve, the loser's is orphaned and pruned); the stale CDN body case (the fake serves a stale slide body and the snapshot wins); the retention prune keeping a named version's snapshot; `documentAtRevision` through the record's key and through the replay for a record without one. ### 11.8 The canvas walk

`apps/studio/e2e/canvas.spec.ts` (B4), against a dev server with `TURBOSLIDE_STORE=tmp` over a scratch copy of `decks/gt-brand` seeded through the window API:

1. One slide of every kind: a Title slide, a Main point (statement), a Section header (opener) whose photograph is a material asset, a Caption (mood), the Closing, and one content slide per grammar layout type (`cols`, `split`, `center`, `left-mid`, `stack`). On each, one object of every kind the slide holds (the heading, a paragraph, the lead, the big line, the mark, the photograph, the plate group and a plate block, a table, a list, a picture figure, a material block, an icon, a rule) is dragged 40 px right and 24 px down, resized 80 px wider from the east handle, rotated 15 degrees with the handle and Shift, sent backward and brought to front, in that order. The photograph's drag moves its bounding box past the right and bottom edges, which shows on the workspace (the editor lifts the sheet's clip, 0.96) and is one `freeform/off-sheet` finding at severity 2.
2. After every gesture the spec reads the server's version log through the window API and asserts exactly one write landed, and that the first write on each slide is a `slide.update` whose first mutation is the `slide.replace` of the conversion followed by the gesture's `pos` mutations; it asserts `slide.get` returns `pos` on every object and `template` naming the layout; it asserts Cmd+Z once restores the slide's kind and bytes.
3. Every slide the walk did not touch is read back through `slide.get` and compared byte for byte (canonical JSON) with the committed file; `node scripts/compare-to-shoot.mjs` on the committed GT deck stays at or under 0.5 percent (step 12), because the committed deck is never written.
4. The spec records the `pos` of every converted slide at `.turboslide/canvas-walk/<slideId>.json`; `apps/cli/src/commands/canvas.test.ts` (B1) and the verifier run `node apps/cli/bin/turboslide.mjs slide to-canvas <slideId> --deck <local copy> --json` on a fresh copy of the GT deck and assert the CLI's `pos` equals the recording for every object (1.3; the identity holds in Chromium, the browser the walk runs, 0.104), then `block set <slideId>#<blockId> /pos <box>` on an untouched grammar slide and assert one revision moved and the slide converted.
5. The photograph and the shader: on the Section header the picture object is dragged, resized, cropped (a double click, one handle, Enter) and sent to back after a text box was inserted over it; the material's canvas is asserted to be mounted at the object's new box after the resize (the `.ts-material-live` bounds equal the `pos` box at the stage scale within 1 px, at a box that is not 16:9, 0.105); the Editable text export of the copy asserts `slide.background` for the converted slide.
6. Rulers, guides, zoom and pan: View > Show ruler draws two rulers whose numeral counts are 14 and 8 (0 to 13 in and 0 to 7 in, the origin carrying its numeral, 0.108); Add vertical guide writes `deck.guides` with 800, a drag of the guide writes the moved position with the inch readout shown, Delete guide from its menu removes it; Cmd+plus steps the Zoom box to the next ladder value, 1600 typed into the Zoom box scales the sheet to 25600 px wide and the stage scrolls (0.101), a `wheel` with `ctrlKey` over the sheet changes `view.zoom` and not the page zoom, Space+drag scrolls the stage, Fit restores; View > Snap to > Guides off makes a drag write an unsnapped position.
7. Undo after each of steps 5 and 6's writes; the spec ends with the copy validating (`validate.run`) and `lint.run` reporting no finding above severity 2 on the converted slides (the photograph's severity 2 `freeform/off-sheet` is the one finding above 1 the walk expects, 0.96).
8. A fresh presentation from `/new`: the Title slide's empty heading is dragged before anything is typed; the write lands with `pos.h` of at least one line box and the deck validates (0.97). On the converted Section header, a press on the workspace starts a marquee that selects the plate group, a click there deselects, a right-click there opens the empty canvas menu, and the photograph's right-click menu ends with Change background and Guides (0.100).
