## Decisions

1. **Measurer precision 1/64 px, and the request that `boxPos` keep it.** The conversion's
   `boxPos` (B1, `packages/schema/src/canvas.ts`) rounds a measured box to integers. A GT slide's
   blocks sit at fractional layout positions (731.5, 420.4375), so the rounded object moves up to
   half a pixel and every glyph edge with it: 64 of 342 grammar to canvas pairs over the 0.5
   percent budget. With the measured value kept, the failures fall to 10, all theme dependent
   (below). Measured with the script's `--precision` flag: 2 gives 8 failures, 4 gives 9, 8 gives
   2, 16 gives 0 on a subset, 64 (Chromium's LayoutUnit) the same as raw. `MEASURE_PRECISION` is
   64; `measure-dom.ts` records the numbers.
2. **Theme dependent geometry.** The 10 remaining exact mode failures are the dark theme of five
   GT slides with a code `panel` (`nearest-page-routing` 1.61, `agent-api` 0.72, `archive` 0.67,
   `dx` 0.60, `cli` 0.51 percent); the dark theme draws a 1 px panel border the light theme does
   not, so the block's box differs by a pixel between themes while the conversion measures in
   light. Nothing in the renderer can close this without moving GT pixels; SPEC-2 1.3 says the
   editor measures in the current theme, so an editor conversion in dark and a CLI conversion in
   light would differ by that pixel on these slides. Recorded for the integrator (request R3).
3. **Prompts in the fidelity comparison.** The conversion measures with the prompts drawn (the
   editor's view), so both comparison renders draw them; comparing a prompted render with an
   unprompted one put the blank Title slide at 0.499 percent for the wrong reason.
4. **`.free > * { margin: 0 !important }` and the plate scoping.** A paragraph's inline
   `margin-top` pushed a converted paragraph down inside its wrapper; the rule takes it out of the
   positioned box. The plate's `max-width: 56ch` and friends are repeated under
   `.free[data-group="plate"]` only, after an unscoped repetition wrapped every free paragraph.
5. **Crossing shapes show on the page.** The geometry pass reports a shape whose box crosses an
   edge as `crossing` and in bounds (SPEC-2 0.96); wholly outside stays out of bounds. Without
   this the fixture's opener picture, moved 40 px right by design, failed every native check.
6. **Chart boxes are picture regions.** Reported, never gated, and out of the per block ink
   comparison; a viewer lays a chart out itself.
7. **The picture object as `slide.background` only when it covers the sheet.** SPEC-2 1.5 states
   the condition; the fixture's `canvas-opener` picture is moved 40 px right and trimmed (11.2),
   so its box no longer covers the sheet and it travels as a `p:pic` at its box. The background
   form is exercised by `background-picture`. The MILESTONES-2 B2 acceptance sentence names
   `canvas-opener`; see request R4.
8. **A curve travels as the polyline through its points** (SPEC-2 2.4.3), named in the residual;
   the elbow and curved connectors are `bentConnector3` and `curvedConnector3`.
9. **pptxgenjs 4.0.1 seams found and worked around**: the numbering scheme is declared as
   `bullet.numberType` and read as `bullet.style` (both are set); a `margin` array is read as
   left, right, bottom, top (`marginPt` orders it so); `descr` is written for pictures and charts
   only (`writeAltText` in the post-process writes a shape's and a text box's alt); `TableProps`
   has no `rotate` or `altText` (a rotated table is written unrotated and named in the residual);
   chart colours must be hex (the computed `rgb()` the page measures is converted in
   `enrich.ts`; a pie's slices take one colour per category through `sliceColorsHex`).
10. **`BuildResult.counts` is optional** because B6's `batch/merge.test.ts` builds a
    `BuildResult` literal without it.
11. **No nested row group in the fixture.** No fixture block carries both a `pos.group` and row
    groups (a `plain` with `numbered: true` inside a group would); the nesting is proven in
    `ooxml.test.ts` on a synthetic part whose members include a `p:graphicFrame` and a `p:cxnSp`
    (SPEC-2 0.67). See request R5.
12. **One italic run.** The fixture's `styles` paragraph holds one italic run, so the native check
    reads "1 italic run(s)" where the MILESTONES-2 acceptance says "17 italic runs or more"; the
    count is what the deck holds.

### Decisions of the fix round

- **F1, proportional numerals in the table block.** `.ts-sheet .table` no longer sets
  `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (SPEC 7.3 named tabular numerals). A PPTX run carries no
  OpenType feature switch, so an `a:tbl` renders proportional digits in every viewer, and a
  tabular "1" in a right aligned cell measured 4 to 6 px outside the 3 px cell budget, which sent
  both tables to the ruled rows fallback. The alternative the finding offered, a wider cell
  budget, would hide a real glyph difference in the gated file. Google Slides tables set
  proportional figures. The `.rows` idiom keeps its tabular numerals. Recorded as a deviation
  from SPEC 7.3's wording.
- **F2, the cell constant.** With `lineSpacing` on the cells every cell read dy -1 or -2 against
  the text box model; `CELL_FIRST_BASELINE_PX` keeps one pixel of the shift back in the cell's
  top margin and the 32 cells read 0 or -1. A measured constant like the model's anchors, not a
  budget change.
- **F3, a curve as cubics.** SPEC-2 2.4.3 accepted the polyline through the points as the
  residual. The file now holds the sheet's Catmull-Rom cubics (the same construction the renderer
  draws), the residual line says so, and `lines#closed` measures 0, 0, 0. The fixture test's
  residual assertion and docs/pptx.md follow.
- **F4, the decoration model.** The sheet draws a decoration at the medium DrawingML size,
  three line widths over the 0.7 mm floor LibreOffice applies (`max(nLineWidth, 70)` in 1/100 mm
  in its oox import), and centres the circle, square and diamond kinds on the line end the way
  the viewer draws `oval` and `diamond`. PowerPoint's own floor is unmeasured (the manual pass of
  docs/export-verification.md). The round one `arrow` kind and the dia grammar's arrowheads are
  untouched.
- **F5, the grid form cell fills its track.** `align-self: stretch` with `align-content` for the
  vertical alignment, so a cell's rule sits on the row's bottom edge and its fill covers the row,
  as in Google's table and the `a:tbl` the export writes; before, a 120 px row would have drawn
  its rule under its text with a gap to the next row. Fixture only (no GT slide has a table).
- **F6, the deltas that stay.** `shapes#plus` is B1's interpreter; `styles#p1` is a viewer
  constant the renderer could only meet with a viewer specific superscript size (0.6 em would
  leave about 5 px); `word-art` in dark is LibreOffice's run outline. Each is named with its
  budget above and in docs/export-verification.md.
