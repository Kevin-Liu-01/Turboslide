# Interface craft: consistency, states, panels, appearances

Audit of every chrome surface of https://turboslide.vercel.app (production serving `6e15d60`, the return round's ship of 2026-09-19 18:06 PDT), driven on 2026-09-19 between 18:18 and 18:50 PDT with headless Chromium (playwright-core 1.62.1 from the repository root, the `chromium_headless_shell-1234` build) at 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800, in the light and the dark appearance, at human speed (the mouse moves in steps, 40 to 90 ms per key, the browser's own double clicks, real drags held through a pointer move stream). The appearance of a context was set the way the product stores it: `gt-theme` for the boot script and `ts-chrome-appearance` for the editor chrome, both in localStorage before the first paint; View > Appearance was driven as well to prove the switch. Every number below was read from the DOM and the computed styles of the live page (a text colour is composited over the ground under it before its contrast is computed) or from the pictures; nothing was inferred from the source alone, and the file and rule named beside a number say where it comes from.

The surfaces: `/home`, `/decks`, `/decks/trash`, `/new` (the skeleton and the first paint), the editor (the title row, the menu bar and its nine menus, the toolbar with the default tail and the text, shape, line, picture, table and chart tails, the filmstrip and its cards, the sheet with the selection ring, the handles, the chip and the readout, the notes pane, the bottom bar, the Format options panel for every kind and with nothing selected, the Themes, Comments and Version history panels, the Layout picker, the Share, Download, Details, Slide numbers, Keyboard shortcuts, Help and Open dialogs, the right click menus on the sheet, on an object and on a filmstrip card, the ? key), the in place show, the presenter view at `/present/<id>`, the viewer at `/deck/<id>`, the Not found page and the You need access page.

Six scratch decks were created from `/new` and each was trashed and deleted forever, and each answers 404 on `/edit/<id>` and `/deck/<id>`: `untitled-20260920-c14z` (the four editor passes), `untitled-20260920-wf5l` (the dialogs pass), `untitled-20260920-rvhu` (the before and after pictures), `untitled-20260920-rc9p` (the show check), and `untitled-20260920-jm4d` and `untitled-20260920-7x3g`, the decks of two runs stopped for a reader fix. Those two could not be trashed from a new browser context, because the deck's owner is the anonymous principal of the context that created it and a fresh context sees a view role only ("You can view this presentation. Request edit access to change it"); they were trashed and removed through the HTTP transport (`POST /api/actions/deck.trash` and `deck.remove` with the agent bearer of `~/.config/turboslide/hosts.json`, read and sent, never printed). The four editor passes therefore carry one browser storage state (cookies and localStorage) from the context that created the deck into the later contexts. The scripts are under `audit-interface/scripts/` (they import nothing from the repository but playwright-core), the tables of every run under `audit-interface/tables/`, the 347 pictures under `audit-interface/` named `<size>-<appearance>-<surface>.png` and `before-` and `after-<appearance>-<item>.png`.

This document touched no source file and ran no git write command. Copy rules of the brief: plain technical English, sentence case, no em dashes, no metaphors, full sentences, no trailing periods on headings. It reports what happened.

## Summary

### What a seller feels today

The chrome is one grammar and it mostly holds: Inter at 13 px, paper and ink, one hairline per boundary, 32 px controls in 44, 28 and 40 px rows, square boxes, a 350 ms tooltip with the key on every control, dialogs that close on Escape and on a click outside, panels at 320 px with 36 px section heads, menus at 28 px rows. The editor reads as one product in both appearances at both sizes, and the rows, the gaps and the type sizes measured on the light page are the dark page's to the pixel.

What a seller runs into is not the grammar but its edges. The toolbar cuts its own tail: with a shape or a table selected at 1440 the last three controls (Format options, the pointer, Hide the menus) are outside the window, at 1280 the text tail loses two and the table tail seven, and no More button appears because the fold engages only at or below 1100 px. The home page shows forty eight black plates with titles where thumbnails should be, so a seller with forty decks reads a list. Secondary text is titanium at 3.25:1 on light chrome (the save words, the menu keys, the slide numbers in the filmstrip, every field label), a field's boundary is the same hairline as a divider at 1.5:1, and a pointer resting on a toolbar button changes almost nothing (a 1.5:1 outline, a 1.08:1 plate on a menu title), so the states a seller reads a toolbar by are the weakest part of the picture. Tooltips fire on keyboard focus inside menus and dialogs and draw over them, so the first row of every right click menu carries a plate over the second and the Share dialog opens with a plate over its owner row. The text tail leads with a dead "Font" word. Bold does not light up after Cmd+B. Nothing here is broken in the sense of the return audits; every control works. It is the craft between the controls that a seller from Google Slides or Pitch notices in the first minute.

### The ten items this round should do first

1. Fold the toolbar tail into the More menu whenever it does not fit, at any width (item 1 of the table).
2. Render slide 1 on every card of `/decks` and the trash (item 2).
3. Raise the light `--pt-titanium` to 4.5:1 or move the small texts to ink-2 (item 3).
4. Stop tooltips on focus inside menus and dialogs (item 4).
5. One field boundary token at 3:1 for every input and select (item 7).
6. A hover ground under the hairline on shell buttons and a readable plate tint on menus (item 8).
7. The frame weight on every floating plate: menus, tooltips, the layout and colour plates (item 9).
8. The Font control as a read only value until the catalog ships (item 10).
9. The pressed state of Bold, Italic and Underline follows the selection (item 14).
10. Menu plates sized to their longest label, and mnemonics off on macOS (items 11 and 18).

Two larger items should start as design work this round and ship in the next: the brand kit (item 5) and an assistant entry over the actions table (item 6), because both change the title row's right cluster and the panel roster and should be drawn together.

### What to leave

The You need access form (item 33) is a privacy choice the specification made on purpose; only its wording and order should change. The bottom bar (item 35) and the frame marks in the show (item 32) need the title row's cluster and the theme owner's call. The empty state assists (item 36) wait for the assistant entry. ## 1. The grammar as measured

### 1.1 Rows, columns and the stage

At 1440 by 900 the editor's rows are the title row at 44 px, the menu bar at 28, the toolbar at 40, the body at 756 and the bottom bar at 32 (`--pt-title-h`, `--pt-menu-h`, `--pt-tool-h`, `--pt-status-h`; EditorShell.css). The filmstrip column is 256 px (the thumbnail density), the stage 1184 by 692, the notes pane 64 px under it, the right panel 320 px when open (`--pt-panel-w`), which takes the stage to 864 px. At 1280 by 800 the rows are the same and the stage is 1024 by 592. The sheet stands on the plate ground (`--pt-plate`, #f6f6f6 on white, 1.08:1) at 1128 by 635 with 28 px at the sides and 29 px above and below, a 1 px hairline and no shadow (`.pt-stagewrap`, ViewerShell.css). The same rows measured in the dark appearance are identical.

### 1.2 Control heights

32 px is the control height of the title row (the mark's link box, the deck name, the save words, the comments toggle, the Slideshow split button, Share), of the toolbar (every button, the 42 px wide dropdown buttons, the 92 px Background, 75 px Layout, 61 px Theme and 110 px Format options text buttons), of the dialog X and actions, of the panel X, of the Share dialog's fields and Send, of the `/decks` sort select and the trash head's buttons. Inside a 32 px box a field is 30 px (the zoom field 52 by 30, the Font size field 36 by 30, the Format options inputs 30). 28 px is the menu row, the bottom bar's one button, the trash cards' Restore and Delete forever, the version name field and the Share dialog's role and mode selects in their sheet (share.css). 36 px is a panel section head and a dialog list row. 40 px is the panel head, the `/decks` search field and the `/home` hero buttons; 44 px the title row and the product page's navigation. 18 px is the height of every chip (the selection chip, the readout, the key chips in tooltips and the search pill); 16 px the filmstrip's comment and collaborator chips; 24 px the presence chips; 20 px the toolbar separators and the filmstrip's section labels; 13 px the notes handle. The heights sit on a 4 px grid (28, 32, 36, 40, 44) with three exceptions: the 30 px fields inside 32 px boxes, the 26 px card title on `/decks` and the 13 px notes handle.

### 1.3 Radii

Square is the default (`border-radius: 0` in every sheet). Three exceptions live in chrome: 6 px, "the one corner in chrome" (`--pt-radius`), on the toolbar's search pill, the segmented controls, the key chips in tooltips and the `/decks` search field and sort select, and on one field, the version name field (VersionsPanel.css); 8 px on the title row's Slideshow split button and Share (TitleRow.css), on the solid Present of the viewer and the Request access button of the access page (ToolButton.css `.pt-ib.is-solid`); 50 percent on the rotation handle and the dialog radio dot. The solid New Presentation buttons of `/home` and Not found are square. The viewer's segmented control shows 5 px inner corners (6 less the 1 px border). So chrome draws 0, 5, 6 and 8 px corners, and the solid button is square on two pages and round on three.

### 1.4 Icons and glyphs

Every icon is a filled path on a 20 unit viewBox rendered at 16 px (`icons.tsx`, 61 glyphs; `fill: currentColor`, no strokes), so there is no stroke weight to vary: the weight is the path's. Chevrons are 12 px (the split arrows, the zoom arrow, the dropdown buttons' `has-chevron`), the submenu chevron 14 px, the filmstrip skip glyph 14 px. Three glyphs are type, not paths: the Text box "T" (15 px Inter 500), the theme half disc (a 16 px text character) and the more button on a `/decks` card ("⋮" at 14 px 500). The title row mark is 24 px (`--ts-mark`), the lockup mark 16 px. The toolbar's icon set reads as one weight; the print glyph and the paint format brush are the heaviest fills, the undo and redo arrows the lightest.

### 1.5 Type sizes and weights

Two weights, 400 and 500 (with one 700 on the Share dialog's group labels Links and General access, share.css), and twenty sizes: 11 px (the picker titles in uppercase, the presenter's Timer and Time labels at 500), 11.5 (the version meta, the layout rule, an inspector error), 12 (menu keys, field labels, the filmstrip numbers at 500, the sort and filter selects, the hex field, the card date), 12.5 (the save words, the inspector labels, the tooltip, the recent lead, the hero facts), 13 (the body size of menus, toolbar text buttons, dialogs, notes, cards, the chip), 13.5 (card titles, template labels, the help table, the sidebar's product name), 14 (the deck name, the panel title, the hero buttons), 14.5 (the viewer's deck name and brand), 15 (the `/home` body and the "T" glyph), 16 (the `/decks` band headings), 17 (the trash confirm's title), 18 (the dialog title, the help card heading), 20 (the empty state title, the access title, the h3 ladder, the presenter's elapsed time), 22 (the trash head, the `/decks` lockup), 32 (the Not found title, the h2 ladder), 40 (the number strip), 44 (the sheet's title in the presenter's clone) and 66 (the `/home` h1). The half pixel sizes (12.5, 13.5, 14.5) sit beside their whole neighbours on the same rows: the save words at 12.5 next to the deck name at 14 and the menu bar at 13; the card title at 13.5 over the card date at 12.

### 1.7 Spacing rhythm

The toolbar is a 4 px gap row (measured gaps 4 px between every pair of controls, 17 px across a separator: the 1 px rule with 4 px margins in the 4 px gaps, so 8 px of air on each side, EditorToolbar.css `.ts-tb-sep`), with two exceptions: the New slide arrow overlaps its plus by 4 px (`.ts-tb-split-arrow` margin-left -4) and the zoom field sits 5 px from its arrow. The title row is an 8 px gap row with a 10 px inset at the left and 12 px at the right (TitleRow.css). The menu bar's titles touch (0 px gaps, 12 px of padding each). Menus are 220 px plates with 12 px side padding, 4 px above and below, 28 px rows and 4 px around a divider. Dialogs have 24 px side padding, a 20 px top, a 14 px gap between body blocks and a 12 by 16 px actions row. Panels have 16 px insets, 40 px heads, 36 px section heads and 8 px between fields (a 4 px gap between a label and its field). The filmstrip has 14 px above, 12 px right, 8 px left, 12 px between cards and a 28 px number gutter. `/decks` uses 24 px bands and 20 px card gaps; `/home` 72 px bands on a 1120 px rail. So the chrome is a 4 px grid with an 8 px rhythm between groups and 12, 16 and 24 px insets by surface, and the numbers hold in the dark appearance and at 1280.

## 3. Tooltips

Every control walked carries `data-tip` and shows one plate: the name in 12.5 px Inter 500, the key as an 18 px chip on `--pt-hair-soft` with a 6 px corner, one sentence in ink-2 at 12.5 px, 6 to 7 px under the control, at most 280 px wide, a `--pt-hair` frame (1.51:1) and no shadow. The delay measured 355 to 373 ms (350 in Tooltip.tsx) on the `/home` navigation, on Share, on the Slideshow halves, on the deck name ("Rename"), on the save words, on New slide ("New slide, Ctrl M, The arrow picks a layout for the new slide."), on Undo, Select, Insert image, Paint format, on Font ("The GT theme sets Inter."), on the bottom bar's one button (it flips above the control there, 104 px up), on the dialog X ("Close") and on the notes handle ("Speaker notes"). The plate is worded as a name and one sentence throughout; no tooltip repeats its label without adding the key or a sentence, and none names an internal thing.

The one defect is when a tooltip fires: on keyboard focus with the same delay as hover, inside menus and dialogs. The first row of the `/decks` card menu, of the object menu ("Cut, Cmd X" over Copy) and of the slide menu takes focus when the menu opens and its plate covers the row under it. Every dialog opens with the plate of its autofocused control over the card (dialogs-run.json, `tipOnOpen`): Share's email field ("Add people by email. Addresses separated by commas; Enter sends" over the Owner row), Download's Include skipped slides, Slide numbers' On, Keyboard shortcuts' search field, Help's Documentation and Open's Presentations tab; Details's Done plate sits under the card. The trash confirm autofocuses Delete forever and its plate ("Delete forever, Enter, Deletes the files; nothing brings them back.") crosses the dialog's bottom edge. Hovering a layout tile shows the layout's sentence over the neighbouring tiles' names.
