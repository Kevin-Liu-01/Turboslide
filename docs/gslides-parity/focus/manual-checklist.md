# The manual checklist of the focus round

The orchestrator's ruling (3) on docs/FOCUS.md section 9: a matrix row whose only obstacle is the headless browser is marked "manual" with a checklist step for Kevin here, and does not park its feature. Every other not driven core row is driven by the ship or its feature leaves the default view. This file lists the manual steps, one per row, with what to do and what to see. A step here is never counted as passed by a run; the ship note records who walked it and when.

Kept by the drivers lane (b4). Rows join this list only when the driver cannot reach the step: the operating system's print dialog, a second physical screen, a native chooser Playwright's `setInputFiles` or `setFiles` cannot reach. Everything a spec or the probe can drive stays a driven row.

## Rows that are not manual

- The product round (docs/PRODUCT.md 8.2): the two measurement rows `export.download.large-deck-pdf` and `export.download.large-deck-pptx` are driven by core/export.spec.ts and carry `measure: true`; a red one is recorded in the ship note with its mechanism and never holds the ship, and it is never a manual step. `assist.quota.429` is driven only on a preview with Upstash; on any other base the spec records it not driven with the reason and the unit test of PRODUCT.md 8.3 covers the rows. Neither is a manual row.

- The sync and costs round (docs/SYNC.md 6.1): the ten `sync.*` rows of `core/sync.spec.ts` are driven by two browsers with one person's cookies and a third as a stranger, each two person exchange three times inside the test, and never by hand. The six rows of the cost probe (`scripts/probes/sync-cost-probe.mjs`, the driver `cost-probe`) record their counts beside their ceilings; `cost.editor-hidden.calls` is recorded not driven with the reason when the headless browser's `document.visibilityState` never reads `hidden` after a second page is brought to the front, and it is not a manual row for that: the verifier reads the hidden tab's requests on production by hand only as an aside to the run, and the row stays the probe's.
- `present.presenter-view.arrow` and `present.presenter-view.s-key`: Presenter view is a second window, not a second screen; Playwright drives the popup, so core/present.spec.ts drives both rows.
- `images.insert.upload`, `images.insert.first-on-new-deck`, `images.insert.upload-while-pending`, `images.replace.upload`, `images.background.upload-picture`: the native chooser is reached through Playwright's `filechooser` event and `setFiles`, so core/images.spec.ts drives them.
- `images.insert.drop`, `images.replace.drop-on-picture`: a `DataTransfer` built in the page carries the file into the drop, Playwright's documented file drop pattern (the images audit's method), so core/images.spec.ts drives them.
- `images.insert.paste`: a paste event carrying the PNG as a clipboard file, dispatched on the stage, so core/images.spec.ts drives it.
- `title.slideshow.presentOnAnotherScreen`: a Later stub, parked with the switch (FOCUS.md 3.1); no row.

## How to record a walk

Fill the Seen column with the date, the origin and the commit (for example `2026-09-16 production 1a2b3c4`) after the step is walked. The ship note lists this file's rows as "manual" beside the run ledger; a manual row never reads passed in a run's table.
