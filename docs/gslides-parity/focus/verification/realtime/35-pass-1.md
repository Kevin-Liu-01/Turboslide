## V.1 Verdict

The round is not ready to ship. Its people surfaces do what the specifications say on the do tier by hand, at 1440 and 1280 in both appearances, with two browsers on two node processes and one object between them: the join chip in 42 to 77 ms with the object's colo named, the keystroke in 212 to 227 ms, the caret in 85 to 111 ms and after a merge at the other person's word end in all 12 hand rounds, the remote outline 2 px inside the own ring, the drag ghost with "moving", Follow by link with its six ends, the live pointer from the View rows in the default view, the card chip in the pixels, the agent banner through the other process's actions route in 41 ms, a reload and 35 s offline losing nothing. The realtime gate read 16 of 16 green with retries zero. Against that, six findings hold the ship (V.5 has them in full):

- Severity 3, R1: after a hibernation wake the object does not know its deck id, so every checkpoint posts to `/api/decks//checkpoint` and fails until an HTTP request names the deck, and the ticket refresh after a wake is refused (`4401 ticket refused: deck`) and the tab reconnects. Read on the wake probe (four alarms, zero checkpoints in 30 s, the record landing only after a bearer read), on the ten minute ticket drive (twice) and in the Worker's log (`the close checkpoint failed` with deck null). No test or earlier run waited long enough for the local runtime to hibernate.
- Severity 3, R1 with the integrator: on the do tier a picture cannot be inserted or replaced by upload, drop, paste or URL: eleven `images.*` rows red there and green on the memory tier, "Your new object was not added. Try again", because `asset.add` moves the store's revision outside the object.
- Severity 3, R1 with R2: `realtime.title.two-typers` is flaky on both tiers: red in 4 of 12 do readings (the integrator's three and one of this pass's nine, ` ub2` lost) and in 1 of 6 memory readings (check step 32, ` ub2` lost again). The client id tie rule exists in the server and no client declares it.
- Severity 3, R2 with R3: undo and redo after toolbar changes, groups, duplicates and handle gestures do nothing on the round's tree on both tiers (nineteen walk rows), while production, which serves main, reads all of them green; the regression is the round's.
- Severity 3, R1 with R4: `people.versions-author-account`, `people.labels-disambiguated` and `share.dialog.grant-email-line` red in both accounts harness modes, as the integrator read them.
- Severity 3 by rule (a cost row over its ceiling), R2 with R5: `cost.editor-idle.calls` on do reads 3 function requests a minute against 1, the studio session's poll.

Severity 2 findings beside them: a tab on the do tier whose object cannot reach the app (the protected do preview today) retries `4500 seed failed` forever and never falls to the blob tier; and `cost.do.requests` passes on 0 request units for 36 edits because the object's counters are not durable across a wake. A third and a fourth severity 2: a viewer whose link is revoked keeps its socket and the deck when the instance that answers the reauth holds the old record in its cache (finding 9), and an upgrade without a ticket reaches the object, which calls the app's seed route before any join is verified (finding 12).
