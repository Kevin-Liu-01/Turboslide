# Judge operations: money, the cutover and the secrets

The operations judgment of the sync round, written 2026-09-20 against `docs/SYNC.md` (375 lines, 13:03 PDT), `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` (212 lines, 13:03 PDT) and the three audits under `docs/gslides-parity/sync/` (`audit-costs.md`, `audit-hosting.md`, `audit-ordering.md`) with their evidence folders. The lens is operations and money: whether every dollar figure comes from a priced page with a date and a counted call, whether the Hobby limits are stated as the pages state them, whether the zero cost redirect deployment is zero, whether the cutover and the rollback leave no window in which a seller's write is lost, whether the migration verifies by count and hash, and whether the documents keep every secret out. Nothing was created, changed or deleted on Vercel or GitHub; no source file was touched; no git write command ran. Section 6 lists what was read and run.

## 1. Verdict

Revise. The shape of the plan is right (a team project deploying from the connected repository through a Git Sources override, the store transferred rather than copied, a WAF 307 as the instant switch, a static 308 deployment with no function and no store afterwards, the secrets kept a week, `shadow` before `enforce`), and most of the prices and the Hobby limits match the pages they cite. Nine claims do not hold against the code, the metrics or the pages, and four of them sit on the write path of the cutover.

## 2. Rejections

Each names the section, the problem and the file that proves it.

1. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` section 5 step 5 and section 3 say open tabs follow the WAF redirect to the team origin and the room client keeps a pending edit through the switch; the redirect is cross origin, `apps/studio/src` sets no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header (grep on 2026-09-20 returned nothing), the client posts with the default redirect mode (`apps/cli/src/remote.ts` and `packages/realtime/client/room-client.ts` pass no `redirect` option), so the browser refuses the redirected POST and stream under CORS, the client marks itself offline and backs off (`room-client.ts` 1190 to 1198), and the pending queue lives in the old origin's IndexedDB (`packages/realtime/client/pending-store.ts` 2), so every word typed in an open tab after the flip is lost when the seller reloads onto the new host.
2. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` section 4 (the `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` row, "set by connecting the store to the project") and section 5 step 4 ("this sets `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`") rest on an assumption the Blob docs contradict: `/docs/vercel-blob` (updated 2026-08-26, read 2026-09-20) says connecting a store adds `BLOB_STORE_ID`, `VERCEL_OIDC_TOKEN` and `BLOB_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY`, and the read-write token is added when a store is created; `packages/store/src/select.ts` 6 selects `tmp` on Vercel when that variable is unset and `packages/store/src/blob-vercel.ts` 132 passes the explicit token so the SDK's OIDC path is never reached, which means a team deployment without the variable would serve the seeded decks from a per instance overlay and lose every edit without an error.
3. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` section 7 (the second acceptance bullet) and `audit-hosting.md` section 2 (e) say an agent's old `hosts.json` entry keeps working through the 308 until it is updated; `apps/cli/src/remote.ts` 110 to 125 sends `Authorization: Bearer` with the default redirect mode, and Node's fetch removes the `Authorization` header on a cross origin redirect (the Fetch specification's HTTP redirect fetch, implemented by undici), so every agent call through the redirect reaches the team host without its bearer and answers 401; `curl -sI -X POST` without a header proves the 308 and nothing about the bearer.
4. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` section 6 (the rollback) promotes the previous production deployment first and transfers the store back second, and names "redeploy `main` on the personal project" as a ten minute step; between the two the promoted deployment writes 5xx against a store the team now owns and the team deployment has no store, and the redeploy cannot build the studio after section 7 emptied Root Directory, set Framework Preset Other, turned the Build Command override on and empty and disconnected Git, none of which section 6 restores; the "dry run on the rehearsal store" covers the store alone.
5. `audit-costs.md` section 3, `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` 1.2 and `docs/SYNC.md` section 1 say the seven days cost "about $6.0 in memory" and "about $9"; `audit-costs/metrics/gbhr-7d-by-path.json` sums to 466.6 GB-h (the figure the same section quotes as "413 of 467 GB-h"), which at $0.0106 per GB-h is $4.95, so the seven days cost about $8.2 and the memory line is off by a fifth.
6. `audit-costs.md` section 6 and `audit-costs/costs.mjs` line 27 (`INCLUDED.pro`) count 1 million invocations, 100,000 simple operations, 10,000 advanced operations, 5 GB of storage and 100 GB of transfer as included on Pro; the pages they cite say Pro includes nothing beyond the $20 credit (`/docs/functions/usage-and-pricing` 2026-06-16: "Vercel bills Pro invocations on demand", the 1.5 million example billed whole; `/docs/vercel-blob/usage-and-pricing` 2026-09-08: "Included (Pro): Usage-based"), and the Blob figures come from that page's pricing example, which also says "10M included" edge requests where the included tier is 1 million; the model must bill Pro from the first unit and apply the credit once (the 50 hour invocation line is $1.80, not $1.20, and the Blob line about $0.50 higher).
7. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` 2.2 and section 5 step 4 put the freeze between the transfer and the team redeploy at "three to five minutes" without a measurement; the team has one concurrent build shared with `dashboard`, `landing` and `admin` under `WAIT_FOR_NAMESPACE_QUEUE` (`audit-hosting/vercel-facts.md` section 3) and a production build traces Chromium into eight function directories of 87.36 MB (section 2), so the gap is the build queue plus the build, and the plan neither records the deploy time from step 2 nor reserves the slot for the window.
8. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` 2.2 and section 10 item 5 accept the transfer on "`/decks` lists the 72 decks and a twin loads from the unchanged host"; no count and no hash proves the store arrived whole, although `audit-hosting/scripts/blob-sizing.mjs` already produced the baseline (17,201 objects, 1,359,673,370 bytes, per prefix, at 2026-09-20T19:07Z in `blob-sizing.json`).
9. `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md` section 3 accepts the copy fallback when "`--verify` reads equal counts and etags for `decks/`" while `--skip exports-older-than=1d,presence` leaves out the 4,219 presence, pulse and copies sidecars that live under `decks/<id>/.turboslide/`; as written the verify cannot pass.

## 4. What held

- The Hobby included amounts (1 million invocations, 4 CPU hours, 360 GB-hours, 1 million edge requests, 100 GB fast data transfer, 10 GB fast origin transfer, 10,000 simple and 2,000 advanced Blob operations, 1 GB storage, 10 GB Blob transfer), the 30 day wait and the non commercial rule match `/docs/plans/hobby` (2026-09-14), `/docs/functions/usage-and-pricing` (2026-06-16) and `/docs/vercel-blob/usage-and-pricing` (2026-09-08), read 2026-09-20. `costs.mjs` line 26 carries the same numbers.
- The Pro prices match their pages: $20 a month with one seat and $20 of credit; iad1 $0.128 per active CPU hour and $0.0106 per GB-hour; $0.60 per million invocations; Blob $0.40 per million simple, $5.00 per million advanced, $0.023 per GB-month, $0.05 per GB in iad1, `del()` free; rate limits 7,200 and 4,500 a minute on Pro, 1,200 and 900 on Hobby; 40 WAF rules on Pro; the Flat Rate CDN included tier of 1 M requests and 1 TB.
- The redirect facts match `/docs/routing/redirects` (2026-08-11): 308 for `permanent: true`, 307 for `false`, method and body unchanged, query passed through, processed at the edge, and a WAF redirect executing before configuration redirects without a redeploy. The `has: host` clause the page recommends is absent from the plan's `vercel.json`, which is acceptable here because every hostname of the personal project should redirect and the destination is another project.
- The 50 hour base reproduces from `costs-model.md` apart from the included amounts of rejection 6: 6,911 GB-h at $0.0106 is $73.26 and 68.9 CPU hours at $0.128 is $8.82.
- The client's handling of a 429 and a 5xx keeps the ops pending and retries (`room-client.ts` 1241 to 1257), and a 403 other than `client_unbound` returns them as refused (1268 onward), so the plan's choice of a redirect over a WAF deny as the freeze is right.
- The transfer page (`/docs/storage`, 2026-09-03) describes Transfer Store as the Hobby to Pro upgrade path and says nothing about connected projects, tokens, two Pro teams or the store id; the plan's scratch store rehearsal is the right answer to that silence, and rejection 2 adds the third thing it must read.
- Secrets: `docs/SYNC.md`, `docs/HOSTING-MOVE.md`, the three audits and `vercel-facts.md` name environment variables and never value them; the only long hex strings in the evidence are the 64 character `request_path` identifiers Vercel's metrics assign to server functions (`metrics/memory-7d-by-path.json`), not secrets; `.turboslide` and `.vercel` are in `.gitignore`; the three documents and the sync folder are untracked, so nothing has been committed yet.
