## Summary

## 1. What exists today

### 1.1 The material block, the catalog and the actions

The block (`packages/schema/src/blocks/material.ts`): `type: 'material'`, `materialId`, `preset?`, `uniforms?` (a record of `u_*` names to numbers, number lists or strings), `anchor?` (ms), `twoTone?`, `plate?` (`lower-left`, `lower-right`, `upper-left`), `asset?` (the frozen frame), `height?`, `caption?`, `captionSize?`, `alt`, plus `pos` and `link` as on every block. `MATERIAL_ANCHORS` is `[4000, 5500, 7000]`, the times the brand deck sampled its openers at. A picture whose asset has `source.kind: 'material'` carries the same recipe (`packages/schema/src/assets.ts` 155 to 165: `materialId`, `uniforms`, `size [3200, 1800]`, `timeMs`, `backend` `angle-metal` or `swiftshader`, `renderer`, `recipeKey`).

The catalog (`packages/materials/src/catalog.ts`): 17 entries bound to `@paper-design/shaders` 0.0.78 fragment shaders, each with a sizing family (object or pattern), the textures it samples, its palette roles (`back`, `front`, `colors`, `inner`) and a typed spec per uniform (float with min, max and step; int; color; colors with a maximum count; enum with named values). `material.list` on production answered 22 entries: liquid metal (19 uniforms, 7 presets), gem smoke (20, 5), smoke ring, god rays, mesh gradient, simplex noise, swirl, spiral, grain gradient, dithering, static radial gradient, neuro noise, metaballs, waves, dot grid, perlin noise and static mesh gradient (12 to 21 uniforms, 3 presets each), and the five `proto:*` engines with `available: false`, no uniforms and the sentence "Not ported: SPEC open question 9 ... is Kevin's decision". The presets (`presets.ts`) are three per entry computed from `GT_PALETTE` (`ink-paper`, `paper-ink`, `brand-blue`) plus the brand deck's recorded recipes for liquid metal (`diamond`, `sphere`, and Paper's `chrome` and `noir`) and gem smoke (`brand-blue`, `fire`, Paper's `default`). `insertableMaterials()` inserts with the first preset, which is `ink-paper` for every entry but gem smoke (`packages/chrome/src/dialogs/InsertMaterial.tsx` 21 to 33; the walk's block read `preset: "ink-paper"`), so the liquid metal a seller gets is not the deck's diamond.

The actions (`packages/schema/src/actions.ts` 2277 to 2333, 5262 to 5300; `packages/materials/src/actions.ts`): `material.list`, `material.capture { materialId, preset, uniforms, size, anchors, id, role, alt, twoTone, treatment, plate, backend }` (CLI `turboslide material capture`, MCP `deck_material_capture`), `slide.setBackgroundMaterial { slideIds, materialId, preset, uniforms, anchor, dither }` (server side: capture then a covering picture at the back), and the block writes `block.insert` and `block.set`. Every one is registered on the hosted dispatcher (`apps/studio/src/server/agent-actions.ts` 49, 50, 121, 184 to 187).

## 2. Glyphfield's shader library, read from the repository

### 2.1 The catalog

`glyphfield/src/lib/liveMaterials.ts`: 29 Paper Shaders families with 120 upstream presets (`PAPER_SHADER_FAMILIES`; one library entry per preset, `paper-<family>[-<preset>]`), 4 house renderers (`glyphfield-glyph-field`, a canvas 2D field of glyphs; `glyphfield-mesh-gradient`, `glyphfield-grain-gradient` and `glyphfield-dither-gradient`, three GLSL fragments), 9 Shaders.com studies written as original GLSL (`shaders-pixel-beams`, `soft-register`, `spectral-bloom`, `pistons`, `fluid-chrome`, `chroma-flow`, `drift`, `mosaic`, `circuit`), 6 design studies (`study-line-field`, `chrome-glares`, `relief-gradient`, `orbit-gradient`, `radiant-void`, `galactic-rings`), the ShaderGradient sphere (`@shadergradient/react`, three.js), Pavel Dobryakov's fluid (`pavel-fluid-energy`) and Holo Cloth: 142 entries by count, sorted by a quality score (100.5 for Holo Cloth down to 55 for Halftone CMYK) with a featured list of 16 first (`shaderLab.ts` `SHADER_LAB_FEATURED_IDS`), five categories computed from the name (Fluid, Light, Metal, Gradient, Graphic), search over name, description and engine, and 15 palettes (`LIVE_MATERIAL_PALETTES`, three colours each) plus `brandMaterialPalette(identity)` that takes the active brand's ink, accent and paper as Base, Mid and Light, and 9 look presets (`LIVE_MATERIAL_LOOK_PRESETS`). The library cards (`ShaderLibrarySidebar.tsx`, `AuthenticShaderPreview.tsx`) show a 320 by 200 webp thumbnail per entry (`public/shader-previews/`, 134 files, about 4.5 KB each), the source badge and the name; a card never mounts a live canvas. Older GLSL presets (`shaderPresets.ts`, 18 fragments named Aurora, Liquid metal, Polished chrome, Brushed aluminum, Black nickel, Satin steel, Mercury, Brushed steel, Topographic, Plasma, Radial beams, Halftone, Warped grid, Glass waves, Iridescent, Heatmap, Gem smoke, Grain gradient) share the uniforms `u_resolution`, `u_time`, `u_color_a`, `u_color_b`, `u_scale`, `u_distortion`, `u_softness`, `u_repetition`, `u_contour`.

### 2.2 The controls

One settings record drives every material (`LiveMaterialSettings`; `agentCatalog.ts` `AGENT_SHADER_LIBRARY.controls` publishes the ranges): `colorA`, `colorB`, `colorC` (hex, labelled Base, Mid, Light), `strength` 0 to 2 step 0.01, `detail` 0.5 to 8 step 0.1, `frequency` 0.2 to 10 step 0.1, `amplitude` 0 to 8 step 0.1, `density` 0.1 to 2 step 0.05, `brightness` 0.1 to 2 step 0.05, `grain` 0 to 100 step 1, `rotationX`, `rotationY`, `rotationZ` 0 to 360 step 1, `centerX`, `centerY` 0 to 1 step 0.01, `speed` 0 to 2 step 0.05. The inspector (`LiveMaterialControls.tsx`) groups them as Quick look (the look presets), Material (the select with the source link), Color system (the brand palette first, then the 15 palettes, then three hex fields), Form (Strength, Detail, Frequency, Amplitude, Density), Light and texture (Brightness, Grain), Orientation (Rotation X, Y, Z), Center (X, Y as percent, Paper only) and Motion (Shader speed 0 to 1.5). Every slider is a range input with a value readout. Beside them: Shader zoom, a logarithmic slider 0.1 to 10 with eleven stops (`shaderZoom.ts`); the time explorer (`ShaderTimeExplorer.tsx`: a 30 s window that grows, a readout in seconds, Play, Pause, step by 1/60 s, Capture frame, Resume live); the finish (`materialFinish.ts`: Clean, Soft depth, Mirror, Liquid glass, Gallery glass, with shadow colour, opacity, blur, X and Y, border, reflection and glass refraction fields); and the converter (`compositionEffects.ts`: Bayer with the 8 by 8 matrix, ASCII, Halftone, Posterize, each with cell size, tone count, threshold, contrast, foreground, background and invert). Paper's own presets keep their authored uniforms until a studio control moves (`paperShaderControls.ts` `preservePresetAppearance`); the mapping from the common controls onto each Paper family's parameters is one table (`paperControlOverrides`: strength scales intensity, contrast, bloom and glow; detail scales iteration and count parameters; frequency scales frequency, repetition, gap and stroke; amplitude scales amplitude, radius, size, distortion and swirl; density scales density, proportion, softness and noise; grain sets grainMixer and grainOverlay; rotation and center add to the preset's; the three colours land on `colors`, `colorBack`, `colorFront`, `colorTint` and the rest by role). Glyph field has no glyph set control: the glyphs are the string `GLYPHFIELD`, the columns and rows come from Detail, the kept cells from Density, and the particles from Grain (`LiveMaterialCanvas.tsx` 931 to 1030); Prototemplate's glyph field carries the eight scripts and 31 glyphs (`glyph-field.ts` `SCRIPTS`, `GLYPHS`, `POOL` 1280). Seeds are per material constants (the static mesh gradient's `u_positions` "Positions seed" 0 to 100 is the one seed a Turboslide user can set today).

### 2.3 How it renders

`browserSupportsWebGL2()` probes a `webgl2` context with `failIfMajorPerformanceCaveat` once per 30 s and caches a failure for 2 s (`webglContext.ts`); without it the provider is never constructed and a three colour static fallback shows. Paper families render through `@paper-design/shaders-react` components (two in the startup graph, 27 loaded on demand, `paperShaderRegistry.ts`); the house shaders and studies through one WebGL program per canvas with the shared `FRAGMENT_SHARED` header; the glyph field through canvas 2D with a preallocated pool; the sphere through React Three Fiber; the fluid through a velocity and dye feedback loop. The frame loop (`liveMaterialFrameLoop.ts`, `liveMaterialRenderBudget.ts`) paces to a requested rate with a vsync tolerance (60 when animating, `PAPER_PREVIEW_FRAME_RATE` 30 for previews), sleeps when paused and redraws on invalidate; the pixel ratio is the device ratio times a render scale capped at 2.5 (3 for one path) and by a per instance pixel budget shared fairly across instances. The gallery holds at most four live previews (`shaderPreviewBudget.ts` `MAX_ACTIVE_SHADER_PREVIEWS`), releases a context 250 ms after unmount, pauses hidden documents and off screen canvases, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` holds a representative frame (`usePrefersReducedMotion.ts`; `docs/shader-preview-performance.md`). The measured cost is recorded rather than promised: the landing page's three hero shaders were ready at 1,090 to 1,132 ms cold in Chromium against a 250 ms gate the document keeps red.

### 2.4 How a still and a motion file leave

`docs/shader-frame-contract.md`: a saved shader frame is a lossless PNG of the ready renderer plus its editable recipe plus a native time anchor; a timestamp alone is not an image. Pause freezes the mounted renderer without encoding (`pauseShaderFrames.ts`, `freezeLiveMaterialFrame`); PNG and JPG export read the visible canvas and leave the canvas paused without adding a saved frame; Capture frame is the one action that writes a durable PNG (content addressed in IndexedDB). Stills export at a width between 320 and 3840 px; GIF and MP4 sample the provider's native clock at 12, 15, 24 or 30 fps for 1,200 to 4,000 ms at fast, balanced or best quality, composite the same grain and filter as the preview (`shaderFramePresentation.ts`, a pre rasterised 86 KB grain tile), and refuse Fluid because its state is not reconstructible from time (`docs/shader-capture-export-audit.md`). Motion capabilities are declared per material (`shaderMotionCapabilities.ts`: static, direct time, procedural or stateful; nine Paper families are static; three Dithering presets have analytic loop candidates that are not pixel verified).

## 3. The brand deck's openers and Prototemplate's engines

The GT brand deck has eight openers (`slides/01`, `16`, `31`, `42`, `47`, `56`, `60`, `79`), each a full bleed two tone picture with a paper plate lower left carrying the section title, one sentence and a 15 px titanium credit (`DECK-GRAMMAR.md`; `OPENERS.md` "Opener slide"). Five are cut from Prototemplate direction engines and credited "Material: <Direction>, a Prototemplate direction": Event Horizon (the careers page horizon field, a WebGL2 lens shader with the 22 parameters of `HORIZON_DEFAULTS`: pitch, gauge, rule alpha and fades, three ring radii and alphas, deflection, swirl, light gain, exposure, chroma, doppler and its angle, breathe, period, core and ink colours; three pointer modes lens, dither and redshift), Chroma Flow, Glyph Rain (the glyph field, canvas 2D, eight scripts, 31 glyphs, a pool of 1,280, one frame under reduced motion), Lens Gate and Singularity. Three are Paper materials rendered in Glyphfield at 3200 by 1800 and credited "Material: <material>, Paper Shaders, rendered in Glyphfield": liquid metal in its diamond form (variant lm-gem-c5, the recipe `presets.ts` records as `diamond`), gem smoke in the brand blue and gem smoke in Paper's fire colours. The shader pipeline (`OPENERS.md` 29 to 39) is what `@turboslide/effects` `twoTone` implements: crop a text free box, cover 800 by 450 with Lanczos, grayscale (invert a light render), autocontrast at 0.5 percent, a black point, a gamma curve, the 8 by 8 Bayer screen at `(m + 0.5) / 64`, 2x nearest to 1600 by 900, the dark file and its inverted light twin; the openers were sampled at about 4, 5.5 and 7 s. The deck's rules that bind a shader library: "Cells never shimmer: nothing re-seeds a threshold map, and the identity animates nothing" (`docs/brand.md` section 8), the sheet shows a dither or a shader "only as content the user placed", the Motion rules slide reads "Every loop starts paused and plays only while its section and tab are visible" and "Under prefers-reduced-motion, nothing is animated. The markup is the final frame" (`29-motion.html`), and the dither is "the brand's only texture" (`26-dither.html`). The engines slide records the runtime rule for the docs page: "Every engine mounts on first view, runs on screen, pauses off screen, and renders one frame under reduced motion" and "Studio field: Ten Bayer presets share one GL context" (`72-engines.html`). The studio field (`studio-field.ts`) is the engine to port first: it is "ported from the founder's glyphfield studio", takes the Glyphfield grammar (`colorA`, `colorB`, `colorC`, `strength`, `detail`, `frequency`, `grain`, `amplitude`, `density`, `brightness`, `rotation`), ships ten Bayer presets (`bayer-flow`, `bayer-8x8` the founder's pick, `bayer-contour`, `bayer-radial`, `bayer-sweep`, `bayer-waves`, `bayer-chunk`, `bayer-pulse`, `bayer-ink`, `bayer-hot`), caps the device pixel ratio at 2 and draws every subscriber from one shared WebGL context with `pause`, `resume` and `renderStatic(time)` on its handle. `dither.ts` is the CPU path: `createDitherLoop` at 30 fps by default, paused off screen and on a hidden tab, and "honouring prefers-reduced-motion by rendering exactly one static frame and never scheduling a rAF loop at all".

## 6. The proposal

### 6.1 The shader block and the background

The block kind stays `material` in the document and becomes "Shader" in every word a seller reads (item 13). A shader is either an object (the block, dragged and resized like a picture, item 14) or the ground of a slide (`SlideBackground.shader`, item 11), and the kit may name a background shader that title and section layouts pick (item 11). Insert > Shader enters the default view only when every row of 6.10 passes on the preview twice and on production once (`docs/FOCUS.md` section 1); until then it sits behind Tools > Advanced tools as Material does today.

### 6.2 The document shape

Item 16: `MaterialBlock` gains `motion`, `fit`, `controls` and `palette`, each optional with a defined absence; `SlideBackground` gains `shader`; the kit gains `background.shader`; `Asset.source.backend` gains `client`. Nothing existing changes meaning, `migrate` stays the identity and `schemaVersion` stays 1. The common controls are stored beside the resolved uniforms so a deck written by an agent with raw uniforms and a deck written by a seller with sliders read alike.

### 6.3 The picker

Item 3: one gallery for Insert > Shader and Change background > Shader, thumbnails at 320 by 200 built once per catalog change in the default kit and tinted to the deck's kit, presets as a row of tiles under each material, search, five categories, the featured order (liquid metal, gem smoke, god rays, mesh gradient, the studio field, the dither gradient, smoke ring, grain gradient first). One click inserts in the largest free rectangle and selects, or places the ground.

### 6.4 The Shader section of Format options

Item 4 with items 5, 6, 7 and 8. Rows in the panel's 32 px grammar, each a label, a slider and a number field; a section per group with the remembered collapse (`formatOptions.section.<id>.toggle`): Shader, Preset, Colors, Form, Light and texture, Orientation, Motion, Frame, Dither, Advanced. Live preview through the mount while dragging, one commit on release, one undo entry, every control with a `data-control` id the window API lists.

### 6.5 The still, the show and the exports

The renderer's resting rule holds: the document carries the frame, every still surface draws it, and only the editor and the show mount a live shader over it (items 2, 9, 10). The frame is made in the editor from the live mount at 2x, the hosted job is the fallback and the agent's route, and both record their backend and renderer. The PDF and the PowerPoints get the 2x raster; the show plays the animation when the block's `motion.play` and the View setting say so and the device allows.

### 6.6 Performance rules

Items 15 and 17: one live mount per stage, 30 frames per second and 1x pixels in the editor below zoom 100, 60 and 2x in the show, speed 0 when hidden, off screen or under reduced motion, no mount in clones, thumbnails or the picker, the 3200 by 1800 pixel cap, and a measured row for the longest animation frame with one shader on the stage.

### 6.7 The brand kit's palette

Item 8: the kit's six roles map to the catalog's palette roles; presets are computed per kit; a kit change re renders and re captures.

### 6.8 The actions on every transport

Item 18: `shader.list`, `shader.insert`, `shader.set`, `shader.capture`, `shader.render`, `slide.setBackgroundShader`, with CLI usages and MCP tools, the `material.*` ids kept as aliases.

### 6.9 Licences
