The monogram from rectangles

Five rectangles in the GT bar monogram's units are sheared 12 degrees and cut once at mid cap height. The result is 7 parallelograms in one path in currentColor, with no font, no mask and no id.

Both are set at an 80 px cap. They share the skew, the cut, the crossbar's 30 units and the bars' 30, 36 and 30.

UnitsThe GT monogram's: a cap of 120 units from y 40 to the baseline at y 160
Skew12 degrees: x becomes x minus tan(12°) times y
Cuty 96 to 104, 8 units, applied to the geometry
Crossbar134 by 30 units: an arm of 50 on each side of a 34 unit stem
Top barExtends the arm 22 units past its left end, overlapping it by 2
Middle bar64 by 36 units on the cut, left as two lines of 14, 12 units clear of the stem
Bottom bar44 by 30 units, 12 units clear of the stem
