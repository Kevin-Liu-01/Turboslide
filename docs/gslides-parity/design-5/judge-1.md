# Judge 1 of 3, the Google Slides power user and the sales enablement lead

Written 2026-09-14 against the three proposals of `docs/gslides-parity/design-5/`, the eleven reports of `research-5/`, SPEC-3 section 17, SPEC-4 section 7 and the code at `main` `d5d7f07` read with `git show`. The lens is the person who lives in Google Slides all day and the person who equips a sales team: the fidelity of motion, media, templates, import and page behaviours, and the length and honesty of the path a rep walks. Scores are 1 to 10 on fidelity, ease for sales, architecture fit, export fidelity and buildability; the total is the sum.

## What was checked beyond the proposals

The three proposals agree on most of the round (the slide level `transition` and `animations` fields, `compileMotion` in the render package, the still rule, the OOXML post process modules, the media block over a `MediaAsset` union, R04's reader, R08's page and print design, R09's writers, R10's text tools, the Temml equation block, Edit theme over a record, the `ts-plate` second theme, transient chat). The judgement therefore turned on the points where they part, and each of those was read against the report or the code it rests on.

## Proposal 1, Google faithful

Scores: fidelity 9, ease for sales 7, architecture fit 8, export fidelity 9, buildability 7. Total 40.

Fidelity. This is the proposal a Google Slides power user would recognise row by row. Every entry point of the Motion panel is Google's (six openers plus the chord), the panel carries every control, the Insert video dialog draws Google's three tabs with the search tab disabled and explained, Format options carries Google's two playback sections with Google's defaults, the Import slides dialog is Google's two steps with "Select slides: All", the Template gallery keeps Google's three headings, Tools > Dictionary is a panel with a search field and definitions as Google's is (the other two settle for a link), the Accessibility menu draws under the toggle with the Verbalize rows, File > Language is a submenu, Help > Training and Updates are pages, and the present toolbar's pen and in show downloads retire their stubs, which the other two proposals leave standing in what is meant to be the last parity round. Every Turboslide addition is marked `turboslide: true` so the completeness test keeps counting Google's rows alone; the special characters drawing box gets a standalone recogniser rather than an omission. The slips are small: the animation default of 1000 ms has no source while PowerPoint's 500 ms does (R01 6.5), the `spd` thresholds ignore R01's recommendation, and the Presenter view's next preview stays at rest where a rep would want the next click's state.

Ease for sales. Eight templates with real copy led by a fourteen slide Sales pitch whose Pricing slide is a `rows` block (so By paragraph and `bldP` both hold), about thirty building blocks in nine categories stored as JSON under `decks/templates/building-blocks/`, which a sales operations person can author without touching code, the pen for a live demo, downloads inside the show for the "send me that" moment after a call, the import report sentence with Details, and chat with a 200 message window so a manager who joins late sees the thread. The path is Google's path, which is what a rep already knows. It loses points for the resting next preview, for leaving media on restricted decks to Kevin (the demo of an unreleased product is the asset a rep least wants public), and for adding no sales shortcuts of its own.

Architecture fit. The foundation holds: the schedule is one pure function in the render package, the still rule is enforced where the state lives, the post process gains named modules in the existing pattern, `themeCss` is one override stylesheet, templates are decks, `importedThemes[]` gives Google's "In this presentation" list a home (the other two proposals apply an imported theme and store nothing). Two choices are less clean: chat has two retention paths, and the motion actions are filed under `slide` and `block` instead of a group of their own, which the palette and the generated skills would show.

Export fidelity. The transition table with its `p14` fallbacks, the five level timing tree with R01 6.4's presets, `bldP` per paragraph, the media nodes, the read back in `export check`, the LibreOffice attribute assertions in the container, ODP from the same schedule, SVG in three text modes, OMML with a picture fallback, the handout gate per cell, and a residual that names every object whose still state differs from its state after the last step. It is the only proposal that writes Reflection and Recolor as native OOXML (`a:reflection`, `a:duotone`, `a:grayscl`) rather than baking them into the raster, which is what PowerPoint would open as editable effects.

Buildability. The only proposal with hours (about 628), a ten day plan with per day deliverables, acceptance commands per builder, a fixer round and a risks section. The load is uneven: B6 carries the theme mode, the second theme, eight templates of about seventy slides, thirty building blocks, chat, the help pages, the hero, the card, the dither families, the crate patterns and Reflection and Recolor in 96 hours, and B4 carries the page, print, ODP, SVG and the shape interpreter. The seams land on day 1, not day 0. The scope is the widest of the three, which is also what the brief asks for.

## Proposal 2, sales first

Scores: fidelity 7, ease for sales 9, architecture fit 7, export fidelity 8, buildability 7. Total 38.

Fidelity. Google's labels sit in Google's places for the panel, the dialogs, Page setup, the print dropdown, Preferences, the spell card and the Accessibility menu, and the unverified labels carry `unverified: true` in the fixture. The departures are product decisions dressed as defaults: Blank and the work templates move to the Plate theme, every work template slide ships with a Fade transition, the gallery's categories are Turboslide's, the Import slides checkbox starts checked on a guess, Dictionary is a link, and the pen and in show download stubs stay. None of these is a Google behaviour, and the first two change what every new presentation looks like, which SPEC-4 7 records as recommended against and R03 8 leaves to Kevin.

Ease for sales. The strongest of the three. The seven scenarios (the imported PowerPoint, the demo video, the pricing reveal, the branded template, the leave behind and the PPTX for procurement, chat on a call, the 4:3 customer template) are the right seven and each is answered in a named section. PPTX import is the front door with the one sentence report; the Sales pitch has a demo video slide set to Play (on click); the presenter console previews the state after the next click, which is the single most useful addition for a rep clicking through a reveal; "Sound is off until you click" tells the presenter what happened; media on restricted decks are keyed from day one; the corner slot takes the customer's mark and no other company's mark appears on a customer's deck; the chat panel's first line says where a comment belongs; the 4:3 flow is walked end to end. The import default of `adopt` for both doors, so an imported near black follows the appearance switch, is the right call for a deck shown in dark.

Architecture fit. The same spine as the others (one schedule, the still rule, one media controller shared by the show and the standalone script, a pure autocorrect engine, the pointer regex closed to `/themeEdits`). It loses a point for the pricing table animated By paragraph, a document model slip in the flagship template, and one for the file ownership: B5 owns `Editor.tsx`, `Gestures.tsx` and the 123 sites while B4 owns nested groups whose level state lives in those files, and B3 owns `decks/templates/blank/deck.json`'s theme line "after B4's theme lands", so the templates the check chain uses as fixtures depend on B1, B2 and B4 landing first.

Export fidelity. The same post process, `export.run` gaining `media: 'embed' | 'poster'`, the ODP writer with LibreOffice's conversion as the oracle, three SVG modes, OMML, the handouts, and the Sales pitch as the fixture the motion e2e, the media e2e and the import round trip share, so a real sales deck's export is gate checked. The acceptance row asserting `bldP build="p"` on the pricing table's shape cannot pass as written; Reflection and Recolor are baked into the raster.

Buildability. A day 0 schema merge, disjoint files, ports, a merge order, 36 steps, nine acceptance gates and a 28 row table for Kevin with a default per row. No hours. B3 carries R04's entire reader plus four templates, the building blocks, the panes, the gallery, the Open and Import slides dialogs and two e2e specs; B4 carries the theme mode, the second theme, the equation block with its OMML transform, the dither families, the crate, Reflection and Recolor and nested groups.

## Proposal 3, architecture

Scores: fidelity 7, ease for sales 6, architecture fit 10, export fidelity 9, buildability 7. Total 39.

Fidelity. Google's panel, dialogs, dialog steps, Page setup, print rows, Preferences, spelling, language and the Accessibility menu are all present with Google's labels. The file side is the most careful: `spd` from LibreOffice's public thresholds as R01 6.2 asks, `nextAc="seek"` with `onPrev` and `onNext`, the `mc:AlternateContent` regex fix in `ooxml/groups.ts`, `cleanContentTypes` correcting `audio/mp3`. The surface side is thinner: two templates (the second is the first re-themed), Dictionary as a link, the special characters drawing box kept omitted as "a handwriting recognition service" when the other two show a standalone recogniser is feasible, the pen and in show download stubs left standing, the next preview at rest.

Ease for sales. The twelve slide sales starter is real and complete (agenda, problem, what changes, product, key statistics, how it works, pricing, timeline, team, quote, next steps), building blocks scale into the content box on a 4:3 page, `motion.compile` answers "what plays on the third click of slide 4" for an agent, and the report is rows an agent and a dialog both read. But there is no demo video slide, no next step preview, nothing for media on restricted decks, and Insert > Templates "by purpose" over two templates is a pane with little in it.

Architecture fit. The best design document of the three. It extends the one truth rule explicitly: the renderer emits the resting document and nothing else; `compileMotion` is read by the show, the HTML script, the PPTX timing tree and the ODP animation tree; one `Scene` gains `page`, `transition`, `schedule`, `media`, `equations`, `language` and `themeCss` so the pptx, odp and svg writers read the scene and never the deck, and a fourth writer would read the same object; a block id has four spellings, one per surface, and `renderSlide` writes `data-block` so the present layer addresses what the exports name; every schema addition is tabled with its meaning when absent, its validator code, its writer and its consumers; motion on a grammar slide does not convert it; dangling animations are normalised in the reducer, not refused by the validator; `deck.set`'s pointer regex stays closed to `/page` and `/themeEdits`; the paragraph count is supplied by the caller so the browser and the exporter count the same paragraphs; `MOTION_LABELS` is the one table the panel, the CLI help and the skills read; the new action groups follow the existing sixteen; chat rides the stream as `kind: 'chat'` beside `kind: 'comment'`; the integrator lands typed seams on day 0 with `NotImplementedError` handlers and the seams every builder types against are listed by name.

Export fidelity. Equal to proposal 1 on the writers, ahead on the checks: the motion e2e asserts the Perfect report is `perfect: true` with the resting raster equal to the editor's render, the SVG `export check` validates the namespace, the `viewBox`, no script, no `on*`, every `href` a fragment or data URI and every `use` target in `defs`, the import fidelity table is written to `verification-5/import-fidelity.json` with a visual number reported but not gated, and the PowerPoint manual checklist is extended with the Transitions gallery, the Animation Pane order, the eight media fields, native math, the edited theme and the 4:3 page. Reflection and Recolor are baked into the raster.

Buildability. Every file, fixture and test is named, the day 0 seam is the most complete, the template content is the lightest. No hours. B6 is the most overloaded owner in any of the three proposals: the equation block, renderer, toolbar and OMML transform, `themeCss`, the theme mode, the second theme, the Themes panel, and all ten deferred engineering items including the Rust crate, the dither families, Reflection and Recolor, nested groups, the WebSocket client, the per deck card, the vitals route, the Lighthouse workflow and the shape interpreter.

## Gaps shared by all three

- Import slides land after the current slide in every proposal; Google's help (R02 c.1) says imported slides land at the end of the presentation. The action keeps `after`; the dialog should default `after` to the last slide.
- Captions in present mode (`captionsStub`, "Captions are a browser speech service in English only") stay a stub in all three, while all three build Dictate speaker notes over the same Web Speech API. A standalone captions row is one more consumer of that code and belongs in the round or on Kevin's list with a reason.
- None of the three states the default of "Keep original theme" as unverified with the reading taken; Google's phrasing reads as unchecked and the fixture should say so.

## Winner

Proposal 1. The totals are close (40, 38, 39) and the lens decides: this judge weighs the fidelity of motion, media, templates, import and page behaviours and the rep's path, and proposal 1 leads on the first (9 against 7 and 7) and is second on the second (7 against 9 and 6) while giving up little on the file side. Proposal 3 is the better architecture document and its spine should be grafted whole; proposal 2 is the better sales document and its scenarios and additions should be grafted where they do not change Google's defaults or the product's identity without Kevin.

## Rejections

1. Proposal 2's move of Blank and the work templates to the Plate theme by default. SPEC-4 7 records the question as recommended against and R03 8 item 1 leaves the default to Kevin; the round ships the second blank card (proposals 1 and 3) and puts the switch on Kevin's list with proposal 2's argument attached.
2. Proposal 2's Fade transition pre-applied to every work template slide. Google's templates carry none; a rep who wants the S3 deck ticks Apply to all slides once.
3. Proposal 2's pricing table animated By paragraph and the acceptance row asserting `bldP build="p"` on the table's shape. By paragraph is for text carriers (R01 3) and a `p:graphicFrame` carries no `bldP`; the pricing slide is a `rows` block as in proposal 1.
4. Proposal 2's gallery categories Work, Brand and Blank. Google's Personal, Work and Education are unverified but they are the only evidence; they stay with the flag.
5. Proposal 2's "Keep original theme" default of checked. Google's help reads as a box to check; the default is unchecked and flagged.
6. Proposal 3's omission of the special characters drawing box as "a handwriting recognition service". Proposals 1 and 2 show a standalone stroke histogram recogniser; proposal 1's stays.
7. Proposal 3's two template gallery. Insert > Templates "by purpose" needs the purposes; proposal 1's eight templates stay, with proposal 2's demo slide grafted.
8. Proposal 3's Dictionary as a link only. Google's Dictionary is a panel with definitions; proposal 1's Wiktionary panel with the link as the checkout fallback stays, the provider on Kevin's list as R03 decision 4.
9. Proposal 3's B6 ownership of the equation block, the theme mode, the second theme and all ten deferred engineering items under one builder.
10. Proposal 1's 1000 ms default for a new animation and its two path chat retention (replaced by grafts 4 and 8).
11. Proposals 2 and 3 leaving the pen and the in show download stubs standing; proposal 1 retires them and that stays.

- The unverified Google facts adopted by the winning design are proposal 1's section 13 item 14 plus the "Keep original theme" default and the end of presentation landing of Import slides; the verifier lists them with the reading taken.
