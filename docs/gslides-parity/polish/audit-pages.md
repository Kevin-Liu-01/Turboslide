# Polish audit: the pages

The decks list, the new draft, the title, the trash, Share, Slideshow and the presenter, the downloads and print, version history, comments, the browser back and forward, and a reload mid edit. Driven on production https://www.turboslide.com (main at `877eb690`, the blob tier, anonymous principals) on 2026-09-28 between 19:17 and 20:05 UTC, by the pages auditor of the polish round.

Method. Headless Chromium 151 from the worktree's `playwright-core` 1.62.1 at 1440 by 900 and again at 1280 by 800, `deviceScaleFactor` 2. Every gesture went through the product at human speed: the pointer moved in steps, keys landed 40 to 90 ms apart, double clicks and drags were the browser's own, menus were opened from the menubar and the card buttons, dialogs were driven by their own buttons. Every scratch deck was made from `/new` and trashed and deleted forever through the product in a `finally` block (File > Move to trash, Delete forever on `/decks/trash`, a 404 on `/edit/<id>`); no run left a deck behind. Every value below was read from the screen, the DOM, `window.turboslide.studio.describe().state`, the clipboard, the downloaded file or a screenshot, and every screenshot named here was looked at. Nothing was fixed and no source file was touched. Google Slides is the reference: the "Google" column says what Google does for the same gesture.

The scripts are under the session scratchpad, `polish/pages/` (`lib.mjs` the harness, `01-decks.mjs`, `02-file.mjs`, `03-share.mjs`, `04-present.mjs`, `05-versions.mjs`, `06-trash-lag.mjs`), with their JSON rows beside them (`<area>-1440.json`, `<area>-1280.json`). The screenshots are in `docs/gslides-parity/polish/audit-pages/`, named `<area>-<nn>-<step>.png` (the 1280 set carries a `-1280` suffix); the downloaded files were opened by the scripts (the PDF's page count, the PowerPoint's slide parts, the bundle's entries, the PNG and JPEG dimensions, the web page's text).

Severity: 3 a seller would call it broken, jank or wrong; 2 a seller notices; 1 a person who looks closely notices. Effort 1 to 5.

## Summary

What a seller feels in this area today. The editor's own pages are solid: a deck is made from `/new` in one write, the title renames from the field and from File > Rename, the show runs every key Google runs, the presenter window follows the audience window and back, every download arrives and opens, version history restores and undoes, comments post, reply and resolve, and every one of the 115 interactions driven landed where the product says it lands. What breaks that is the step from the editor to the list, and the words the product uses on that step. The seller makes a deck, clicks the mark, and the list does not show it; nor does the Recent row, the Open dialog or the Import slides dialog, for up to half a minute. They rename a deck from its card and the page shows the same deck twice with two names. They open Share and the dialog has already made the deck editable by anyone with the link, and tells them to change that before they send it. They change the link's role and the address they already sent is dead. They click Print and every page is black. They click Details on a download and read "Perfect: no" and "SwiftShader". A stranger who types the deck's address gets an editor with insert tools they cannot use. None of these is hard to fix; most are one file each.

The ten to fix first, in order:

1. The list, the Recent row, the Open dialog and Import slides lag the store by up to half a minute (items 1 and 8).
2. Rename from a card leaves the Recent row with the old name, so one deck shows twice with two names (item 2).
3. The print preview draws every slide dark while the PDF it downloads is light (item 3).
4. The Share dialog opens a fresh deck as "Anyone with the link, Editor" and tells the seller to pick Viewer (item 4).
5. Changing the link's role mints a new address and the one already sent stops opening (item 5).
6. A stranger at `/edit/<id>` gets a View only editor with insert tools and the viewer's tree inside the filmstrip (item 6).
7. The download's Details dialog is an engineering report with the wrong format in its heading (item 7).
8. File > Move to trash holds the editor for 12 seconds before it leaves (item 8).
9. A refused download shows the store's raw "Vercel Blob: Too many requests" sentence and does not retry (item 9).
10. The same deck listed twice, in the Recent row and in the grid, and card titles that clip mid letter (items 10 and 11).

Features driven: 115 distinct interactions (39 on the list, the draft, the title, the trash and the browser; 22 File rows and downloads; 20 Share and the visitors; 12 Slideshow and the presenter; 18 version history and comments; 5 timed list and trash steps), each at 1440 and again at 1280 except the timed steps, which ran once. Two rows were not driven: the show's Auto-play row (hidden behind Advanced tools, a Later stub) and File > Page setup (a Later stub the default view does not list). Every scratch deck answered 404 at the end of every run.
