# Audit: the chrome

The polish round's audit of the editor's chrome: the title row, every menu and submenu row, the toolbar at 1440 and 1280, the Format options panel, the dialogs, the right click menus, tooltips, Tools > Advanced tools, Help > Keyboard shortcuts against the real keys, both appearances and the layout shift on load. Driven on 2026-09-28 between 12:46 and 13:21 PDT (the main run) with headless Chromium (playwright-core 1.62.1 from the worktree) at 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800, device scale 2, at human speed (the mouse moved in steps, 40 to 90 ms per key, real double clicks and drags), with a picture after every action worth judging under `audit-chrome/`, named by the step. The script is `audit-chrome/audit-chrome.mjs` (a copy of the scratchpad's; it imports nothing from the repository but playwright-core and reads the menu model from `audit-chrome/model.json`, the dump `audit-chrome/dump-model.mjs` makes of `packages/chrome/src/menus/model.ts`); its record is `audit-chrome/audit-chrome-run.json` (454 rows, the reads of every menu, toolbar, panel, dialog, right click menu and tooltip, the layout shift frames, the console errors and the failed responses) with the follow-up run's `audit-chrome-run-arrange-toggles-print-advanced-shift.json` beside it and the domain run's under `audit-chrome/www/`. `audit-chrome/sheet.py` tiles the pictures into the contact sheets the auditor read.

## 1. Summary

What a seller feels in this area today. The chrome is quiet, square and consistent at first sight: one face, hairlines, no colour but the selection blue, every plate the same paper card. Then the small things pile up. Half the menu rows have a glyph and half do not, so every menu looks half finished. A tooltip pops up on a greyed row and covers the two rows under it. With a text box selected, Bold works from the menu and from Cmd+B while Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Superscript and Subscript do nothing, and on a shape they answer "Select a text block first" from rows the menu just enabled. A spacing row on a table writes a field the schema refuses, and Redo then prints a validator's sentence with a JSON path in it. Change shape opens a gallery that runs 55 px past the bottom of a 900 px screen. Check slides repeats "Slide 2 has an empty table cell; type the copy or remove the placeholder (gslides-parity SPEC 5.4)." nine times. The Background dialog lists the deck's pictures as clipped sentences in narrow boxes with no thumbnails. Share asks for your name with "studio" filled in. The Word art bar stays above the sheet through every later menu and dialog. Print settings and preview drew "The store is busy" on a deck four writes old. And on the domain itself none of the last three rounds' work is there, because www.turboslide.com serves a build from before them.

The ten items to fix first, in the order the fix should run:

1. Point the domain at the deployment of main (item 1): until then every seller reads a build from before the vector, objects and ship two rounds, and every other fix below is invisible on turboslide.com.
2. The spacing rows on a table and the Redo that fails with a validator sentence (item 2).
3. Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Superscript and Subscript with a block selected, from the menu and from the keys (item 4), with the enabled predicates and the tail buttons that refuse on a heading (item 17).
4. The Change shape and Mask image plates that run past the screen (item 5).
5. Check slides' sentence: no spec citation, one finding per table (item 3).
6. The Background dialog's picture list (item 6) and the Format options fields a rectangle does not have, with the overlapping Orientation label (item 7).
7. The print route's error card and the store's 503s on the write path (items 8 and 24).
8. Tooltips: none on a disabled row or over the rows under it, no block id on a handle, a sentence or nothing (items 21, 19, 47).
9. A glyph on every menu row, or none (item 25), and the two controls that share the cursor glyph (item 9).
10. The words: "studio" as a name, "Perfect" as a mode, "(change 8)", "12:39 PM to 12:39 PM", "Revision r4 arrived from outside this editor", "Colour" beside "Fill color", "Film strip" beside "filmstrip" (items 10, 29, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38).

What was driven, counted from the three runs' records: 10 menus and 360 model rows read with nothing selected and with a text box, a picture, a shape and a table selected (the reads in `menus.none` and `menus.selected` of the JSON), 203 menu rows clicked, 136 toolbar controls clicked at 1440 and the five tails read at 1280, 5 Format options panels with 22 sections opened, 21 dialogs and plates, 8 right click targets, 104 tooltips, 21 chords, 2 appearances, 5 loads for the layout shift, plus the follow-up run's 20 Arrange rows, 14 check rows, the print route and the Advanced tools switch, and the domain run's menus, toolbar, dialogs and right click menus.
