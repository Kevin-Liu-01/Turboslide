## 4. The rounds

### 4.0 The rules every round follows

- Rows. Every row is in the matrix's shape (`docs/gslides-parity/focus/core-matrix.json`: `id`, `feature`, `interaction` with its bound, `driver`, `today`, `severity` on a broken or flaky row, `evidence`, and `measure` for a measurement row). The round's integrator lists every row with its bound in the round's day 0 note, so each bound is fixed before the run (`docs/FOCUS.md` 254). A row enters `core-matrix.json` in the push of the mechanism that makes it pass. No push therefore ships a known red row of an unparkable feature, which would block the ship (`scripts/probes/core-matrix.mjs` 724 to 729; `docs/FOCUS.md` 265). A row whose fix a round drops is either parked through `parks` naming only that row's own new controls, or not written.
- One mechanism per push. `docs/FOCUS.md` 273 makes a ship one branch, one mechanism, its tests and its note. Each round lists its pushes; a lane ships as several pushes when it holds several mechanisms.
- Features. `perf` joins `CORE_FEATURES` (`scripts/probes/core-matrix.mjs` 106; the check at 558 refuses an unknown feature) in Round 2 lane P3, as a parkable feature. Every `perf` row carries `measure: true`, which the verdict records and never holds (716 to 719). The hold is the exit code of `scripts/perf-budget.mjs` (1589: exit 1 on a failure unless `--report`), run without `--report` against the round's preview in the gate step. A `cost` row is a measurement row that holds the ship only over its ceiling on the preview (`core-matrix.mjs` 271 to 272). The `accounts.*` rows keep the realtime round's feature `share`, which is unparkable.
- The load rule. A `perf` reading taken at a load average of 20 or more is void. The perf step refuses to judge it, records the load, and the run ledger names "load" as the reason for the rerun, which `docs/FOCUS.md` 266 allows. The flaky clause of `docs/FOCUS.md` 266 judges rows that pass or fail; a measurement row records a number. If the load stays at 20 or more, question 28 applies.
- Gates, under `R:docs/REALTIME.md` 19 rules 1 to 3. (1) Each push drives its rows on the local memory tier, and pushes that touch the channel also on the local `do` tier with two node servers and `wrangler dev` (`R:docs/CLOUDFLARE.md` 502 to 507). The integrator runs the whole matrix once on the local memory tier on the merged tree. (2) One preview per round, deployed by the integrator from the merged tree and reused by every fixer. The hosted run is narrowed to the rows only a deployment can read (the store tier's classes, the access rules, the CDN, `cost.*`, `perf.*`, `setup.*`) plus the guard's seller path. Because one preview serves the round, a regression is found per push on that push's local run; the preview's `perf` and `cost` readings are compared per row with the previous round's table. (3) The production table is read once after the round's last push, with narrowed reruns beside it.
- Ship. One push per mechanism through `~/.config/turboslide/gt-follow.sh` in the integrator's order, with `deployed <sha>` logged before the next push (`R:docs/CLOUDFLARE.md` 521). `docs/FOCUS.md` 6.2 rules 3 to 5 (lines 274 to 276) apply: the hand walk on the seller path before each push, the rows red on production read again narrowed before the push, and a row red once on production read again narrowed within the hour. A docs-only push runs the guard's smoke alone (`R:docs/REALTIME.md` 19 rule 3).
- Evidence. Each round follows the policy of section 5.3: tracked pictures under 25 MB for the round, raw drives under `.turboslide/<round>/`. This program's own folder `docs/gslides-parity/next/` holds 239 pictures of 22.7 MB, 33 of them over 200 KB (`find` at 04:20Z). B6 prunes the pictures no note links under 5.3 rule 5 after Round 1.
- Sizes. A feature round's size is pipeline days at the measured pace of about 25 effort points a day (audit-features 139), including the round's rows and its narrowed hosted gate. The sizes of Rounds 1 to 3 and of the version log round are this document's estimates: no audit sized them. Each round's day 0 note restates its size from its lanes' notes.
- Guard time. Every push costs one guard check of 982 to 1,152 s (3.3). A round's ship time is therefore at least its push count times about 17 to 19 minutes.

#### 4.4.3 The rules the features rounds share

- Each lane is one mechanism and one push. A round of two features is two pushes.
- `packages/chrome/src/menus/model.ts`, `menus/strings.ts` and `packages/schema/src/actions.ts` are shared in every features round: the first lane in the round's merge order owns its hunks, the second lands after it, and the integrator regenerates `packages/agent/generated/*` once.
- A feature's rows enter the matrix in its own push. A row may carry `parks` naming the feature's own new controls, so a red feature parks its own controls and holds no other lane (`docs/FOCUS.md` 264).
- A feature that moves bytes or calls a priced service carries a `cost.*` measurement row in its own push.
- Gates: the rule of 4.0; a feature on the Durable Object channel or D1 also drives the local `do` tier.

## 6. Risks and the rule that stops a round

Kevin asked "why do your deploys keep regressing?". The people round shipped `0554a01f` with three core rows red twice on production and left them "named for its owner" (`docs/FOCUS.md` 270), and several lanes have reached production in one push before. These rules answer that.

### 6.1 The rule that stops a round

1. A core row red twice on production after a ship is a stop: within the hour it is fixed forward on a hotfix branch, or the ship is reverted by promoting the previous production deployment; no row is left in the table named for an owner (`docs/FOCUS.md` 272).
2. One mechanism per push, with `deployed <sha>` in `gt-follow.log` before the next push, so a red on production names one change (`docs/FOCUS.md` 273; `R:docs/CLOUDFLARE.md` 521). A row enters the matrix in the push that makes it pass (4.0).
3. Never two rounds shipping at once. This program's first push waits for the realtime round's last code lane on `main` with `deployed <sha>` logged and its production table read. If the realtime flip still waits on Kevin's Google client or the bypass at that point, the flip ships later as one push placed between this program's pushes under rule 2, and its production table is read then. A round's first push waits for the previous round's production table and its release note in `docs/updates.md`. The next round's lanes may build while the previous round ships, and they rebase onto `main` before their first push.
4. A round stops pushing when the guard holds one of its shas (`~/.config/turboslide/gt-follow.held`), when a `cost` row is over its ceiling on the preview, when the perf step exits 1 on the preview at a load average under 20, when a `realtime` row is red on production (unparkable, `R:docs/CLOUDFLARE.md` 517), or when production's Durable Object rows written pass 50,000 in a day (`R:docs/CLOUDFLARE.md` 608).
5. A row red once on production is read again narrowed within the hour, with the row's own setup (`docs/FOCUS.md` 276).
6. A round that stops twice waits for Kevin before its next push.
