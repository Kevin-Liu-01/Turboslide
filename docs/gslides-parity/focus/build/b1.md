### 13.1 The diagnosis

The failing run's strings decide the mechanism. `<scratch>/ver9/pw/realtime-1.log` Expected: 100 A's, the paragraph, 100 B's (492 characters). Received: 98 A's, "Ever", 7 A's, "y line of copy…", 100 B's (497 characters, 105 typed A's). Five characters landed twice and the caret moved four places to the right once, after the 93rd A; four is a B burst's length. That is `absorbedText` diffing against a stale base: the document held five of A's characters that the session's `lastBurst.current` did not, so `textDiff(base, remote)` read them as part of the collaborator's change (a replacement from offset 93), shifted the local re-insertion point and the caret by 5 plus 4, and re-inserted the five with the two unflushed ones after "Ever" (a splice at 93 plus 9, four characters into "Every").

The one writer that advances the document without the session seeing it is the Editor's re-send: `packages/viewer/src/Editor.tsx` `useEffect([doc])` reads `sessionReconcile(expectedDocText, docText)` (`text-fit.ts`), which answers `resend` whenever the plain text differs, so every collaborator's insert arms its 150 ms timer, and when the document has not moved again by then it calls `writeText(live, textFromNode(editable))` with the editable's text. `writeText` runs `textBurstMutation` and sends A's unflushed keystrokes as one splice, and sets `committedText`, but `InlineText`'s `flushBurst` never ran, so its `lastBurst.current` stayed at the last burst (or the last absorbed remote).
