- The placement, `apps/studio/src/editor/place-insert.ts` (pure, unit tested): a chrome `block.insert` of a table or a chart with a box and no `after` or `z` (`wantsPlacement`) is placed before the store action runs. The free rectangles are the maximal empty rectangles of the body on the grid of the taken edges. The object wants its default box (or 640 by 360 for a chart that shares the slot with content), takes the free rectangle that gives it the most area, shrinks to the rectangle on each axis when the rectangle is smaller (never below 480 by 120 for a table or 320 by 160 for a chart), and sits at the top of its rectangle centred across it. ### 5. Decisions recorded

- The placed object sits at the top of its free rectangle, centred across it, and not at the rectangle's centre (1.1 says why with the numbers): the row forbids the chart's overlap with a typed table, and a centred table leaves no strip a chart fits in. Google centres every insert and lets it overlap; Turboslide stacks the inserts down the body, which is what a seller lays out by hand (a title, a table, a chart under it).
- A chart that shares the slot wants 640 by 360 (the spec's number) and takes the room left when the room is smaller, down to 320 by 160; a table down to 480 by 120; below that the slot is taken and the object cascades. The numbers are `place-insert.ts` constants with their reasons.
- The agent contract is exact and untouched: an insert through the window API or HTTP keeps its box and selects nothing; only the chrome's own dispatch places and selects. The distinction is the dispatch context's `origin`, read by the `block.insert`, `diagram.insert` and server side write handlers; every other handler ignores it.
- The chrome insert of a slide that is not a canvas yet measures the conversion twice (the placement's `withCanvas`, then the store action's), about one hidden render more on the first insert of a grammar slide; the store action's input carries no measured boxes and this lane did not widen it. If the round after wants it, `blockInsert` can take `measured` as `withCanvas` does.
- The `subtitle-body` entry is filtered out of `LAYOUTS` until its id is in `LAYOUT_IDS` (R1), so the tree typechecks and every test passes in both states and nothing breaks for another lane while the one line waits; the row `slides.layout.subtitle-prompt` is not driven until then.
- The body paragraph of Title and body is `p1` (the layout's only paragraph); the walk finds placeholders by their `data-run` and slot, not by id (grep of `scripts/probes` and `apps/studio/e2e` for `p1`, `p2` and `headRight`: none).
- The serverSide failure snackbar shows for the window API's caller alone; the chrome's route (B2's `uploadPicture`) says the rejection itself, so one failure gives one snackbar. The error word (`publish({ error })`) is written for both.
- The selection after an insert travels as a window event and not as a handle call, because the controller has no handle on the stage and the stage cannot import the studio; the name is pinned by a test on both sides. The same route can carry any later "select these objects" from the controller (an assist card's accept, a paste through the API on request).
