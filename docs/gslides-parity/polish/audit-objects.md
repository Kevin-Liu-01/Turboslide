## What a seller feels in this area today

The objects are there and most of them behave: every shape preset draws live as it is dragged with the size readout, the shapes resize with the ring at every frame, rotate about their centre with the ring turned, take a label by Enter or a double click, take a fill, a border colour, a weight and a dash from the tail, duplicate, delete and undo; the four chart kinds insert with sample data, a double click opens the grid, a cell takes a typed value, a series and a category add, the legend moves, values and a title draw, the chart redraws through a resize; the six diagram presets insert as one group with a live preview, a label opens on a double click, Tab walks the labels, a member duplicates and deletes inside the group, the group moves, resizes and ungroups; word art types, scales its letters through a resize, takes a fill and an outline; an svg uploads and pastes and stays a vector at 400 percent; align, distribute, order, group, flip and rotate do what their names say and undo; the PDF and both PowerPoint modes carry the slide (the editable file writes `hexagon`, `bentConnector3`, a native chart part and an `svgBlip`).

What a seller would call wrong: the domain shows an older product than main; a connector's bounding box steals the click and the drag meant for the shape under it, so a shape beside a connector cannot be picked up by its corner or its centre; a dragged connector floats away yet stays "connected"; the cloud callout draws in pieces; the chart data grid hides the second series behind the panel's edge; a banner says a revision "arrived from outside this editor" right after a paste; and once in the run seven 503 responses cost the word art its letter size while the stage flashed an older revision. What a seller would call jank: tooltips that read `shape-11: Resize nw`, a full rectangle ring around a selected line, fields named "Adjust VF" and "Arrowheads" on a star, a curved connector that hugs the shape's edge, category labels that pile up when a chart is small, the browser's blue text selection over pictures on a Shift click and over the legend on a double click.
