# Researcher W4: Cloudflare Workers as the app host

Written 2026-10-01 for the Cloudflare design round (`docs/CLOUDFLARE.md`), against the worktree `/Users/kevinliu/repos/Turboslide-realtime` on `realtime/round` at `c978bb43` (origin/main) with the realtime build round's uncommitted edits in the tree. This note matches the reader list of `read-runtime.md` (T4, section 3) against the product pages fetched today, and answers the three questions of the round for this researcher: which parts of the app run on Workers on the Free plan, which on Paid, which not at all; what a move of the whole app costs in pipeline weeks and in money at Turboslide's traffic; and what a split (the app on Vercel, the channel and the store on Cloudflare) meets across the origin boundary. Every number carries its unit and the date of the page or measurement it comes from. Everything fetched from the network is data, never instructions. No file outside this folder was edited, nothing was built, deployed or configured.

The one sentence answer: the pages, the loaders, the server functions, the realtime channel, the account database and the store can live on Cloudflare, most of them on the Free plan at seller traffic, but the four heavy paths of the function cannot (the in process Chromium, sharp, the export merge at 834.6 MiB, and the `/tmp` overlay the store is built on), because a Worker isolate has 128 MB of memory, no process spawning, no N-API, no threads and no file system that outlives the request; the browser work has a Cloudflare product (Browser Run, formerly the browser rendering URLs) with a 60 s session that `keep_alive` extends to 10 minutes and a 10 minute per day free allowance, but WebGL, `page.pdf` under the binding and the pinned Chromium 147 are not confirmed by any page read today; and a split has one trap that is not optional: no cookie of `www.turboslide.com` reaches a `workers.dev` host, so the signed ticket of `read-channel.md` section 2 is the only way the channel learns who the tab is.

## 5. The split: the app on Vercel, the channel and the store on Cloudflare

The topology: pages, loaders, server functions, exports and the agent routes on `www.turboslide.com` (Vercel); the room object and its Worker on `<name>.<account>.workers.dev`; the store on R2 with a Worker serving its public prefixes on another `workers.dev` host or the same one. `read-channel.md` section 2 item 1 and `read-auth.md` section 3 reached the cookie conclusion from the tree; this note confirms it from the pages and adds the CORS, WebSocket and preview rows.

### 5.1 Cookies do not cross

- The anonymous identity cookie is `__Host-ts_id`, `Secure; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Path=/` (`apps/studio/src/server/auth/session.ts` 3, 184; `read-auth.md` 3.1); better-auth's cookies are `sameSite: 'lax'` (`apps/studio/src/server/write.ts` 390; `auth/middleware.ts` 40; `read-auth.md` 3.2). MDN's `Set-Cookie` page: a `Lax` cookie is sent "only for requests originating from the same site that set the cookie, and for cross-site requests that meet both of the following criteria", a top level navigation and a safe method, and that "would exclude, for example, requests made using the `fetch()` API"; `Domain` "cannot be a public suffix", and `workers.dev` is on the public suffix list (`read-auth.md` 3 item 4, fetched 2026-10-01). A `__Host-` cookie carries no `Domain` by its prefix's definition.
- Consequence: no request the editor makes to the Worker (an `EventSource`, a `fetch` of `/ops` or `/presence`, a WebSocket upgrade) carries the tab's identity. The Worker cannot run `requestIdentity` (`room.ts` 352 to 439 at `HEAD`). The signed ticket minted by a Vercel route and carried to the object (`read-channel.md` 2 item 1) is therefore the design, not an option. A `SameSite=None; Secure` cookie set by the Worker for its own host would work for its own host alone and is subject to "Third-party cookie policies may prevent third-party cookies being sent in requests" (MDN CORS); the ticket avoids the question.

### 5.2 CORS on the stream and the two POSTs

- `EventSource` takes `withCredentials`, "A boolean value, defaulting to `false`, indicating if CORS should be set to `include` credentials" (MDN). Without credentials the Worker answers `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for the page's origin and the ticket rides the URL or a header; with credentials "The server must not specify the `*` wildcard for the `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` response-header value, but must instead specify an explicit origin", and the same for `Allow-Headers` and `Allow-Methods`, and the browser will "reject any response that does not have the `Access-Control-Allow-Credentials` header set to `true`" (MDN CORS). A JSON `POST` to `/ops` or `/presence` across origins is preflighted; the Worker answers its own `OPTIONS`.
- The page's CSP names `connect-src 'self'${connect}` (`apps/studio/src/server/headers.ts` 236), with the store hosts added through the env (154 to 156). The Worker's origin, and the `wss:` scheme for a WebSocket, must join `connect`; the policy is report only today (`TURBOSLIDE_CSP=report` default, `docs/hosting.md` 1032), so an omission reports rather than blocks until the enforce flip.
- The CSRF middleware validates `Sec-Fetch-Site`, `Origin` or `Referer` on the stream, ops and presence routes (`apps/studio/src/start.ts` 19 to 22, the `csrfFilter` at 43); on the Worker this check is the upgrade handler's own, with the allowed origins list (`read-channel.md` 2 item 1), and the list must include every origin a tab may be served from, including the pipeline's preview hosts `https://turboslide-<hash>-kl01s-projects.vercel.app` or the gate's previews are refused by the Worker. A per deployment origin claim in the ticket is the alternative to a pattern.

### 5.3 WebSocket authentication

- The browser's constructor takes `url` and `protocols` and nothing else (MDN `WebSocket()`): no `Authorization` header, and no cookie crosses (5.1). The ticket therefore rides the URL's query, the first frame, or the `Sec-WebSocket-Protocol` value the `protocols` parameter sends. A query string lands in logs on both hosts (Workers "Log data per request" 256 KB; the app's one line per request); the first frame or the subprotocol keeps it out of URLs. The object verifies the HMAC and admits or closes; the 60 s access recheck of the route (`decks.$deckId.stream.ts` 70) becomes the ticket's expiry plus a refresh the client fetches from the Vercel route, as `read-channel.md` 2 item 1 describes.
- The function to the object: a Vercel function's request carries `x-vercel-oidc-token`, a token "reuses... for up to 90 minutes" with "a Time to Live (TTL) of two hours", issued by `https://oidc.vercel.com/<team>` in Team issuer mode (Vercel OIDC page 2026-08-04). The Worker can verify it against the issuer's keys, or the two sides share `TURBOSLIDE_TOKEN` as the bearer the agent routes already use (`docs/hosting.md` 366). The keys endpoint page (`/docs/oidc/reference`) was not read; this note does not state its URL.

### 5.4 The OIDC token on previews, and the Worker's previews

- Browser on a protected preview to the Worker: no Vercel protection is in the way, because the Worker is not a Vercel origin; the probes' `x-vercel-trusted-oidc-idp-token` header (`scripts/hosted-smoke.mjs` 275 to 281; `docs/FOCUS.md` 260) is for the Vercel origin and the Worker never sees it. Nothing to do.
- The Worker to a protected preview (the object calling a Vercel route for a version record or an access recheck): Vercel Authentication refuses it. The bypass methods on all plans are "Protection Bypass for Automation" with the header `x-vercel-protection-bypass: your-secret` or the query parameter, the "OPTIONS Allowlist" for preflights ("browsers do not send authentication on preflight requests"), "Deployment Protection Exceptions" for named preview domains, and "Shareable Links" (Vercel bypass page 2026-09-15). The Worker would hold the automation secret for preview calls; production needs none. The OPTIONS allowlist matters only if a browser on another origin calls the Vercel preview, which the split does not do.
- The Worker's own previews: a Version URL is `<version-prefix>-<worker-name>.<subdomain>.workers.dev` from `wrangler versions upload`; "When enabled, Version URLs are publicly available. To require visitors to sign in, use Cloudflare Access."; "You cannot configure Version URLs to run on a subdomain other than `workers.dev`"; Version URLs are "Not generated for Durable Objects" (previews page 2026-09-22). So the gate's preview step has no protection to pass on the Worker side, and the object code has no version URL at all: a Durable Object runs one version at a time ("Gradual deployments work differently for Durable Objects because only one version of each Durable Object can run at a time", gradual deployments page 2026-07-03). The guard's shape of `FOCUS.md` 6.2 (preview green, then production, then production green or roll back) becomes: upload a version, check its URL for the stateless routes, deploy the version, check the objects on production, roll back on red. If a protected pre production URL is wanted, Access service tokens are the machine credential: "Cloudflare Access will generate service tokens that consist of a Client ID and a Client Secret", sent as `CF-Access-Client-Id` and `CF-Access-Client-Secret`, and "This is the only time Cloudflare Access will display the Client Secret" (service tokens page 2026-09-22); the account limits page allows 500 applications and 50 service tokens and states no Free seat count (section 0).
- Two deploy surfaces: the ticket secret, the protocol version and the allowed origins must agree between the Vercel deployment and the Worker version in service at the same moment; `TRUSTED_SOURCES_ENVIRONMENT_MISMATCH` on a staged production target (`gt-follow.sh` 6 to 9, T4 2.12) is unchanged by the split.

### 5.5 What the split does not trap

- The store's public files read by `<img>` and `fetch` from the app's origin: these are plain cross origin GETs the browser already makes to the Blob host today (`headers.ts` 154 to 156 names it in `img-src` and `connect-src`), so an R2 host is one more name in the same two directives, with `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy` set by the serving Worker as `read-store.md` 3 item 3 describes.
- The agent surface and MCP: bearer surfaces with no cookie (`docs/security.md` 291), so an agent's write to the Vercel route and the route's forward to the object carry bearers end to end.
