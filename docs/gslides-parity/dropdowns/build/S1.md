Found in the hand drive and fixed in this push (each blocked a site of the push):

- The person row's role, expiry, removal and transfer named the person by a bare string, and the
  share actions' schema (`grantWhoSchema`) refuses it: "share.setRole: invalid input at /who". This
  was on production: a person's role, expiry, removal or ownership transfer from the Share dialog
  failed.
  `Share.tsx` now sends `{ principalId }` or `{ email }` (`grantWhoOf`), and a unit test pins the
  payload through the row's dropdown for an address and for a principal.
- With Anyone with the link on and Settings open, the people list shrank to 1 px in the dialog
  body's flex column, so no person row could be reached. `.ts-share-list` takes `flex-shrink: 0`
  and the body scrolls instead.
- Add expiration drew its field as a fifth grid item that wrapped into the 24 px chip column with
  its placeholder in no room. The role and expiry fields both sit in the row's fourth cell, the
  expiry field over the role field while it is open.
