### 1.2 Presenter view (SPEC 9.3; MILESTONES B6 item 3)

`apps/studio/src/routes/present.$deckId.tsx` replaces the integrator's shell. It loads the document
through `readEditorDeck` (the editor's read: the speaker notes and the skip flags are there, which
the audience payload of `/deck` leaves out on purpose), renders the unskipped slides once with
`renderSlide` for the console's live frames, and mounts
`packages/viewer/src/present/PresenterConsole.tsx`: the timer top left with Pause (Resume) and
Reset, the clock, the connection status, the current slide between Previous and Next with the
slide list dropdown under it, the previous and next slides at 0.3 of the current frame, the
Speaker notes tab with the notes body and the plus and minus text size buttons (14 to 28 px in
steps of 2, stored per browser), "No speaker notes for this slide", and the Audience tools tab
present, `aria-disabled`, with the stub tooltip. Arrow keys, Space, the page keys, Home, End and a
number then Enter move the show from the console.

The console is a window API owner (`presenter`, actions `view.goto` and `view.present`) and
attaches to the studio's session registry, so `deck_goto_slide` over /mcp lands here when this is
the attached page and the audience window follows over the channel. `?screen=1`, the address
`docs/spec/SPEC.md` 6.10 gave the audience form, redirects to `/deck/:id?present=1`.

### 1.3 The channel (SPEC 9.1 item 1; 9.3)

`packages/viewer/src/present/presentSync.ts`: `BroadcastChannel('turboslide:<deckId>')` with
`localStorage` (`turboslide:present:<deckId>`, storage events) as the fallback; the environment is
injectable, so the protocol is unit tested with fakes. Messages: `hello`, `state` (the audience's
slide, index, total, blank, laser), `goto`, `present` (on or off), `bye`, each in an envelope with
the protocol version, the sender id, a clock and a sequence number; a window never receives its
own. The audience posts its state on mount, on hello and on every change and follows `goto`; the
console says hello, follows the audience's moves and posts `goto` for its own; `view.goto` in
either window therefore moves both. Closing the presenter window leaves the show running; leaving
the show tells the console ("No slideshow window is open").

### 1.4 The Slideshow button's handlers (SPEC 9.1; MILESTONES B6 item 1)

`apps/studio/src/components/presentActions.ts`: `PresentHost` (deck id, the play list's first
slide, `goto`, `present`), `startSlideshow(host, { from, fullscreen })` (the main part: from the
current slide, full screen when the browser allows), `presentFromBeginning(host)` (Cmd+Shift+Enter),
`openPresenterView(deckId)` and `presenterView(host)` (the second window, then this tab into the
show), `presentClientHandlers(host)` keyed as the menu model names them (`presenterView`,
`presentFromBeginning`), `presenterPath`, `audiencePath`, `presenterWindowName`,
`requestPresentFullscreen`, `exitPresentFullscreen`. B3's TitleRow and the integrator bind them
(section 4, request 1).

## 3. Decisions taken where the SPEC was silent, and deviations

1. **The surround is the panel ink.** SPEC 9.2 says "the sheet alone on an ink surround". `--pt-ink`
   is light in the dark theme, so the surround reads `--pt-panel-ink` (#101010, the same in both
   themes): a light sheet sits on Google's black, a dark sheet reads as one field with it.
2. **The toolbar draws eight controls.** Google's compact bar is Previous, the number, Next and
   Options (R04 A3); the round's plan names the laser pointer, the Captions stub, full screen and
   Exit as toolbar controls too, so they are buttons after a rule, and the Options menu still lists
   them as Google's does.
3. **The Options menu items are built in `Slideshow.tsx`**, not in B3's `menus/model.ts` (the
   model has no present mode menu). Their ids are `present.options.*`; `data-menu-item` carries them
   so the parity audit can read them if it grows a present mode pass.
4. **The console reads the document, not the audience payload.** `getDeck` will drop notes and
   skipped slides for `/deck` and `/embed` (B5, SPEC 7.2.1); the console needs both facts, so it
   uses `readEditorDeck` and renders client side, the way the editor does. `ViewerSlide` has no
   `skip` field; `playList` reads one if a payload ever carries it (`'skip' in slide`), so the
   audience surface is right whether the loader drops the slides or flags them.
5. **Present mode from the P key on the view route asks for no full screen**; the Slideshow button
   and Start from beginning do (SPEC 9.1). The reading surface keeps its behaviour.
6. **Enter advances and Backspace goes back** (SPEC 9.2 adopts the PowerPoint convention R04 leaves
   unverified); a focused control keeps Enter and Space for itself.
7. **Auto-play, the pen and the two downloads inside the show are Later stubs** with the shared
   formula; Captions preferences is omitted; Print is `window.print()`.
8. **The snackbar sits above the toolbar in a show** (a scoped override in `Slideshow.css`): the
   bottom bar it usually clears is not on screen.
9. **`view.present` on the presenter owner** posts `present` to the audience window (off leaves the
   show; on is a no-op unless an audience is presenting) and answers the declared view state.
10. **The presenter window** opens with `popup=yes,width=1180,height=760` under one name per deck,
    so a second Presenter view focuses the first.
11. **No new action for the blank slide, the laser or full screen** (SPEC 7.5 lists none); they are
    queryable through `describe().state` and drivable by the keys and the menu. A later round can
    widen `view.present`'s input additively (`{ on, blank?, laser?, fullscreen? }`); recorded for
    Kevin under SPEC 15.

## 4. Requests to the integrator and the other builders

1. **Integrator, `apps/studio/src/routes/edit.$deckId.tsx`.** In `EditorStage`, when
   `shell.present`, mount the Slideshow beside the Stage:

   ```tsx
   <Slideshow
     deckId={snap.deckId}
     slides={viewerDeck.slides.filter((s) => snap.document.slides[s.id]?.skip !== true)}
     activeId={shell.active}
     theme={theme}
     onGoto={shell.select}
     onExit={() => shell.setPresent(false)}
     say={shell.say}
   />
   ```

   and map the Stage's `onStep` while presenting to the play list (`currentPlayIndex`,
   `stepPlayIndex` from `@turboslide/viewer/present/presentModel`, as `DeckViewer.tsx` does). Bind
   the TitleRow's Slideshow arrow to `presentClientHandlers(host)` and its main part to
   `startSlideshow(host)` with a `PresentHost` over the controller (`firstSlideId` is the first
   unskipped slide). Optionally report the show's facts in the editor's `describe().state` through
   `onState`.

2. **B3, `packages/chrome/src/ViewerShell.tsx`.** The `fullscreenchange` listener ends present mode
   whenever full screen ends. In a show, "Exit full screen" (Cmd+Shift+F, F11, the Options item and
   the toolbar button) must keep presenting in the window (SPEC 9.2, R04 A3); only Esc and Exit
   stop the show. Proposed: on fullscreen exit, call `setPresent(false)` only when no
   `.ts-slideshow` is mounted (or drop the coupling, since the show's Esc leaves both).
3. **B3, `packages/chrome/src/menus/strings.ts`.** `PRESENT` holds eight words; the present
   components draw more (Previous, Next, Options, Turn off the laser pointer, Captions, Enter and
   exit full screen, Auto-play, Turn on the pen, More, Download as PDF, Download as PPTX, Print,
   Keyboard shortcuts, Resume, the connection sentences). They live in
   `packages/viewer/src/present/strings.ts` because the viewer cannot import the chrome; adding the
   same entries to `PRESENT` puts them under the default view words test.
4. **B3, `packages/chrome/src/icons.tsx` and the theme sprite.** `ellipsis-horizontal`,
   `cursor-arrow-rays`, `pause`, `play` and `arrow-path` would let the Options button, the laser
   button and the console's timer buttons drop their text glyph fallbacks (`PresentIcon` takes them
   through the `icons` prop; nothing else changes).
5. **B5, `apps/studio/src/server/decks.ts`.** No change needed for `/present` (it no longer calls
   `getDeck`); the audience payload of `/deck` and `/embed` dropping skipped slides and notes is
   what SPEC 7.2.1 asks and what `playList` expects. If B5 prefers a flag over dropping, B4's
   `ViewerSlide` needs `skip?: true` in `packages/viewer/src/model.ts`; `playList` already reads
   it. `viewer.spec.ts` (mine) can then gain SPEC 14.3's "the payloads carry no notes and no
   skipped slide" assertion; it is not there yet because it would fail on today's payload.
6. **B5, `apps/studio/src/routes/new.tsx`.** During the round the file imported
   `@turboslide/store/templates` in client code; `routeTree.gen.ts` imports every route statically,
   `templates.ts` reaches `store/lease.ts`, and its `node:fs` named import throws in Vite's browser
   shim at module evaluation, so no page hydrated in dev (measured 03:00 to 03:08 on the 4436 server:
   "Module node:fs has been externalized for browser compatibility. Cannot access node:fs.existsSync
   in client code" at `packages/store/src/lease.ts:1`). The import was gone by 03:14 and the plain
   config passed; the rule to keep is that a route module imports no store, export or headless module
   outside `import type`.
7. **Integrator.** `pnpm generate:contracts` needs no run for B6 (no action table change). The
   `routeTree.gen.ts` is regenerated by the dev server and by check step 2; nothing to do.
8. **Verifier.** `hosted-smoke.mjs` should ask `/present/<id>` for 200 with a body holding
   `ts-presenter` once the client boots (the route is `ssr: false`, like `/edit`), and `/deck/<id>?present=1`
   for the `ts-slideshow` root after hydration.

## 5. What was cut or is unproven

- Full screen: `requestFullscreen` runs on the button, Cmd+Shift+F, F11 and the Options item;
  headless Chromium cannot prove the change of state, and request 2 stands until B3 acts.
- Print opens `window.print()`; not exercised by the spec.
- The `localStorage` fallback of the channel is proven against fakes in `present-sync.test.ts`;
  every browser the studio targets has `BroadcastChannel`, so the real fallback path was not seen.
- The editor's show (request 1) is unmounted until the integrator wires it; the audience surface
  was proven on `/deck/:id?present=1`, the console on `/present/:id`, and both together.
- The toolbar's 2 s fade is a timer; the spec asserts appearance on hover, not the fade.
- Reduced motion reads the `--pt-dur-*` tokens; not measured.
- Safari and Firefox were not opened.
- The presenting group in B3's ShortcutsDialog lists Google's rows from `keys.ts`; the show's own
  card lists the same rows plus Space, Enter, the page keys and Backspace, which `keys.ts` does not
  carry as alternates (R04 unverified 9).
