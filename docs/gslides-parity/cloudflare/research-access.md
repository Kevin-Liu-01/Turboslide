R2 free tier: 10 GB-month a month, 1 million Class A and 10 million Class B operations a month, egress free (https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/, last updated Oct 1, 2026), but R2 needs "an R2 subscription": "Complete the checkout flow to add an R2 subscription to your account" (https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/get-started/, last updated Apr 21, 2026), and "A primary payment method is required to purchase Cloudflare products and services" (https://developers.cloudflare.com/billing/create-billing-profile/, last updated May 29, 2026).
