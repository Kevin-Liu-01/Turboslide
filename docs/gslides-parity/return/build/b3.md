#### 1.1 The guide drag at a crossing (`arrange.guides.drag`, flaky severity 2 in RETURN.md)

- The audit's reading (audit-surface row 45): the guide moves when the press rests before the pointer travels and stays when the pointer moves at once; RETURN.md names the fix "start the guide drag on the first move after a press, whatever the timing".
- Measured here (`guide-probe.mjs`, run of 01:01Z): the timing is not the mechanism. `View > Guides > Add vertical guide` lands at x 800 and `Add horizontal guide` at y 450 (`GUIDE_CENTRE`), so the two cross at the sheet centre, which is the midpoint of both lines; `DeckGuides.tsx` draws the x guides first and the y guides after, so at the crossing the horizontal guide is on top and takes the press (`elementFromPoint` at the vertical guide's centre answers `guide.y.450`; the capture listener saw `down on guide.y.450`). The drag then moves the horizontal guide horizontally: its position does not change, `onGuideDown`'s `up` sees `last === at` and writes nothing. The audit's readout "3.75 in" during the fast drag is y 450 at `PX_PER_INCH` 120 (`rulers-model.ts`), not x 600 (which reads "5.00 in"); the slow run 3 that "worked" pressed where the vertical guide was on top (its evidence says `elementFromPoint guide.x.800`). Away from the crossing (a press at sheet y 200), with no horizontal guide, or with one guide alone, the fast drag of 14 steps and no hold landed the guide every time (probe rows b, d, e). The `deck.guides` move write itself lands (row a, through the window API).
- Change, `Editor.tsx` `onGuideDown` with `stage-rules.ts` `crossingGuide` and `guideForTravel`: at the press the guide on the other axis under the pointer is looked up within half the 7 px hit strip at the stage's scale; when one crosses there, the drag waits for `GUIDE_PICK_PX` (3 CSS px) of travel and moves the guide that lies across the dominant travel (a horizontal travel moves the vertical guide, a vertical travel the horizontal one; the readout appears once the guide is picked); when none crosses, the press drags the pressed guide at once as before. A click at a crossing writes nothing.
- Test: `stage-rules.test.ts` (the crossing found under the audit's setup on either guide, none away from it or with no guide on the other axis, the nearer of two, the pick threshold, both travel directions, the diagonal tie).
- Measured after the change (`mech-probe.mjs` run of 01:13Z): six drags of the vertical guide at 800 pressed at its centre (the crossing with y 450, `under press guide.y.450`), three fast (14 steps, no hold) and three slow (300 ms hold, 20 paced steps), 800 to 600 and back; every one landed (`guides after guide.x.400,guide.x.600,guide.y.450`, `deck.info {"x":[400,600],"y":[450]}`, the readout "5.00 in" at 600 and "6.67 in" at 800). Before the change the same six drags stayed at 800 (runs of 00:57Z and 01:08Z).
- Rows served: `arrange.guides.drag`. For B4's step: the press must land at the guide's centre, the crossing, to prove this; a press at sheet y 200 passed before the fix too.
