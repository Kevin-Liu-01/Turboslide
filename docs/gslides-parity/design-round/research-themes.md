# Themes research

The themes researcher of the design round, 2026-10-05. Kevin's request (item 2 of the round): "for themes, it shouldnt all just reset to gt, the general translation stuff should just a theme you can select among other themes." This file reads how themes work today, lists every way a deck ends up on the GT theme without the person choosing it, describes Google Slides' theme picker from public pages, and proposes a theme library, its default, its picker, its relation to the brand kit and the migration of existing decks.

## 1. Summary

Turboslide has one theme. `THEMES` is `['gt-ink-paper']` (`packages/schema/src/deck.ts` 30 to 32), every deck stores `theme: 'gt-ink-paper'`, and every sheet draws GT's `sheet.css`. What looks like a choice of theme is the brand kit (`deck.brand`), a record of overrides on top of GT's sheet; a deck without a record, or with a record that is silent about a slot, draws GT's part for that slot. The Blank template avoids the GT parts only by carrying a kit that turns each one off (`decks/templates/blank/deck.json` 5 to 21). So every action that removes kit fields (Reset, a per field reset, an agent's `brand.reset`) brings the GT mark, the GT wordmark band, the counter and the rails, rules and crosses back, and the button says so: "Reset to General Translation". Two of Google's eleven layouts (Section header, Caption) and the Closing layout put GT's own dithered materials into a Blank deck, and Closing draws the GT mark whatever the kit says. The proposal makes the theme a real field with a library of ten themes (nine in Inter), makes Simple the theme of every new deck and of Blank, moves every GT part into the General Translation theme, and makes Reset return to the deck's theme. The migration maps each existing deck to the theme whose drawing equals what it shows today, so no deck changes on screen.

## 5. Google Slides' theme picker

From Google's help page "Use a Template or change the theme, background, or layout" (support.google.com/docs/answer/1705254, read 2026-10-05) and the round one research `docs/gslides-parity/research/03-home-themes-layouts-io.md` (rows cited by line):

- A theme is "a preset group of colors, fonts, background, and layouts" (help page). In the data model it is one master, its layouts, a colour scheme of twelve slots (Text and background 1 to 4, Accent 1 to 6, Link, followed link) and two fonts (research 81, 86).
- A new presentation opens in Simple Light, one Title slide with "Click to add title" and "Click to add subtitle" (research 64, 65).
- Slide > Change theme, or the toolbar Theme button, opens a right panel titled Themes: large thumbnails of each theme with its name under it. A click applies the theme to the whole presentation. "In this presentation" at the top lists the themes the file holds (research 126 to 129).
- Google ships 23 built in themes (TechRepublic's 2021 count, research 130). Names seen on public pages: Simple Light (the default), Material, Modern, Momentum and Focus (research 130), and Simple Dark, Swiss, Spearmint, Coral and Beach Day (secondary pages, section 9). The full list needs a signed in account and was not read. The range runs from plain white and plain dark, through coloured grounds (Spearmint, Coral, Beach Day), to editorial and geometric looks (Swiss, Material, Focus).
- Import theme sits at the bottom right of the panel: pick a presentation from Drive or upload a file (PowerPoint too), pick one of its themes, then Import theme (help page; research 131, 135 to 140).
- Slide > Edit theme (View > Theme builder in BrightCarbon's guide) opens the theme builder: the master and its layouts; a change to the master reaches every slide. Edit theme > Colors edits the twelve slots by preset, picker or hex (help page).
- Every built in theme has the same eleven layouts (research 93). A presentation holds up to 5 themes and a theme up to 100 layouts (research 89). Workspace admins can set organisation templates and themes that appear for their users (research 55).

What Google does that Turboslide does not: more than one theme to pick, a default that is a plain theme with no logo, thumbnails of each theme, Import theme, and a Reset that has nothing to return to except the theme the deck has.

### 6.2 The default theme

- `/new`, the Blank card and `deck.create { from: 'blank' }` start in Simple, light. Blank's `deck.json` names `theme: 'simple'` and carries no kit; Simple itself draws no logo, no wordmark, no counter and no frame.
- `blankDeckDocument` (`packages/store/src/templates.ts` 956 to 981) writes `theme: 'simple'` and `defaults.appearance: 'light'` (closes G8).
- `deckAppearance` falls back to the theme's default appearance (Simple: light; General Translation: dark) instead of dark for every deck (`packages/schema/src/deck.ts` 679 to 681; closes G7).
- `turboslide deck create` without `--from` uses the deployment default that `/new` uses (`readDefaultTemplateId`), and the `deck.create` example reads `from: 'blank'` (closes G9, G10).
- The deployment default template still decides what a new presentation contains (Use for new presentations); on General Translation's deployment an organisation template in the General Translation theme can be that default if Kevin wants it (Q4).

### 6.3 The picker

Picture 12 is the proposed panel. One panel, opened by Slide > Change theme and the toolbar Theme button as today, titled Theme:

1. Appearance: a two button switch, Light and Dark, writing `/defaults/appearance` as today (`panel.brand.appearance.light`, `.dark` keep their ids).
2. In this presentation: one tile, the deck's theme drawn with the deck's own first title slide and first body slide, the theme's name, and a row "Brand kit: none" or "Brand kit: 3 colors, logo" with an Edit Kit button that opens the kit sections below.
3. Themes: one tile per library theme. A tile is two live clones side by side, 140 by 79 px each (the deck's first title slide and first body slide rendered in that theme and the current appearance), the name under them. The current theme draws one 2 px ink border, the same rule as the layout tiles (`LayoutGrid.css`). A click is one commit `deck.set /theme` with the history label "Theme: Swiss", so Cmd+Z takes it back in one step and Version history lists it. Arrow keys move between tiles (a radio group), Enter applies. Every tile carries the Tooltip primitive with its name, one sentence ("Applies Swiss to every slide; your content stays") and no key.
4. Import Theme (button at the foot): picks another presentation through the Import slides dialog's first step and copies its theme and its kit into this deck as one commit.
5. The kit sections of today (Logo, Colors, Fonts, Footer, Slide numbers, Frame, Words that never translate) follow under "Brand kit", collapsed until Edit Kit or a kit field is set.
6. Reset at the foot reads "Reset to <theme name>" ("Reset to Simple") and removes the kit only; the theme stays.

The tiles render lazily when the panel opens: ten themes, two clones each, through the renderer the panel already receives (`ThemesPanel.tsx` 218 to 228 renders two today). `SlideRenderer` takes the theme id as a third argument for this.

### 6.5 The brand kit over a theme

A kit is a customer's colours, faces and logo over any theme. The theme gives every value a base; the kit overrides the fields it names; an absent kit field reads the theme's value. In code: `themeCss(deck)` merges the theme record under the kit and stays empty for General Translation without a kit (so every existing gate keeps its pixels); `frameBandOf`, `titleMarkSlot`, `deckCounter` and the frame rules read the theme's defaults where they read GT's today (`stage.ts` 91 to 101 and 147 to 148, `slide.ts` 303 to 306 and 443 to 445, `deck.ts` 688 to 696, `theme-css.ts` 200 to 210). With that one change, G1 to G6 stop reaching GT: Reset returns to the theme, a per field reset returns that field to the theme, Closing draws the kit's logo slot, and "Use the default logo" becomes "Use the theme's logo", shown only on a theme that has one. The deployment's default kit (`readDefaultKit`) stops naming the Reset target; an organisation's look is an organisation template (a theme, a kit and slides), listed under Your organisation in the gallery and pickable through Import Theme.

### 6.6 Migration of existing decks

A normalization in `validateDeck`, the way layout defaults are filled today (SPEC 4.4), reported as a `migrated` issue at severity 1 and written on the deck's next save; no `schemaVersion` bump. The rule keeps what each deck shows:

1. A deck whose kit draws the GT title mark or the GT wordmark band (no kit, an empty kit, or a kit silent about either slot) keeps `gt-ink-paper`, shown as General Translation. This covers the GT brand deck, decks made from it, decks reset by G1 to G3, and decks that chose the default logo.
2. A deck whose kit turns both off (Blank's record, and every deck made from Blank since it shipped) becomes `simple`; the kit fields equal to Simple's defaults (`mark: none`, `footer.logo: none`, `counter.show: false`, the three frame toggles false) are dropped, and every other field stays.
3. A deck without `defaults.appearance` gets the appearance it draws today, written explicitly (dark), so the new theme default in 6.2 changes nothing for it.

Because Simple's values equal today's sheet values, no deck changes on screen under any of the three rules. The count of decks under each rule on production was not read: a read of every deck is a sweep, which this round does not run.

### 6.7 Layouts and starter pictures

- The four materials belong to General Translation. Simple and the other themes carry none. Caption without a picture keeps the grid's existing "Add a picture first" state (`layouts.ts` 196 to 217).
- The disclosure row "GT layouts" reads "More layouts" (`layouts.ts` 60).
- Closing draws the kit's logo slot (`slide.ts` 303 to 306), so a deck with no logo shows none.
