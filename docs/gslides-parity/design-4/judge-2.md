## 2. Reasoning per proposal

### 2.1 Proposal 1, the dither is the identity

The mark. A square with a window at columns 1 to 4 and rows 4 to 6, the body a Bayer density field from 48 px. At 512 px it is the strongest picture of the three: it is a GT opener and it is the pipeline. At 16, 24 and 32 px it is a solid square with a lower left notch, and there it says nothing: in `previews/favicon-tab-strips.png` the titanium ICO reads as a grey square with a white rectangle, which is the "rectangle in a rectangle" silhouette report 01 6.3 item 12 asks the mark to avoid (offset to a corner, but the reading is the same at a glance). The wordmark lockup (`previews/wordmark-paper-3x.png`) sets a 48 px cellular mark beside 66 px letters and the mark reads as a speckled square next to crisp type; the proposal's own test found 32 px reads as noise and moved the threshold to 48, and at 48 it is still borderline. Distinctiveness 6. Legibility at 16 px 7: two colours, crisp, no isolated pixel, but the form has no meaning at that size.

Fit with Prototemplate 9. No accent, every `--pt-` token untouched, ten `--ts-` tokens in `styles.css` (where report 01 6.6 item 24 puts them), the plate as the ground showing through with no rule, Inter 500 with `cv11` and `ss01`, titanium never under 19 px, the line law on every seam, dark and light as negative twins. The hero is a GT opener at page scale and the plate cut from the picture follows the deck's own plate grammar, so no lit cell can intrude by construction.

Technical and agent signal 8. The generator imports `bayer8` from `packages/effects/src/bayer.ts`; the progress bar's leading edge is `rampInk`; the anonymous author chips are keyed by a hash of the author id and the mock names `agent:claude` and `agent:codex` beside `kevin`, which is the right picture of who edits a deck here; the CLI banner prints `describeBackends().selected` and the mock honestly shows `effects backend: typescript`. The "Why it is fast" band names a file under every number and the closing sentence names the slow things with report 04's numbers.

Home page 8. The hero is the best picture of the three and the plate cutout is exactly the deck. Two problems. First, the three frames cut every four seconds: a hard cut between two 1 bit images every four seconds under the sentence a visitor is reading is motion outside the shell's vocabulary (the six durations move opacity and transform over 120 to 220 ms); it will read as a flip book, and it is the kind of thing I said no to for the Locadex mark. Buildability 8. `mark.mjs` and `build-assets.mjs` are real generators, the ICO decodes at zero differing pixels, the twins are reproducible through `turboslide material capture` with the recorded recipe (`assets/hero.recipe.json`, the sidecar shape `capture.ts` writes). ### 2.2 Proposal 2, the shader is the identity

The wide frame leaves 4 of 16 rows empty above and below. Distinctiveness 7, legibility 7.

The mark. No accent (argued well: the one solid button is already the accent; GT blue belongs to the customer's slide), the tile's frame is the line law's frame role, the tokens in `styles.css`, nothing animates, `scroll-behavior: auto` explicit. The frame makes three colours at 16 px against report 01 acceptance 5, owned as decision 2.

Technical and agent signal 7. `hero-report.json` records the recipe, the renderer, the backend (`typescript`, honestly) and the stage time (179.6 ms for the whole stage set, correctly distinguished from the 52 ms screen alone in `docs/native.md`); the "For agents" band is complete; the contrast table cites WCAG 2.2 1.4.3 and 1.4.11 with the logotype exemption. Home page 7. The most complete honest numbers of the three ("399 ms for a one slide presentation and 723 ms for the 85 slide GT deck, warm"), the fifteen shots, the same nine sections. Against it: the hero twin from `paper:mesh-gradient` is a sparse dot field in the corners rather than a picture, so the hero reads as a dark page with type; the mock's nav "New Presentation" button shows a link underline (a styling leak, trivial); and a still only hero under a directive that asks for shaders in the branding under delivers. Buildability 8. The smallest asset budget (one 3.7 KB PNG per theme, reused for the empty states), the ICO verified, the build script real. The title row at 20 px and the app bar at 24 px need hand hinting because the 16 unit grid lands on 1.25 and 1.5 px units; the mock shows the unhinted worst case, which is the honest thing to show and also the extra work.

## 4. Rejections

1. Proposal 2's accent (`--ts-accent #2f5ce0` on the live hero and the pipeline figure). A 640 px band of GT blue becomes the page's colour rather than a controlled edge; the identity is paper and ink.
2. Proposal 2's hard coded hero caption. The line "backend native (crates/turboslide-native)" is accepted only as a value the build writes from `dither-record.json`; a Vercel build produces `typescript` until performance plan 3.9 lands, and the page may not say otherwise.
3. Proposal 2's Heroicons inside the three hero buttons. The call to action grammar is Title Case text; drop the glyphs.
4. Proposal 2's monospace density. Keep the source line under each number and the command boxes; drop the two column mono caption under the hero into one sentence in `--pt-ink-2`, so mono stays an instrument.
5. Proposal 1's three frame cut every four seconds. One still twin under reduced motion, the live mount otherwise; no cycling.
6. Proposal 1's titanium favicon base. 2.48:1 on Chrome's light inactive strip by its own table; the opaque plate of proposal 2 reads on both strips and in Safari.
7. Proposal 1's sprite claim. `i-bars-3-bottom-left`, `i-table-cells` and `i-check-badge` are in `sprite-ids.json`; report 05's assignment stands.
8. Proposal 3's sentence as the `h1` with a trailing period. The name or the wordmark is the heading; the sentence is a paragraph.
9. Proposal 3's still only hero as the default. The live mount after first paint, in monochrome, is what directive (b) asks for; the still is the reduced motion, hidden tab, narrow viewport and no WebGL2 form.
10. Proposal 1's `apps/cli/src/commands/version.ts` as the banner's file. That module is the `version save|list|restore` command; the banner gets its own module.

## 5. The performance plan

I verified every line citation the plan makes against the tree today and all of them hold: `blob-store.ts:1105-1115` (`list` with `eachLimit(ids, 4, sync(true))`), `blob-store.ts:691-740` (head, the leases pull, `putSnapshot`, `putDocument('deck.json', synced)`, the slide puts and dels, the record put), `blob-store.ts:339` (`pollMs` default 3000) and `859` (`watch`), `decks.index.tsx:54-57` (the loader awaits `listDecks` and `getServerHealth`), `decks.trash.tsx:54-56` (`refresh` is `router.invalidate()`) awaited at 63 and 88, `edit.$deckId.tsx:2210` (`warmThumbs` calling `warmThumbnails`), `new.tsx:13` (`import * as editRoute from './edit.$deckId'`), `present.$deckId.tsx:13` (`renderSlide` at module level), `sessions.ts:197-199` (`poll` answers `[]` at once for an unknown id), `useStudioSession.ts:80-86` (the loop with no delay after an empty answer), `InlineText.tsx:62` (`TEXT_BURST_MS = 400`), `Thumb.tsx:111-115` (the clone to static swap), `thumbs.ts:229-244` (`getThumbnail`), `Sidebar.tsx:1283` (one `Thumb` per card), `TitleRow.tsx:265-274` (the plain `<a href>`), `contracts.ts` reading `repoRoot()`, `.gitignore` lines 22 to 25, `router.tsx` (`defaultPreload: 'intent'`, `defaultPreloadStaleTime: 0`). `perf-budget.mjs` is 1,115 lines, carries `BUDGETS.local` and `BUDGETS.deployment`, the floor logic for the fps row, the platform GPU flags, the `chromium-1217` path and the Trusted Sources header from the environment, as section 9 says.

Findings:

1. The diagnosis is right and matches the baseline: the canvas paints in 4 to 21 ms and the filmstrip scrolls at 120 fps, so the plan spends nothing there and everything on the list call, the thumbnails, the shared graph and the write path. The production run's `edit->decks` at 4,481 ms as a document load confirms that the title row's mark as a router `Link` is the cheapest large win, and every brand proposal already asks for it.
2. Section 4's table (today, after, never) is the contract the home page copy needs. All three proposals' speed bands stay inside the "today" column and every one says the hosted studio runs the TypeScript stages; "Built on Rust" is marked never and "with a Rust core for the image pipeline" is marked after 3.9. The copy and the plan agree.
3. 3.1 (the list reads manifests instead of mirrors; the shell streams before the list through `Await`) is the right shape and the gain estimate (4.7 s to under 0.4 s first byte) follows from the round trip arithmetic; the deck index follow up is correctly recorded as not this round.
4. 3.2 reverses SPEC 5.5 for the editor's filmstrip (clone first, capture never). The measured ground is sound and the deviation is recorded for me. Two risks the plan under weights: a clone at 200 px still decodes the slide's 1600 px twins (a 320 px twin variant is deferred), and the Blob thumbnail cache adds one `put` (an advanced operation) per rendered stamp. The CDN cached 302 depends on Vercel caching a redirect with `s-maxage`; the hosted smoke row the plan adds is the right proof, so I accept it as written.
5. 3.5's `ssr: 'data-only'` with a token drawn skeleton and `pendingMinMs: 0` is correct for TanStack Start; the dehydrated `EditorDeck` for `gt-brand` is about 500 KB before compression, the same bytes delivered earlier, acceptable. Trimming the version log out of the payload is overdue.
6. 3.8's server sent events replace 176 to 700 `_serverFn` calls per minute per tab with one stream. Two notes. The stream route inherits the heavy function rule (`maxDuration: 800`, `memory: 3009`); an idle stream does not need 3 GB, so the rule for `/api/events/**` should set the duration alone and leave memory at the default, or the plan should say why not. The Blob mailbox versus Redis is correctly left to me; the mailbox is the no dependency answer and 3.5 s command latency is acceptable for `deck_goto_slide`.
7. 3.9 commits the Linux addon and the wasm module as build outputs. One gap: the server bundle must treat `@turboslide/native-linux-x64-gnu` the way `SERVER_ONLY` treats `sharp` in `vite.deploy.config.ts` (external on the server, a stub on the client), or rolldown will try to bundle a `require` of a `.node` file; the plan names `traceDeps` and not the externalization. A committed binary in a public repository reviewed by SHA-256 against the check step's rebuild is acceptable to me.
8. Section 5's `defaultPreloadStaleTime` from 0 to 30 s changes behaviour: a hover then a click within 30 s opens the deck at the preloaded revision, and the watch channel adopts a newer one within about 3 s. Acceptable, but the plan should say so where it makes the change.
9. Section 6 prerenders `/home` at build and makes `/` a config level redirect with the `x-robots-tag` header; both are right, and the prerender is compatible with proposal 2's deferred live mount (client only after hydration).
10. Section 7 names the chunks by byte count and the owner per step, and the `check-client-bundle.mjs` budget is the regression gate. The 600 KB ceiling for `/home` and `/decks` depends on every step landing; the plan says so.
11. Section 8's budgets are ceilings from the measured fast mode and the arithmetic rather than targets a page advertises; the write check has not yet run (it creates a deck, so it was correctly kept off production), and 58 of 121 budgets met on production is an honest starting line.
12. The plan reads as plain technical English throughout and every estimate is labelled as one.

1. Proposal 2 is the identity, monochrome (the accent is out).
2. The `/home` hero is the still twin at first paint and the live `paper:liquid-metal` recipe in the `ink-paper` preset after the largest contentful paint, never under reduced motion, on a hidden document, without WebGL2 or under 900 px.
3. The title row's mark links to `/decks`; the words on `/decks` link to `/home`; `start_url` is `/home`.
4. The favicon is the opaque plate with the scheme block; the titanium base is out.
5. The `monochrome` manifest icon stays (cheap); the per deck social image waits for the next round.
6. No Turboslide wordmark on any sheet; the GT mark stays the deck's.
7. The performance plan proceeds in its ranked order with the three notes above (the events function rule's memory, the native package's externalization, the stale time note).
