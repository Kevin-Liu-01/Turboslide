## 3. The copy script, the fallback, and its verification

`scripts/blob-copy.mjs` is written only if the rehearsal of 2.2 refuses the transfer. Its shape:

- Two tokens from the environment, `SOURCE_BLOB_TOKEN` and `TARGET_BLOB_TOKEN`, never printed and never in a file; the script prints counts, bytes, elapsed time and the first twenty differing pathnames and nothing else.
- Lists the source with `list({ limit: 1000, cursor })`; for every object reads the bytes with `get(..., { useCache: false })` and one `head` for `contentType` and `cacheControl`, then `put`s under the same pathname with `addRandomSuffix: false`, `allowOverwrite: false`, the same content type and `cacheControlMaxAge`, and compares the returned etag with the source's. An object whose etags differ is fetched from the target and compared by the md5 of the body, since a multipart upload's etag is not the body's md5 (`packages/store/src/migrate.ts` 251 to 296 is the pattern).
- `--skip exports-older-than=1d,turboslide-sidecars` leaves out the 921 export files older than a day (302.4 MiB; no job record names them and the records are pruned at a day) and the 4,286 objects under `decks/<id>/.turboslide/` (29.9 MiB: the presence sidecars, `presence.json`, `pulse.json` and `copies/`, per instance state that regenerates). That leaves about 12,000 objects and 1.0 GiB; at eight in flight and about 0.3 s per object the pass runs about eight minutes, the whole store about eleven.
- `--verify` re-lists both stores and compares counts and etags per prefix over the copied set alone, naming the skipped set it excludes (`decks/*/.turboslide/**` and `exports/` older than a day) and the count it expects per prefix from the source listing taken in the same run: at the 2026-09-20T19:07Z baseline that would read `decks/` 9,331 (13,617 minus 4,286), `users/` 943, `links/` 477, `bundles/` 4, `builds/` 10, `vitals/` 35, `index/` 1 and `exports/` 1,193 (the objects under a day old), and the expected counts are recomputed from the source at run time because the store moves. `--delta` copies the objects whose source etag changed since the first pass and never over an object the target has already rewritten (a target etag that differs from both the first pass's and the source's is left alone and listed).
- Cost of the copy at 2026-09-20 prices: about $0.07 of Blob data transfer, $0.09 of advanced operations (about 13,500 puts), a cent of simple operations, and Fast Origin Transfer on the reads that miss the CDN (about 1.27 GB at $0.06, about $0.08): about $0.25 in all.
- With the copy, `TURBOSLIDE_PUBLIC_STORE_HOST` becomes `<new store id lowercased>.public.blob.vercel-storage.com` on the team project, and the freeze for the delta pass is the WAF redirect of section 5 step 7, because the product's own freeze does not hold across instances (section 8). A WAF deny is the wrong freeze: a 403 makes the room client return the author's edits as refused (`room-client.ts` 1259 onward, the 403 path), while a 429 or a 5xx keeps them pending (1241 to 1257).

Accepted when `--verify` reads the expected counts and equal etags for the copied set of `decks/`, `users/`, `links/`, `bundles/`, `builds/`, `vitals/`, `index/` and the day old `exports/`, with the skipped set named in its output.

## 8. The defects the move exposes, and their order

- Enforce on the first day locks sellers out of their decks on the new host (audit-hosting item 3). A new deck's record is `owner: <creator principal>, generalAccess: { mode: 'restricted', role: 'viewer' }` (`packages/schema/src/access.ts` 317 to 339); 57 of 72 decks carry an `access.json`; the principal is the sealed `__Host-ts_id` cookie (`apps/studio/src/server/auth/session.ts` 2 to 21), which a browser never sends to another host; 943 principal indexes sit under `users/`. Under `enforce` on the team host no browser presents a principal any record names, so only the admin bearer could edit. The plan: ship the team deployment under `shadow`; after step 9, one pass over the 57 records sets general access to Anyone with the link, Editor (`share.setGeneralAccess` through the bearer; `admin.assignOwner` once sign in exists); then `enforce`. Accepted when a seller who made a deck on the old host opens it on the new host under `enforce` and types. The product round's default for new decks is already Anyone with the link, Editor (`docs/PRODUCT.md` section 1), so decks made after the ship need no pass.
- The kill switches hold on one instance on the blob tier (audit-hosting item 8). `docs/security.md` section 5 says every instance reads a flipped switch within 5 s; hosted, nothing binds a flag reader (`bindFlags` has no caller in `apps/studio/src`), `reader()` falls to `fileFlagStore()` on the instance's own `/tmp` (`apps/studio/src/server/flags.ts` 139, 167 to 170), and the blob realtime channel hands the memory channel's `flag` (`packages/realtime/src/blob.ts`). So the product has no cross instance freeze and `readOnly` is not a tool for this cutover; the freeze is the drain and the WAF redirect of section 5. The fix is `docs/SYNC.md` 5.2 (`flags.json` on the store read on the budget's instance wide line, the round after) with the row `sync.flags.read-only-on-every-instance`.
- Open tabs and the flip (judge-operations rejection 1). The redirect is cross origin and nothing in the app allows it (1.7), so a tab that is writing when the flip lands loses what it typed after the flip at its next reload. The drain of section 5 step 5 is the answer, the grace hour WAF rule the alternative when the token survives, and the Playwright rehearsal records what an ignored window costs.
- The mixed commit window (2.6): the move runs before the sync round ships, or both deployments pin to one commit.
- Delete forever leaves a deck's exports behind (audit-hosting item 12): `exports/` holds 649.5 MiB for 219 deck ids while 72 decks exist, and no code path names `exports/<deckId>/` on removal (`blob-store.ts` `remove` 2391 onward; `templates.ts`, `export-jobs.ts`, `download.ts`). With the transfer nothing is copied, so the cleanup waits for the move and never runs in its window.
- The presence sidecars accumulate (audit-hosting item 16, 4,149 objects, 29.8 MiB): `docs/SYNC.md` 5.2, the round after; never migrated by the copy fallback.
