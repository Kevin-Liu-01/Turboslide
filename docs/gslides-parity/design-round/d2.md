## DR-D2#1: layering

### Items

- One doorway for an anchored plate, `packages/chrome/src/usePlate.ts` (new): `usePlate(ref,
  { layer, anchor, side, align, gap, fit, follow })` puts the plate in its layer through D1's
  `useLayer` and on its anchor through D1's `place()` (which follows the anchor while the plate is
  open), and `useStart(anchor, side)` gives the place a plate stands at for the microtasks before
  its first placement, so a plate whose row or field takes the focus as it mounts is never hidden
  (a hidden element refuses the focus, VERIFICATION-3 finding 13). `usePointOrElement` turns a
  pointer into one stable anchor.
