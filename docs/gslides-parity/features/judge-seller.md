# Judge seller: the features round

The seller's and the marketer's judgment of `docs/FEATURES.md` (the features round, written 2026-09-20), read against the four production audits under `docs/gslides-parity/features/` (`audit-objects.md`, `audit-fonts.md`, `audit-logos.md`, `audit-shaders.md`) and their evidence folders, `docs/PRODUCT.md` sections 4 and 7, `docs/gslides-parity/focus/AMENDMENTS.md` A1 and `docs/gslides-parity/research/07-sales-users.md`. Written 2026-09-20. The lens is the seller and the marketer at General Translation and at any brand that replaces the kit: whether a table, a chart or a diagram is as easy as Google Slides on first use, whether the logo picker is what a marketer wants, whether the shader library is a brand asset, whether the fonts answer Kevin without bloat, and whether the words are plain.

What this judge did: read the specification and the four audits in full; opened the evidence the rejections cite (`audit-objects/t03-table-one-click.png`, `c02-chart-edit-data-panel.png`, `run-tables-rows.json` rows 2, 5, 7, 13 and 21; `audit-shaders/s06-format-options-material-1440.png`; `audit-logos/12-thesvg-default-marks-on-paper-and-ink.png`, `thesvg-facts.json`); fetched thesvg.org's `registry.json` and the rsms/inter releases with curl on 2026-09-20 and saved the results under `judge-seller/` (`thesvg-seller-coverage.py`, `thesvg-seller-coverage.txt`, `inter-release-check.txt`, `features-copy-check.txt`). No source file was touched, no git write command ran, no dev server ran and no Playwright walk ran; the production measurements below are the audits'.

## Verdict

Revise. The document reads the audits faithfully, keeps their four words, ranks by value then effort, and its copy passes the rules (`features-copy-check.txt`: no em dashes, no heading ends in a period). The tables lane is the right list in the right order, the Inter verdict is correct and confirmed today, the eight families are a sound answer to "a few more fonts", and the logo route and the shader frame pipeline are engineered, not sketched. Eight things stop it from binding as written: the table's first gesture still costs one click more than Google and the typing target after a click is the wrong cell; the chart's double click is named as the meaning of intuitive and then ranked P1; Use on every slide from the logo picker replaces the seller's own company mark and the snackbar says the opposite; the logo source's coverage of the brands a General Translation seller names is never stated and my read finds a quarter of them missing, every localisation competitor among them; the licence row shows an identifier where the document's own rule forbids ids; the one gesture research 07 calls the most common visual edit in a sales deck sits behind the day two condition; and the shader's brand asset form, the kit's background slot, is unranked P1 behind the largest P0 lane.

## Notes

- What holds. The tables lane's P0 list (2.2 ranks 1 to 14) is the seller's daily work in the right order: the chart grid's keys first at effort 1, the caret and the range next, the centred shape label, the arrows, the diagram label's double click, Bold on a range without the refusal card, the row seams, the "+" at the edges, the diagram step as one object. Each is measured by a row and each is one commit. The floating table bar waiting for its gestures (2.3, audit-objects 3) is the right call.
- The Inter verdict is confirmed today. `api.github.com` lists v4.1 (2024-11-16) as rsms/inter's latest release and v4.0 before it; the v4.1 tag and licence URLs answer 200 and the v4.001 forms the code names answer 404 (`judge-seller/inter-release-check.txt`). The upright is the right file; the italic fix, the two links, the fallback face in the stacks, the display features gated on Inter and tabular figures on table cells are the right five changes, all P0 and all bytes or CSS with a test each.
- The fonts answer Kevin without bloat. Eight families add 1.76 MB to a 14.4 MB catalog, nothing loads before a pick after the specimen rows (3.5), and Geist is the face the seller audit asked for. Merriweather's 4.97 MB staying until measured (3.3) is reasonable. Question 7 lets Kevin trim the six; the judge would keep Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Sans, Manrope and Fraunces and let Bricolage Grotesque, Schibsted Grotesk and Newsreader wait for a brand that asks, since a seller's deck reaches for one sans and one serif and Newsreader and Fraunces together add 885 KB.
- The logo picker is otherwise what a marketer wants: Insert > Logo in the default view, "Company name" as the search, Symbol and Wordmark, Color and Mono, the mark drawn on paper and on ink so the white Vercel and OpenAI defaults never vanish on a light slide (`12-thesvg-default-marks-on-paper-and-ink.png` shows the empty paper tiles), a logo size on insert instead of the slot's height, the kit's Find a logo, Tailor's Find the customer's logo, a same origin stored asset with a source record, a daily cache that answers offline, and the two actions on every transport. The registry key mismatch (audit-logos 13) is handled by reading `icons.json`.
- The shader library is a brand asset in its colours and its stills and a toy in its placement this round: the kit's six roles feed the palette (5.7), the frame is automatic and reaches the show, the viewer, the PDF and both PowerPoints (5.5), reduced motion and hidden tabs pause it (5.6), and the words are the seller's (5.1, "Shader" everywhere). Rejection 8 names what is missing for the marketer.
- The P0 effort of 111 by the audits' scale is above the product round's two day cut, which the document says in section 1 and handles by rank order per lane. The seller's risk is uneven: the objects lane (27) lands whole, the logos lane (32) and the shaders lane (39) each carry the chance of a P0 item moving to the round after. The document's rule that an item moves whole with its rows recorded not driven is the right one; the ship note should list the moved items first.
- Copy. The document passes the round's rules: plain English, sentence case, no em dashes, no heading ends in a period, and the "after this round" sentences read as specification. The words the document puts in front of a seller are plain with the two exceptions in rejection 6 and addition 10 and the sentences addition 9 asks for.
- Evidence. Every file the four audits cite in the rows this judgment read is present under its folder (`audit-objects/`, `audit-fonts/`, `audit-logos/`, `audit-shaders/`), the run tables carry clicks, keys and seconds per row, and the five objects decks, the two fonts decks, the two logos decks and the two shaders decks are recorded as trashed and answering 404.
- Method limits. This judge ran no Playwright walk; the production numbers are the audits' from 2026-09-20 at `9293df4`. The coverage read of thesvg.org is one registry fetch on 2026-09-20 over a list of 146 names the judge chose; three of the misses ("remote", "gtm", "shopify plus") are generic words or sub brands and may match under another title, and the number is given to show the gap, not as the final list, which addition 5 asks the deployment's marketer to keep.
