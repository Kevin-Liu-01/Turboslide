# Lane D5 of the design round: the other pages

Lane D5's record of the design round (`docs/DESIGN.md` 9, 10.5, 11), in the worktree
`/Users/kevinliu/repos/Turboslide-design` on `design/round`, the dev server on port 4665 (started
with `-c vite.no-watch.config.ts`, the round's environment, secrets generated per start and never
printed), the node-server build under `.turboslide/build.lock`. Every load is the one minute load
average read beside the reading. Bytes, counts, colours and boxes do not move with load; a timing
read at a load over 24 is recorded and is not a verdict.

The rows' driver is one module, `apps/studio/e2e/core/design-pages.ts`, called once from
`core/present.spec.ts` (`designPresent()`) and once from `core/decks.spec.ts` (`designDecks()`),
the shape of D1's `chrome-foundation.ts`. Its scrollbar readings need the bars drawn, and
Playwright's Chromium runs with `--hide-scrollbars`, so the module launches one Chromium of its own
without that switch (research-scroll 2) and reads a bar as `offsetWidth - clientWidth` less the
borders: the shared bar of `tokens.css` is 8 px, the platform's classic bar 15 px, a hidden one 0.
A context's appearance is the stored key the theme button writes, set by an init script from a
cookie the row switches, so a later page of the same context boots in the other appearance.

### Deviations

- The show's two surfaces enter the top layer only once `Slideshow.tsx` hands in `useLayer`
  (request 1): the viewer cannot import the chrome. Until then their sheets draw the scale's
  z-index, which already orders them over the show.
- `BookView.css` (its alternates and four literal `tabular-nums`) is on the seam list of
  DESIGN.md 10.0 and waits for the merge of origin/main; it lands as DR-D5#3b. `deck.$deckId.tsx`
  needs no change.
- The presenter view's slide list stays in its DOM place under its button: the console is its own
  window and the list's place needs `place()`, which the viewer cannot import.
- The shortcuts card's "PRESENTING" heading keeps its capitals: its `css/no-eyebrow` finding is an
  accepted one, and changing the heading alone would leave a stale acceptance (request 4).
- The print page's DialogCheck is the chrome's (D2's); this push removes only the dead rules.
- Nothing calls `standaloneRuntimeSource()` in the product today (the CLI's `build` writes the
  render runtime's placeholder); the pictures swap it in by hand to show the chrome this file draws.
