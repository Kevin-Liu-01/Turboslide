# Text: the production audit

Audit of the "Text" area of https://www.turboslide.com (production, commit 877eb690), driven 2026-09-28 with headless Chromium at 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800, deviceScaleFactor 2, through playwright-core from the worktree, at human speed (the mouse in steps, 40 to 90 ms per key, real double clicks and drags). Four scratch decks from `/new`, each trashed through File > Move to trash, deleted forever on `/decks/trash` and confirmed 404 on `/edit/<id>` and `/deck/<id>`; the one deck whose Delete forever did not complete (`untitled-20260928-txww`, `/edit` still answered 200) was removed through the actions API with the bearer and re-confirmed 404. The scripts and their JSON tables live under `polish/text/` and this folder's `audit-text/run-1..4/`; every screenshot named below is under `audit-text/run-2/` or `audit-text/run-4/`.

The measure here is a seller driving the feature and looking at the screen beside Google Slides, not the gate. Every failing interaction was driven at least twice (run 2 at 1440 and run 4 at 1440, with a 1280 pass inside each run). The bold, align, spacing and indent findings reproduced on both runs and are read straight from the code path, not from a flaky step.

Features driven: 48 (the title, subtitle and body placeholders; the text box by tool click, by drag and by the toolbar button; select by double click; bold, italic, underline, strikethrough by the toolbar and by the keyboard; size by the field and by the steppers; text colour; highlight; align; line spacing; indent; bulleted and numbered lists; links by Cmd+K, the toolbar and auto-detect; the link chip; clear formatting; the Font dropdown with 34 families and More fonts; autofit and overflow; the Format options panel; find and replace; the object and text right-click menus; the ring and the eight squares on a title, a subtitle and a text box in the light and dark appearances at 1440 and 1280; Cmd+Z after each; the show; the PDF; reload persistence).

## What a seller feels today

The text session itself is good. One click selects a placeholder or a box with a clean ring, eight squares, a rotation handle and a chip; a double click opens the session with the caret where you clicked; a printable key over a selection replaces the text; a pointer down inside the box drags it; the caret keys, Home, End, Shift selection, Option+Backspace, Enter and Escape all behave; Cmd+Z steps back after every action. Italic, underline, strikethrough and text colour mark the selected word inline and undo cleanly. The Font dropdown is the strongest surface: 34 families in their own faces under Brand, sans, serif, display and mono, live search, and More fonts. Find and replace counts as you type and replaces across the deck. The dark appearance draws the ring correctly, the PDF is two clean pages, and a reload keeps everything.

What a seller runs into is the toolbar. The Bold button does nothing you can see, because it sets the whole box to a medium weight instead of bolding the word. Setting a size on one word resizes the whole box and rounds your typed number to a ladder. Align, line spacing and indent from the toolbar look dead until you click away. Building a bulleted list and pressing Enter for the next item throws an error over the stage. Picking Blank leaves two "Click to add" prompts stacked over the whole slide. These are the first things a seller tries after typing, and most of them read as broken.

## The ten to fix first

1. Toolbar and menu Bold do not bold the selected text (they set the whole box to weight 500, Inter Medium)
2. Layout > Blank leaves the title and body placeholders stacked over the whole slide
3. Toolbar Align, Line spacing and Indent show no change until you click away
4. Editing a bulleted or numbered list throws "text.splice: /text is not a string"
5. A font size set on a selected word resizes the whole box and snaps a typed value to the ladder
6. A link shows no chip on a single click, only after you double click into the text
7. A long title does not shrink to fit; it stays 88 px and grows down the slide
8. The highlight amber swatch paints a grey band, not amber
9. The placeholder prompt reads "Click to add title" but a single click only selects it
10. The editor page logs a 404 and a 502 on every load

## Notes

- The ring and the eight squares are correct on a title, a subtitle and a text box, in the light and the dark appearance, at 1440 and at 1280 (run-4/151, 152, 153 at 1280; run-2/130, 132 in dark). The text ring stands off the text by its 10 px outset and does not fuse with the caret.
- Italic, underline, strikethrough and text colour all mark the selected run inline and undo cleanly (run-2/46 to 66, 73). Bold is the one mark that does not, which is finding 1.
- Find and replace counts while typing ("2 matches on 1 slide"), replaces all, reports the count and undoes (run-2/121, 122, 124).
- The PDF is two pages at 960 by 540 pt and matches the stage (run-2/83-pdf-page-1.png, 83-pdf-page-2.png). The show renders both slides and the bullets survive it.
- Bullets and numbers do render and persist (the thumbnail and the show show the dots); the defect is editing the list after it is made (finding 4).
- Clear formatting removes bold and italic and keeps a link, which matches Google; it is not a defect.
