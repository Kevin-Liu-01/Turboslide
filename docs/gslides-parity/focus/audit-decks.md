# Focus audit: deck lifecycle and the home surfaces

Auditor's note for the focus round, 2026-09-15, against https://turboslide.vercel.app (main at `ec61c6b`, the blob tier, anonymous principals). Every interaction below was driven through the product's own user interface with headless Chromium (`playwright-core` 1.62.1 from the repo root, Node 24.13.0, viewport 1440 by 900) at human speed: the mouse moved in steps, keys landed 40 to 90 ms apart, double clicks were the browser's own, menus were opened by clicking the menubar and the card buttons. A failed interaction was repeated up to three times before it was called broken or flaky. Every observed value in the evidence column was read from the DOM, from `window.turboslide.studio.describe().state`, from the network, or from a screenshot. Nothing was fixed and no source file was touched.

The scripts live under the session scratchpad (`focus/decks/audit-decks.mjs`, `audit-decks-followup.mjs`, `audit-decks-trash.mjs`, `audit-decks-cancel.mjs`, `thumbs-probe.mjs`, `access-probe.mjs`, `access-probe-2.mjs`, `cleanup-copy.mjs`). Their raw tables and the screenshots are in `docs/gslides-parity/focus/audit-decks/` (`audit-decks.json` with 57 rows, `audit-decks-followup.json`, `audit-decks-trash.json`, `audit-decks-cancel.json`, `thumbs-probe.json`, `access-probe.json`, `access-probe-2.json`, and the `cleanup-*.json` logs).

Need, as the brief defines it: core is a seller's weekly task per `research/07-sales-users.md` sections 2, 3 and 8 (start a deck, open the last deck, rename it after the customer, clone it, present it, trash and restore, never lose work); useful helps but a seller finishes without it; park is rarely needed by a seller or is a designer's tool. Severity: 3 the seller cannot complete a top task, 2 a wrong or lost result, 1 cosmetic. A step that could not be driven is "not driven" with the reason, never "passed".

## Summary

What works. The whole path from the product page to a saved deck works: `/home` and its calls to action, `/` answering 307 to `/new`, the draft with "Not saved yet", the first write that creates the deck and moves the address to `/edit/<id>` while the save words go "Saving…" then "All changes saved", every rename path in the title row (Enter, Escape, blur, empty name, File > Rename), the Last edit clock and Version history, the reload of `/edit/<id>` keeping the slide through the `#s/<slide>` hash, the Turboslide mark back to `/decks`, the list with its strip, Recent row, search, sort, list view, thumbnails and titles that open the deck, the card menu with Google's seven rows, Present and Open in new tab from the card, Download, Move to trash with Undo, the trash page with Restore and Delete forever, the GT brand deck card (an 85 slide copy in 7 s), Back and Forward across `/home`, `/decks` and `/edit/<id>` including the `/new` address move, the You need access page for an unknown id on `/edit` and `/deck`, the Not found page, and the keyboard focus order of both surfaces.

What is broken, with the mechanism seen. One mechanism accounts for most of it: the `/decks` and `/decks/trash` listings read every deck's manifest through the Blob store's public body URL, and the store's own comment (`packages/store/src/blob-store.ts`, "Three facts about Vercel Blob") records that `list()` lags `head()` by up to a minute and that the CDN keeps an overwritten blob's previous body for a while. The list therefore shows a title and a revision that are behind the store for up to a minute or more after any write.

1. Rename from the card and from the row (severity 2, core). Card menu > Rename sends the card's revision as `baseRevision`. When the list is behind, the store refuses with the snackbar "Rename: baseRevision 1 is stale; the document is at revision 2" and the card keeps the old name; nothing else changes and the rename is lost. When the card happens to be fresh, the rename lands in the store but the card still shows the old name for more than 30 s because the refetch after the rename reads the lagging manifest; a reload within that window shows the old name too. Six attempts in the grid and three in the list view produced no visible rename within 30 s; two attempts in run 2 were refused with the stale sentence.
2. Make a copy from the card (severity 2, core). The same stale revision refuses the copy: in run 1 three attempts opened a blank tab that closed, the dialog stayed open with "Make a copy" and no copy appeared; in run 2, on a fresh card, the copy took 3.5 s and opened in a new tab. Move to trash from the card carries the same `baseRevision` and would refuse in the same window; it was driven only on fresh cards, where it worked.
3. The listing after Move to trash and after Restore (severity 2, core). After File > Move to trash from the editor, `/decks/trash` did not list the deck within 60 s in run 1's cleanup, and did list it a few minutes later. After Restore in run 3, a reload of `/decks` did not list the deck within 60 s and neither did `/decks/trash`; three minutes later `/decks/trash` listed it as trashed although it had been restored, and Delete forever from that card worked. A seller who restores a deck and reloads sees it in neither list for a while.
4. Card thumbnails (severity 1, useful). 33 of 38 cards on `/decks` show the dark title plate instead of a thumbnail, and every one of the 5 thumbnails requested answered 200 (19 ms to 4.4 s). `cardOf` reads `firstSlide` and the appearance through `deckCardFacts`, which reads `decks/<id>/deck.json` from the instance's disk mirror; the Blob listing "writes no mirror" since round four, so on an instance that has not opened the deck the manifest is not on disk, `firstSlide` is null and the card draws the plate (and defaults to the dark appearance). Which cards get a thumbnail depends on which instance answers.
5. The browser tab title after a rename on a deck created from `/new` (severity 1, useful). The tab keeps "Untitled presentation, Turboslide" after the title row rename; a reload shows the new title. The `/new` route's `head` is static and the router stays on `/new` after the address move.
6. The You need access page paint (severity 2, core, seen once). In run 1's browser, which had used the editor and chosen the light appearance, the page for `/edit/<unknown id>` drew the light hero figure over the form and the sign in line (shot 31); the h1, the sentence, the form controls and the link were all in the DOM and the link worked. Two fresh browsers, a browser that had visited `/new`, and a reload of each rendered the page clean (`access-cold.png`, `access-warm.png`, `access-probe-1.png`). Recorded as flaky.
7. The card menu did not open once right after a failed Make a copy dialog (Open in new tab, attempt 1 of 2; Make a copy, attempt 2 of 3). Recorded as flaky, severity 1.
8. Enter in the Delete forever dialog (severity 1, useful). The dialog gives Cancel the initial focus and its key handler leaves Enter to the focused button, so Enter cancels; the Delete forever button's tooltip names Enter as its key. The button click deletes as expected.

What a seller needs here. The list is the seller's second home after the editor, and its three writes (Rename, Make a copy, Move to trash) must work on the deck they just edited, which is exactly when the list is behind the store. Either the card actions should not send a revision they read from a lagging list (let the store take the current revision, as `renameDeckFn` already does when `baseRevision` is absent), or the list should read the manifest heads through `head()` and the version records rather than the CDN body, and the refetch after a write should carry the write's answer into the card at once. The thumbnails need the listing to sync the manifest the card facts read, or the card facts to come from the head the listing already parsed. The tab title should follow the rename on `/new`. Everything else in this area works and works at a pace a seller accepts: the deck list answers in about 1 s, the GT copy in 7 s, Delete forever in about 5 s.

Observations outside this area, for the other auditors. Right after the first write on `/new`, `describe().state.sync` read `{connected: false, transport: "poll", tier: "memory"}` on production (row 12); the room attached afterwards. During run 1's cleanup the console logged "Failed to load resource: 502", "500" and "Error: Vercel Blob: Failed to fetch blob: 500 Internal Server Error" on `/edit/untitled-20260916-jek6`, a deck that had just been moved to the trash. Every page load logged the report-only CSP notice "The Content Security Policy directive 'upgrade-insecure-requests' is ignored when delivered in a report-only policy".
