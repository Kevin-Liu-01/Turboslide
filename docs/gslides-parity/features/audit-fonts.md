# Audit: the right rsms Inter and a few more fonts

Auditor's notes for the feature round of 2026-09-20 (Kevin: "make sure were using the riht rsms inter and add a few more fonts"). Measured on production https://turboslide.vercel.app (the return round plus the caret hotfix) with two Playwright walks at 1440 by 900 and 1280 by 800 on scratch decks created from /new and removed through File > Move to trash and Delete forever (both answer 404 afterwards: `walk.json` steps 14 and 15, `walk-2.json` steps 5 and 6), and read from the product round's tree at `/Users/kevinliu/repos/Turboslide` (uncommitted; the fonts package, the picker and the fonts route are new files there). The scripts are copied beside the evidence: `audit-fonts/fonts-walk.mjs`, `fonts-walk-2.mjs`, `woff2-names.mjs` (a Node reader of a woff2's name, head, OS/2, fvar and GSUB tables; no dependency). Nothing in the source tree was changed.

## 1. What is verified

### 1.1 The bundled Inter

`packages/fonts/assets/InterVariable.woff2` (352,240 bytes, sha256 `693b77d4…a8e3`) is byte for byte the `web/InterVariable.woff2` of Rasmus Andersson's Inter 4.1 release (`Inter-4.1.zip`, published 2024-11-16; `inter-4.1-compare.txt`). Its name table reads "Inter Variable", "Version 4.001;git-9221beed3", designer Rasmus Andersson, licence OFL 1.1, fsType 0; the head table's date is 2024-11-16; fvar carries opsz 14 to 32 and wght 100 to 900; GSUB carries cv01 to cv14, ss01 to ss08, tnum, pnum, calt, case, dlig, frac, zero, subs, sups and the rest (`inter-tables.json`). Production serves the same bytes at `/assets/InterVariable-DiVDrmQJ.woff2` with `cache-control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` (`production-and-urls.txt`).

`packages/fonts/assets/InterVariable-Italic.woff2` (380,904 bytes, sha256 `0470791f…cc05`) is not from the same release. Its name table reads "Version 4.000;git-a52131595" and its head date is 2023-11-20, the v4.0 release. Inter 4.1's `web/InterVariable-Italic.woff2` is 387,976 bytes, sha256 `e564f652…262a`, "Version 4.001;git-9221beed3". The 4.1 changelog names the italic fixes the bundled file lacks: "Fixes ss03 in italic" (#711), "Fixes broken glyphs Cyrillic /uni04F6 /Ghestrokecyrillic and /uni04FA in Italic" (#636), "Remove incomplete OpenType features c2sc and smcp" (#630; the bundled italic still carries smcp and c2sc, the upright does not). `inter.ts` 24 to 37, `inter.test.ts` 41 ("is the italic file of the same release, byte for byte"), `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` 12 to 14 and `export/fonts.json` `source.italic` all say the italic comes from the release `v4.001`; rsms/inter has no such tag (its releases are v4.0 and v4.1), and the two URLs the code names answer 404. The 17 italic export instances under `packages/fonts/export/*-Italic.ttf` are cut from the 4.0 italic.

### 1.2 The OpenType features the sheet enables

- Display text (`.ts-sheet h1, h2, .big`, `packages/theme/src/gt-ink-paper/sheet.css` 251 to 260): weight 500, `letter-spacing: -0.025em`, `text-wrap: balance`, `font-feature-settings: 'cv11', 'ss01'`. This is the GT deck's rule (`Prototemplate/deck/parts/head.html` 58) and `docs/brand.md` 102 to 103's wordmark rule; `packages/theme/src/tokens.ts` 236 holds it as `DISPLAY.features`. Measured on production: the heading run computes to `"cv11", "ss01"`, weight 500, 88 px, letter spacing -2.2 px (`walk.json` step 5); the zoom shows the single storey a and the open 6 and 9 (`title-cv11-ss01-zoom.png`).
- Body text: no features (`font-feature-settings: normal`, `font-variant-numeric: normal`; step 7 and `walk-2.json` step 2). The sheet root sets `font-optical-sizing: auto` and antialiasing (`packages/render/src/block-css.ts` 9; step 3).
- `tnum` is used by the chrome alone (TitleRow.css 363, Filmstrip.css 245 and 268, Sidebar.css 715, comments.css 425, presence.css 176, VersionsPanel.css 315, PresenterConsole.css 45, home.css 322 and 693). Nothing on the sheet uses it: at 500 22px Inter, "1111" measures 34.81 px and "0000" 56.01 px; with `tnum` both measure 56.89 px (step 7; `body-digits-proportional-2x.png`).
- The chrome applies `'cv11', 'ss01'` in about eighty rules across `packages/chrome/src/*.css`, `packages/viewer`, `apps/studio/src/routes/*.css`; the sheet applies them only to display text.
- The true italic works: Cmd+I on the heading renders `font-style: italic` in the Inter italic face (step 6; `title-italic-zoom.png`). The rule keeps `cv11` on the italic run, which has no cv11 (a no-op).
- ss02 to ss08, case, dlig, calt are not set anywhere; calt and the default features are on by the font itself.

### 1.3 font-display, the preload and the fallback

`packages/fonts/src/inter.css` declares both faces with `font-weight: 100 900` and `font-display: swap`, and a third face `'Inter Fallback'` (`src: local('Arial')`, `size-adjust: 107.4724%`, `ascent-override: 90.1394%`, `descent-override: 22.444%`, `line-gap-override: 0%`), computed by `scripts/build-fonts.py` from Inter's and Arial's tables and pinned by `inter.test.ts` and `fonts.json`. `apps/studio/src/routes/__root.tsx` 124 to 137 preloads both woff2 files as `as="font" type="font/woff2" crossorigin="anonymous"` on every route. Measured on production: both files arrive by the preload link (initiator `link`, 323 ms start, 407 and 439 ms end, 352,240 and 380,904 bytes) before the editor's ready mark at 606 ms; `document.fonts` at ready lists "Inter normal 100 900 loaded", "Inter italic 100 900 unloaded" (downloaded, not yet used by any text) and "Inter Fallback normal normal unloaded" (`walk.json` step 2).

The fallback face is never in a stack. The sheet root's computed family is `Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` (step 3); `sheet.css` `--display` and `--text`, `packages/chrome/src/tokens.css` 109 to 110 (`--pt-display`, `--pt-text`) and the identity's `marks-render.ts` 344 name Inter, Helvetica Neue and Arial; a grep of every css, ts and tsx outside `packages/fonts/src` finds no `'Inter Fallback'` and no `FONT_FAMILY_WITH_FALLBACK` (`inter.ts` 74 to 75 exports it). So a first visit paints in Helvetica Neue or Arial at their own metrics and reflows when Inter lands; the preload narrows the window, the face does nothing.

### 1.4 The catalog on the tree (docs/PRODUCT.md 4.2)

26 families (`packages/schema/src/fonts.ts` `FONT_IDS`), 99 files, 14.4 MB under `packages/fonts/assets/<id>/` (measured with `du`: 14,992 KB), each a fontTools format conversion of the TrueType files at google/fonts commit `1ac2012c…`, licences committed beside the files, notices at `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` 599 to 616. Sizes per folder: Merriweather 4,970,312 bytes (two variable files with opsz, wdth and wght, 2.48 MB each), Lato 2,187,552 (ten statics), Source Serif 4 768 KB, Open Sans 592 KB, EB Garamond 580 KB, Poppins 568 KB, the rest under 500 KB each. Features per family are in `catalog-features.txt`: 17 of the 25 carry an ss01 of their own (DM Sans, EB Garamond, Fira Code, IBM Plex Mono, IBM Plex Sans, Lato, Merriweather, Montserrat, Nunito, Open Sans, Poppins, Raleway, Roboto, Source Sans 3, Source Serif 4, Space Grotesk, Work Sans), Source Sans 3 carries a cv11 of its own, and 12 carry tnum (Bebas Neue, EB Garamond, Fira Code, Lato, Lora, Merriweather, Montserrat, Open Sans, Roboto, Source Serif 4, Space Grotesk, Work Sans).

The dropdown (`packages/chrome/src/FontPicker.tsx` 568 lines, `dialogs/MoreFonts.tsx` 162, `font-picker-model.ts` 263, `FontPicker.css` 221), the route (`apps/studio/src/routes/fonts.$.ts`, `server/fonts.ts`: `/fonts/faces/<version>/<ids>.css` and `/fonts/<version>/<id>/<file>`, immutable for the versioned form, five minutes for `current`), the emission (`packages/render/src/fonts.ts`: one @font-face per file with `font-display: swap`, `--ts-font-<id>` on the sheet root), the schema (`typography.ts` 145 and 200: `font-family: var(--ts-font-<id>, inherit)`), the Format > Text row (`menus/model.ts` 1595, `format.text.font`), the switch (`toolbar-tails.ts` 78 `FONTS_PARKED = false`). None of this is on production: `/fonts/faces/current/roboto.css` answers 404 there, the Font control is a disabled label and Format > Text has no Font row (section 2, item 1).

### 1.5 The export path

The PDF is Chromium's `page.pdf` over the print document (`packages/export/src/pdf/build.ts` 1 to 22; `packages/headless/src/pdf.ts` 55), so the browser embeds a subset of every face it drew; the export capture inlines a used catalog face as a data URI (`packages/export/src/scene/extract.ts` 244 to 248). The Editable text PowerPoint names a catalog face by its family name in `a:latin typeface` (`pptx/text.ts` 93 `familyFor`, `pptx/fonts-map.ts` 208 to 235 `catalogFamilyName`), adds the residual "travels by name; PowerPoint substitutes it on a machine without the face (the catalog's licence allows embedding, which the export does not do)" (`text.ts` 88 to 91), and embeds only the Inter export set as `.fntdata` when Embed fonts is on (`dialogs/Download.tsx` 230, default off; `ooxml/fonts.ts` 236). Inter runs travel as `GT Inter Display`, `GT Inter Text <size>` (exact set) or `Inter`, `Inter Medium` (standard set) from `export/fonts.json` (version `4.001+gt.2`). PRODUCT.md 4.5 puts Embed fonts on by default and the `a:fontScheme` from the kit in the round after.

## 3. Summary

What a seller feels today on production: the deck is Inter and nothing else. The Font box reads "Font", is grey, and its tooltip says the theme sets Inter; Format > Text has no Font row; a pasted pipeline table shows amounts that do not line up by column because the sheet's digits are proportional. The Inter that renders is the right one for the upright (Rasmus Andersson's 4.1 release, byte for byte, with the brand's single storey a and open digits on headings, true italics, optical sizing on), preloaded and cached for a year. Under the surface the italic is the 4.0 file from a year earlier, the code names a release tag that does not exist, the licence link a marketer would click answers 404, and the metric matched fallback face the round built is declared but never placed in a stack.

The ten items this round should do first: 1 (ship the product round's dropdown; it is the whole answer to "add more font options"), 2 (tabular figures on tables and numeric columns; one CSS rule for the seller's most common defect, then the toggle), 3 (Geist and Geist Mono; one fetch), 4 (the 4.1 italic and a rebuild of the italic export instances), 5 (the two dead links), 6 (the fallback face in the stacks or deleted), 7 (the display features gated on Inter before any catalog face reaches a heading), 8 (the six families; about 1.5 MB), 9 (SVG specimens so the picker weighs nothing until a pick), 10 (Recent).

What to leave: 11 to 17 as polish for the round after; uploaded brand fonts, Embed fonts on by default, the PowerPoint `a:fontScheme` from the kit and the theme mode stay where PRODUCT.md 4.5 and 4.6 put them; Commit Mono unless Kevin names it (its name table and its source outside google/fonts cost more than Geist Mono for the same slot); Merriweather's weight until a deck in Merriweather is measured on a slow connection.
